## Overview
The planetary guild of alchemists, herbalists, and poison-brewers, headquartered within the subterranean glass cloisters of the Middle Ring of **[[the-ring-city]]**. Led by the reclusive and brilliant Grand Magistra **[[veridiana-hex]]**, the Conclave regulates the synthesis and trade of all licensed healing potions, combat stimulants, and alchemical reagents across [[rubicon]].

The Conclave is locked in a fierce industrial rivalry with Piltover's **Bio-Synthetica**, actively undermining synthetic magitech pharmaceuticals in favor of traditional, high-grade magical alchemy.

> [!read-aloud]
> Endless tiers of bubbling alembics, condenser coils, and spiral glass retorts hiss with luminescent amber, viridian, and amethyst liquids. Masked apothecary apprentices in leather gauntlets carefully measure dragon-blood crystals and crushed banshee moss under the stern gaze of guild alchemists.

## Guild Operations
- **Panacea Distribution:** Supplies licensed healing cloisters and military field hospitals worldwide.
- **Rivalry with Chem-Barons:** Fights an ongoing covert war against Piltover's **[[the-sump-syndicate]]**, hunting down black-market distribution of impure *Shimmer*.
