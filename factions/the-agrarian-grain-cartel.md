## Overview
The planetary cartel controlling the purchase, storage, maritime shipping, and price-fixing of wheat, barley, livestock, and staple foodstuffs across [[rubicon]], headquartered at the colossal Granary Silos in the Outer Ring of **[[the-ring-city]]**. Led by the formidable matriarch **[[maris-grain-bearer]]**, the Cartel holds the lifeblood of nations in its hands.

While other guilds trade in gold, jewels, and magical weapons, the Grain Cartel controls the bread that keeps populations from rioting and armies from starving in the field.

> [!read-aloud]
> Rows of cyclopean stone silos soaring ten stories above the deepwater wharves are filled with mountains of golden wheat, rye, and dried legumes. Massive conveyor ramps unload bulk grain ships arriving from Westfall, while grain-gaugers with brass sampling tubes test moisture and purity before stamping the sacks with the Cartel's gilded sheaf crest.

## Leverage of the Harvest
- **Famine Diplomacy:** Controls massive reserve silos that can feed entire continents during droughts or wars, using food shipments as an absolute diplomatic weapon to force unruly empires to respect Ring City treaties.
