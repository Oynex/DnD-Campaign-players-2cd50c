## Overview
A shadow mercenary organization with no official name, legal banner, or public headquarters. Known simply as *The Nameless*, they accept contracts from monarchs, merchant princes, criminals, or commoners—provided the payment meets their price.

> [!read-aloud]
> A figure wrapped in nondescript dun cloaks slides an unmarked wax seal across the tavern table. No names are spoken, no lineages touted; only the targets, the price in gold bullion, and the ironclad guarantee of a contract that will be honored to the grave.

## Organizational Structure
- **Handler Network:** Operates cells in every major metropolis. Operatives communicate strictly through dead drops and coded missives, ensuring total compartmentalization.
- **Tier 1 (Top 10 Warriors):** Legendary demigod-level killers rivaling the power of the Ten Roses of [[the-order-of-the-graveyard-rose]]. Rarely deployed except for astronomical sums.
- **Tier 2 (Ranked 11 to 30):** Renowned combat specialists, battle-mages, and master infiltrators.
- **Tier 3 (Ranked 31 to 40+):** Elite regional strike operatives.
- **Common Mercenaries:** Thousands of cutthroats and foot soldiers hired for frontline auxiliary warfare.
