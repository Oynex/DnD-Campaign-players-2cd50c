## Overview
The world's premier maritime guild, headquartered at the colossal High Lighthouse in the Outer Ring of **[[the-ring-city]]**. Led by Grand Navigator **[[thalor-wave-strider]]**, the League trains and licenses the master helmsmen, stellar navigators, and oceanic cartographers who guide every major naval and merchant fleet across [[rubicon]].

Without the League's enchanted sea charts and celestial star-dials, navigation through the storm-tossed channels and the dread perimeter of **[[the-serpentine-abyss]]** is considered suicide.

> [!read-aloud]
> Inside the rotating glass dome of the Great Star-Tower, colossal brass astrolabes and floating crystal globes rotate with clicking precision. Navigators in sea-blue coats study glowing holographic projections of ocean currents, measuring tidal swells and recording the migrations of abyssal sea leviathans with ink-stained calipers.

## Guild Operations
- **The Cartographic Monopoly:** Sells licensed sea charts that detail hidden reefs, siren coves, and oceanic safe corridors.
- **The Aegis Pilots:** Supplies the specialized navigators who steer **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]** through the boiling waters of the southern volcanic trenches.
