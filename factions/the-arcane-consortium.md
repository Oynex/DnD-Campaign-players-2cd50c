## Overview
The supreme academic association of mages, astronomers, and planar philosophers on [[rubicon]], headquartered within the soaring white spire of the Celestial Observatory in the Inner Ring of **[[the-ring-city]]**. Led by Arch-Astronomer **[[astrid-silver-weave]]**, the Consortium operates independently of national magical colleges, focusing on cosmic stability, planar drift, and the ancient celestial history of the world.

> [!read-aloud]
> High within the open-air dome of the Observatory, an enormous brass armillary sphere suspended by gravitational enchantments spins silently in mid-air. Prismatic rays of ambient starlight reflect from mirrored lenses, illuminating glowing celestial charts that map the dimensional boundaries between the mortal plane, the fey realms, and the dark void.

## Planetary Stewardship
- **Leyline Observation:** Monitors the subtle shifts along planetary mana lines, sharing concerns with the [[crystal-tower]] and [[academy-of-daggerfall]].
- **Planar Wards:** Maintains the spatial containment circles in the Ring City that prevent dimensional breaches during magical storms.
