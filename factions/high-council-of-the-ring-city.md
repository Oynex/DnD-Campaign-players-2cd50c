## Overview
The supreme governing assembly of **[[the-ring-city]]** and the ultimate geopolitical forum on [[rubicon]]. Operating from the majestic marble rotunda of the **Grand Spire of Concord** in the Inner Ring, the High Council dictates global maritime tariffs, standardizes international banking currencies, negotiates continental peace treaties, and oversees the funding of **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]**.

The High Council is composed of sixteen permanent voting seats:
- **The Mysterious First Seat (The Council Leader):** Occupied by an enigmatic, veiled figure whose true identity is the greatest state secret in the world—in truth, **[[the-lady-rose]]** herself, operating the levers of international power from behind the scenes.
- **The Ten Great Guildmasters:** The legendary supreme leaders of Rubicon's ten sovereign vocational guilds, representing finance, navigation, alchemy, engineering, arcana, espionage, agriculture, law, fine crafts, and mercenary arms.
- **The Five Sovereign Ambassadors:** Plenipotentiary envoys representing the five major empires contributing to the blockade fleet.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the colossal rotunda of the Grand Spire of Concord, shafts of sunlight illuminate sixteen tiered thrones arranged in a grand circle around a sunken mosaic map of the world. High above the polished white marble, flags of every guild and nation flutter softly. Here, an arch-banker in cloth-of-gold whispers with a High Elven ambassador, while a masked alchemical grandmaster glares across the chamber at an armored Noxian envoy. The quiet murmurs of these sixteen figures dictate the price of bread, the movement of fleets, and the fate of empires.

## The Sixteen Voting Seats

### The Leadership
- **The First Seat (Council Leader):** Publicly known only as the *Sovereign Arbiter of Concord*, an enigmatic hooded figure who casts the tie-breaking vote and moderates debates. In truth, the First Seat is held in absolute secrecy by **[[the-lady-rose]]**, founder of **[[the-order-of-the-graveyard-rose]]**.

### The Ten Great Guilds & Guildmasters
1. **The Iron Bank of the Three Rings:** Led by High Chancellor **[[aurelius-vance|Aurelius Vance]]** (The Sovereign of Coin).
2. **The Oceanic Navigators & Cartographers League:** Led by Grand Navigator **[[thalor-wave-strider|Thalor Wave-Strider]]** (The Helmsman).
3. **The Global Alchemical & Apothecary Conclave:** Led by Grand Magistra **[[veridiana-hex|Veridiana Hex]]** (The Chem-Matriarch).
4. **The International Artificers & Master-Masons Guild:** Led by Master Artificer **[[torvin-stone-cutter|Torvin Stone-Cutter]]** (The Great Builder).
5. **The Unseen Syndicate (The Guild of Whispers):** Led by Master **[[silas-mercer|Silas Mercer]]** (The Blind Broker).
6. **The Arcane Consortium of Planar Scholars:** Led by Arch-Astronomer **[[astrid-silver-weave|Astrid Silver-Weave]]** (The Star-Weaver).
7. **The Free Mercenary Brotherhood & Sellsword Guild:** Led by Warmaster **[[korren-blood-shield|Korren Blood-Shield]]** (The Iron Marshal).
8. **The Continental Agrarian & Grain Cartel:** Led by Lady **[[maris-grain-bearer|Maris Grain-Bearer]]** (The Ceres of the Isles).
9. **The High Juridical Synod (The Scales of Law):** Led by Chief Justiciar **[[elias-vane|Elias Vane]]** (The Inflexible Scale).
10. **The Silken Exchange (Fine Arts & Curiosities):** Led by Madame **[[lysandria-silk-bloom|Lysandria Silk-Bloom]]** (The Pearl of the Seas).

### The Five Sovereign Ambassadors
1. **High Elf Ambassador [[lord-faelorn-sun-treader|Lord Faelorn Sun-Treader]]** (Representing **[[summerset-isle]]**).
2. **Cyrodilic Plenipotentiary [[countess-valeria-aurelius|Countess Valeria Aurelius]]** (Representing **[[cyrodil]]**).
3. **Noxian War-Envoy [[centurion-drakos-iron-fang|Centurion Drakos Iron-Fang]]** (Representing **[[noxus]]**).
4. **Cathayan Minister [[minister-chen-long|Minister Chen Long]]** (Representing **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay]]**).
5. **Piltover Trade Consul [[consul-alaric-ferros|Consul Alaric Ferros]]** (Representing **[[piltover]]**).

## The Ultimate Political Playground
Behind the solemn etiquette of the assembly, the High Council is a maelstrom of cutthroat alliances and geopolitical proxy wars:
- **The Blockade Tithe Feuds:** Ambassador Sun-Treader relentlessly castigates Envoy Iron-Fang and Countess Aurelius for slashing their empires' mandated defense tithes to **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]**.
- **Monopoly Wars:** The Corporate Consul from Piltover is locked in bitter patent litigation with Master Artificer Torvin Stone-Cutter, while the Alchemical Conclave sabotages Piltover's chem-exports.
- **The Secret Arbiter:** While ambassadors and guildmasters plot assassinations and market crashes, **[[the-lady-rose]]** uses the worldwide charity network of the Graveyard Rose to intercept their secrets, ensuring no faction ever amasses enough power to fracture the planetary peace.
