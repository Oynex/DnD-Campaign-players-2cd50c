## Overview
The legendary order of sage-mages who maintain the [[crystal-tower]] on [[summerset-isle]]. Regarded as the foremost living authorities on arcane philosophy, dimensional barriers, and planar theory.

> [!read-aloud]
> Robed in spun silver and prismatic glass silk, the Sapiarchs walk the silent marble colonnades of the Crystal Tower. Their movements are deliberate and serene, carrying the collected wisdom of unbroken elven lineages spanning millennia.

## College of Sapiarchs
- **The First Sapiarch:** [[lord-aelindor]] - Master administrator and ideological leader.
- **Sapiarch of Planar Magic:** [[sapiarch-liriel]] - Analytical master of the barrier between planes.
- **Sapiarch of Leylines:** [[sapiarch-fenorel]] - Expert on environmental magic and advocate for mainland cooperation.
- **Sapiarch of Ethics:** [[sapiarch-sylanna]] - Conscience of the order who cautions against elven hubris.
- **Sapiarch of History:** [[sapiarch-thalien]] - Reclusive keeper of historical experiments and forgotten records.
- **Senior Advisor:** [[archon-thessalon]] - Patriarch of the elven race whose counsel guides their greatest deliberations.
