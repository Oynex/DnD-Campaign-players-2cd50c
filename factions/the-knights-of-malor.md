## Overview
The supreme standing holy army and inquisitorial sword of **[[orthodox-malorism]]**, headquartered within the colossal fortified cantons of the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]**. 

Commanded by the legendary **Five Warrior-Bishops**, the Knights of Malor number tens of thousands of consecrated paladins, templars, inquisitors, and hospitaler chaplains. They maintain garrisons and chapterhouses stationed at Churches of Malor across all provinces of [[tamriel]], collecting the mandatory imperial holy tithe, purging covens of dark sorcerers, banishing curses, and stamping out heresies with absolute martial fervor.

> [!read-aloud]
> Columns of holy templars clad in mirror-polished silver plate trimmed with gilded sun-inscriptions march through cathedral plazas to the thunder of brass war-horns. Behind them ride the Five Warrior-Bishops atop barded white destriers, their consecrated warhammers and radiant polearms glowing with solar enchantments that strike terror into fiends, witches, and heretics alike.

## The Five Warrior-Bishops (High Command)
The order is guided by five supreme prelates who represent the spectrum of faith, politics, and ruthless pragmatism within the Orthodox church:
1. **[[inquisitor-general-malachai|Inquisitor-General Militant Malachai]] (The Fanatic):** A terrifying inquisitor of pure, unbending conviction. He genuinely believes he is the living sword of Prophet Malor, personally leading frontline witch-hunts that burn rogue sorcerers and swamp hags across the marshes. He has zero interest in coin or political games, but shows zero mercy to anyone suspected of blasphemy.
2. **[[high-prelate-lucian-vane|High Prelate Lucian Vane]] (The Corrupt Politician):** A velvet-tongued master of political intrigue with secret back-channel ties to merchant cartels and underworld brokers. He siphons vast sums from the Holy Tithe into lavish private estates, sells confidential church pardons to wealthy aristocrats, and treats the holy army as an instrument of financial influence.
3. **[[holy-commander-alyssa-thorne|Holy Commander Alyssa Thorne]] (The Saintly Defender):** Deeply compassionate, incorruptibly pure of heart, and niece to Supreme Commander [[general-valorian-thorne]]. She commands the hospital brigades and vanguard paladins, personally funding orphanages and cloisters while riding to defend peasant settlements from monster raids—tragically shielded from the rot festering in Vivec.
4. **[[arch-justiciar-balthazar-karr|Arch-Justiciar Balthazar Karr]] (The Inquisitorial Judge):** Cold, clinical, and spiritually dead. He presides over heresy tribunals with terrifying legalistic precision, viewing the doctrines of Orthodox Malorism strictly as a mechanism of imperial order and social control.
5. **[[bishop-valen-of-the-marches|Bishop Valen of the Marches]] (The Weary Pragmatist):** A battle-hardened veteran commander who knows full well that the high clergy is compromised by wealth, yet believes the Knights of Malor are the only barrier keeping demonic incursions and foreign conquerors from annihilating the common folk. He compromises his principles daily and mentors Alyssa like a daughter to keep her uncorrupted.

## Presence Across Tamriel & The Tithe
- **Churches of Malor:** Every major settlement and provincial crossroad across Tamriel—from the heart of Cyrodiil to border bastions—features a fortified Church or Cathedral of Malor with an attached chapterhouse of knights.
- **Inquisitions and Witch-Hunts:** Ceaseless inquisitorial patrols cleanse ancient curses, hunt rogue warlocks, and exorcise haunted ruins.
- **The Mandatory Tithe:** The Knights enforce the collection of the imperial church tithe from every merchant caravan, tavern, and freehold, creating widespread public peace at the cost of bitter fiscal resentment.
- **Northern Cold War:** The Knights of Malor view the rugged clans of [[skyrim]] and their breakaway **[[new-malors]]** movement as dangerous schismatics, keeping heavily fortified castle-garrisons along the border mountain passes to contain northern holy raids.
