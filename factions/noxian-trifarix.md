## Overview
The supreme ruling triumvirate of the **[[noxus|Noxian Empire]]**, instituted by Grand General Jericho Swain to replace the decadent hereditary aristocracy with a ruthless meritocracy. 

Operating under the belief that no single individual should hold absolute power, the government is balanced between three supreme leaders, each embodying a core principle of Noxian strength:
- **Vision:** **[[swain|Grand General Jericho Swain]]** - Strategy, imperial administration, and supernatural demonic foresight.
- **Might:** **[[darius|Grand General Darius]]** - The Hand of Noxus, supreme commander of the legions and martial discipline.
- **Guile:** **[[leblanc]]** - The Pale Lady of the Mirrored Veil, master of occult manipulation, espionage, and secrets.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the high granite rotunda of the Immortal Bastion, three colossal stone seats surround a sunken iron council floor. Behind each seat looms an obsidian relief carving: a soaring raven for Vision, a crossed battleaxe for Might, and a fractured obsidian mirror for Guile.

## Governance & Imperial Conquest
From the council chambers of the Trifarix, all imperial decrees, legionary mobilizations, and annexation strategies flow:
- Oversees the pacified eastern lands of [[durotar]], [[stormwind]], [[east-fall]], and [[duskwood]].
- Coordinates the relentless front-line siege hammering against [[rivendel]].
- Formulates clandestine intelligence maneuvers across foreign courts to destabilize continental rivals.
