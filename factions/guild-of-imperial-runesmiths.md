## Overview
The strictly regulated, state-sanctioned guild of master artificers and stone-carvers holding exclusive legal rights to practice **[[runic-magic-crafting]]** within **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay|Grand Cathay]]**. 

Established in the wake of the cataclysm that created **[[the-rune-desert]]** four millennia ago, the Guild operates under the direct oversight of the Dragon Court. Because civilian experimentation with runic magic is punished by immediate execution, the Guild serves as both an elite monastic fraternity of master craftsmen and an internal regulatory inquisitor. Only smiths who pass twenty years of rigorous apprenticeships and swear unbreakable blood oaths to the **[[celestial-dragon-emperor]]** are granted the Golden Chisel of certification.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the vaulted granite cloisters of the imperial foundry, silence is broken only by the synchronized tapping of silver mallets and the hiss of cooling quenching oils. Master runesmiths robed in fire-retardant silk tabards work under the watchful eyes of imperial jade inquisitors. Every crystal shaving is weighed on analytical brass scales, and every engraved rune is audited against the sacred Imperial Lexicon of Permitted Script.

## Organization & Operations
- **The Grand Foundry of Nan-Gau:** The guild's primary research citadel, where senior masters forge sacred armor for the imperial palace guard and dragon generals.
- **The Outposts of Ryuko:** Operating out of **[[ryuko]]** under the patronage of **[[dragon-prince-xian]]**, guild prospectors lead licensed salvage expeditions into the deep sands of **[[the-rune-desert]]**, unearthing pre-cataclysm runestones and relics for historical preservation.
- **Strict Oversight:** Every runic weapon or talisman forged by a certified smith carries a unique registry mark stamped alongside the imperial seal. If an uncertified or black-market runic item is found anywhere in Cathay, guild marshals have absolute authority to confiscate the piece and execute the unlicensed maker.

## Court Politics & The Prince's Ambition
While the Guild officially reports to the Imperial Ministry in Nan-Gau, **[[dragon-prince-xian]]** has increasingly patronized and funded the guild’s desert workshops in Ryuko. By offering the smiths larger crystal quotas and ancient desert relics, Xian has quietly secured their loyalty, outfitting his desert nomad cavalry and elite guards with masterwork runic plate and enchanted dune-runners ahead of the looming imperial succession crisis.
