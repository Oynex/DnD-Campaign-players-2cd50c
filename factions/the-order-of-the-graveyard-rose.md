## Overview
A revered and benevolent humanitarian order spanning continents, operating across all sovereign borders on [[rubicon]]. Founded centuries ago in honor of the saintly folk hero **[[the-lady-rose]]**, the Order is dedicated entirely to the virtue of **unconditional kindness** and relief for the downtrodden. 

Wherever famine, war, or disaster strike, the gray-robed healers of the Graveyard Rose establish orphanages, hospices, and soup kitchens, treating all suffering mortals without regard for race, nationality, or social standing.

> [!read-aloud]
> A quiet relief clinic sits nestled amidst the crowded city alleyways, marked by a stone relief of a blooming rose carved over its archway. Inside, healers in simple ash-gray vestments clean wounds, dish out warm broth to shivering children, and offer soothing draughts to the dying. In a world torn by imperial conquest and dragon wars, the clinic feels like an impossible pocket of quiet, unconditional mercy.

## The Humanitarian Network
Across every major realm, the Order maintains three primary institutions:
- **Hospices & Sanitariums:** Providing skilled herbal and restorative healing to the destitute, penniless laborers, and wounded travelers free of charge.
- **Orphanages:** Providing shelter, food, literacy, and care to children orphaned by war and plague.
- **Sanctuary Houses:** Safe havens where any individual—whether a penniless beggar or a fleeing fugitive—can claim temporary sanctuary under the ancient, universally respected custom known as the **Law of the Rose**, which strictly forbids violence within their walls.

## The Thorn Wardens & The Legend of the Ten Roses
While the Order's healers are gentle and pacifistic, their sanctuaries are rarely disturbed:
- **The Thorn Wardens:** Silent, disciplined guardians clad in blackened mail adorned with gray rose cloaks who stand guard at clinic gates to turn away bandits, press-gangs, and looters.
- **The Folklore of the Ten Roses:** Ballads sung in taverns and bedtime stories whisper of "The Ten Roses"—ten peerless, almost mythical wanderer-knights said to serve as the Order's avenging angels, appearing out of nowhere whenever an atrocity threatens the innocent.
