## Overview
A legendary and deeply enigmatic organization spanning centuries, operating across multiple continents. Openly dedicated to the paramount virtue of **unconditional kindness**, the Order establishes orphanages, hospices, and soup kitchens wherever famine, war, or disaster strike. Behind this humanitarian mission, however, lies an immensely formidable organization feared by emperors and warlords alike.

> [!read-aloud]
> A small relief clinic nestled in the crowded city alleyways bears the emblem of a blooming black and crimson rose chiseled into white marble. Inside, healers in gentle ash-gray robes mend the wounded without asking for coin or allegiance. Yet in the shadows of the clinic, silent guardians in enchanted mail watch over the courtyard with lethal grace.

## The Humanitarian Network
- **Hospices & Sanitariums:** Providing medical treatment to the destitute.
- **Orphanages:** Raising and educating war orphans across every major realm.
- **Sanctuary Houses:** Places where any individual, regardless of crime or origin, can seek temporary safety under the law of the Rose.

## The Hidden Intelligence & Military Structure
The very facilities that feed the poor and heal the sick act as the most comprehensive intelligence network on [[rubicon]]:
- **The Ten Roses:** Ten extraordinary champions possessing demigod-level combat and magical prowess, deployed only when balance or innocence is catastrophically threatened.
- **The Secret Headquarters:** Unknown to the world, the Order's true grand headquarters is in the Inner Ring of [[the-ring-city]], where their founder, [[the-lady-rose]], presides in secret as First Seat of the global Guild Council.
