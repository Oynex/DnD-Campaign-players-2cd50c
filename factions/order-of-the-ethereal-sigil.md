## Overview
The elite circle of archmages who govern the arcane affairs of [[daggerfall]] and oversee the [[academy-of-daggerfall]]. All major magical policies, academy curricula, and research approvals in human territories flow through this conclave. The Headmaster of the Academy serves concurrently as the **Magister Primus** of the Order.

> [!read-aloud]
> Robed figures adorned in indigo silks and silver sigil brooches stand around a glowing crystalline basin in the upper observatory. Arcane equations and planar charts shimmer in the air above them, murmuring in synchronization with the hum of the city's Resonance Tower.

## Conclave Leadership
- **Magister Primus:** [[archmage-aldwyn]] - Visionary headmaster obsessed with pushing magical frontiers.
- **Security & Wards:** [[magister-lysindra-voss]] - Oversees academy security and ward maintenance.
- **Traditions & Archives:** [[loremaster-edric-astellion]] - Moral conscience and guardian of dangerous knowledge.
- **Academic Relations & Politics:** [[arcanist-jorrel-sterling]] - Charismatic lecturer and political maneuverer.
- **Enchantments & Supply:** [[mistress-sabine-hlaalu]] - Oversees commercial magical applications and funding.
