## Overview
The supreme planetary naval coalition and blockade armada of [[rubicon]], officially christened **The Grand Aegis Fleet** (and known in naval parlance simply as *The Aegis* or *The Containment Fleet*). Commissioned 1,500 years ago following the historic covenant that sealed the southern volcanic cataclysm, the fleet operates as an international standing naval armada jointly financed by the contributing empires and headquartered at the colossal deepwater bastions of **[[the-ring-city]]**.

Operating in permanent tactical union with the High Elven dragon-ships of **[[sky-watch]]** under **[[fleet-admiral-aislyn]]**, the Aegis Fleet maintains an unending cordon across the boiling southern trenches, repulsing abyssal sea terrors and rogue primordial behemoths.

> [!read-aloud]
> Staggering rows of multi-decked war galleons, ironclad steam rams, and dragon-prowed corvettes ride at anchor across the vast Outer Ring harbor. Each towering vessel flies the united blue-and-gold sunburst crest of the Grand Aegis Fleet beneath its national ensign. In the harbor narrows, heavy signal sirens blare as patrol squadrons depart for the southern patrol lines, their brass hulls scored by acid scars from abyssal horrors.

## The Contributing Nations & Subsidies
By ancient treaty ratified within the **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city]]**, five sovereign nations provide permanent warships, crews, and maritime tithes to sustain the Aegis:
1. **[[summerset-isle]]:** Provides the vanguard armada of dragon-ships, high-magic ward-casters, and supreme naval officers under High Admiral Aislyn.
2. **[[cyrodil]]:** Provides the heavy timber galleons, marine boarding cohorts, and massive grain rations from the southern agricultural valleys.
3. **[[noxus]]:** Formally contributes ironclad ram-frigates and hardened penal mariners, though contributions have steadily slowed under imperial expansion.
4. **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay]]:** Supplies massive jade-keeled treasure hulks carrying alchemical fire, silk sails, and specialized storm-ballistas.
5. **[[piltover]]:** Supplies cutting-edge magitech navigation compasses, submersible diving bells, and crystal-powered signaling systems.

## The Waning Crisis
The Aegis Fleet is currently in the grip of the **[[waning-of-the-containment-fleet]]**:
- Shorter-lived continental empires increasingly treat the southern menace as ancient folklore, slashing annual naval subsidies.
- Ring City merchant houses frequently lobby the High Council to repurpose reserve war galleons into private commercial trade convoys, dangerously thinning the patrol lines along the southern perimeter.
