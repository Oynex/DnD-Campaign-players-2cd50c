## Overview
The official planetary guild of couriers, cryptographers, and information brokers—known in taverns and royal chancelleries simply as the **Guild of Whispers**. Operating behind the legitimate facade of the International Postal & Telegraph Registry in the Middle Ring of **[[the-ring-city]]**, the Syndicate is commanded by the enigmatic Guildmaster **[[silas-mercer]]**.

From the highest royal bedchambers in Alinor to the trench trenches of Durotar, nothing of consequence happens on [[rubicon]] without an Unseen courier carrying a sealed dispatch.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the windowless, candlelit catacombs of the Postal Archive, thousands of carrier ravens, mechanical clockwork pigeons, and pneumatic message tubes click and flutter continuously. Cloaked figures in ash-gray masks sort encrypted letters onto massive sorting racks, stamping parchment with invisible wax seals that dissolve upon reading.

## The Information Market
- **State Secrets for Gold:** Sells ciphered diplomatic cables, troop manifests, and noble bastardy proofs to the highest bidder.
- **The Lady Rose's Blindspot:** While the Syndicate prides itself on knowing every secret in the world, they are completely unaware that the humble clinics of **[[the-order-of-the-graveyard-rose]]** form an even larger, invisible intelligence network that routinely out-maneuvers them.
