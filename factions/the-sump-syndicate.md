## Overview
The ruthless, highly organized criminal mafia that rules the toxic subterranean foundry slums, drainage networks, and chem-smelting sumps beneath **[[piltover]]**. Commanded by an unholy alliance of **Underground Crime Barons** and renegade chem-alchemists, the Syndicate is the third, shadowy pillar in Piltover's tri-cornered power struggle.

While **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]** and **[[high-patricians-of-piltover|The High Patricians]]** squabble over patents and legal charters in the glittering towers above, the Sump Syndicate controls the oil, blood, and muscle that makes the city run. They manage the smuggling tunnels, operate unlicensed crystal siphons directly beneath the extraction quarries, run illicit chem-distilleries, and maintain a vice-grip on the hundreds of thousands of day laborers who keep the factories alive.

> [!read-aloud]
> Neon green and amber chemical vapors leak from hissing brass pipes along the dripping iron catwalks of the Sump. Enforcers wearing leather trench coats and respirators fitted with glowing chem-valves stand guard outside iron speakeasies. Down here, gold coin means little—deals are sealed in pressurized vials of refined shimmer and stolen crystal shards.

## The Crime Barons of the Sump
1. **[[silas-the-red|Silas the Red]] (The Enforcer):** A hulking, scarred pit-boss who commands the heavy docks and raw muscle of the lower sumps. He manages protection rackets, strikebreaking contracts, and the smuggling of raw, unrefined crystal ore out to continental black markets.
2. **[[madame-rouge|Madame Rouge]] (The Poison Broker):** An enigmatic, masked underworld matriarch who presides over the gilded subterranean pleasure lounges and narcotics dens. She controls the illicit synthesis of industrial stimulants derived from **Bio-Synthetica** reject vats.
3. **[[viktor-garrow|Baron Viktor Garrow]] (The Chem-Artificer):** A disgraced former corporate engineer from Aethelgard Magitech-Refining Ltd. who now builds illegal, black-market magitech weaponry, crude automaton bruisers, and explosive demolition charges for the syndicates.

## Incestuous Entanglements with the Upper City
- **Corporate Symbiosis:** The Council relies on the Syndicate to supply cheap, uncontracted labor and quietly break independent worker unions. In return, the crime barons receive confidential tips on corporate transit schedules and siphoned chemical shipments.
- **Patrician Money Laundering:** The Syndicate launders millions in dirty chem-coin through legitimate real estate trusts owned by corrupt nobles in **[[high-patricians-of-piltover|House Delacroix]]**, giving the mafia massive clandestine leverage over the municipal courts.
- **The Worker Threat:** While the Syndicate exploits the factory hands, they are deeply terrified by **[[the-broken-cycle]]**. The cult of the Split Gear rejects syndicate narcotics and refuses syndicate protection, threatening to tear down the entire criminal racket along with the corporate factories.
