## Overview
The supreme governing body of [[cyrodil]], comprised of the **Six Great Noble Houses**. For 1,500 years, Cyrodiil was ruled as an autocratic monarchy from the Imperial City. Five hundred years ago, a historic rebellion overthrew the tyrannical Emperor, abolishing the monarchy and establishing the Council of Cyrodiil.

Each of the six houses oversees a vital pillar of statecraft, economy, and public welfare. While united by national duty and an enduring dread of imperial autocracy, the houses constantly maneuver for prestige, influence, and policy dominance.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the high domed amphitheater of the Council Tower, six grand semicircular marble desks encircle the ancient imperial seal. Beneath the gaze of solemn marble statues dating back two millennia, the six lords and ladies of the realm debate the fate of the nation with calculated, courtly precision.

## The Six Great Noble Houses & Councilors

1. **House Corvan (Engineering, Industry & Infrastructure)**
   - **Councilor:** [[thaddeus-corvan]]
   - **Portfolio:** Civil engineering, state highways, aqueducts, deep-vein mines, foundries, and the development of [[steam]] machinery. Maintains industrial ties with the dwarves across [[ductus-bridge]].
2. **House Astellion (Arcane Knowledge, Archives & Scholarship)**
   - **Councilor:** [[lady-vivienne-astellion]]
   - **Portfolio:** Preservation of ancient imperial archives, arcane intelligence, planetary history, and diplomatic liaison with arcane institutions like the [[academy-of-daggerfall]].
3. **House Aurelius (Defense, Military & Martial Oversight)**
   - **Councilor:** [[lord-meridian-aurelius]]
   - **Portfolio:** Strategic military readiness, procurement, weapon foundries, naval cruiser fleets, and constitutional oversight of the armed forces.
4. **House Sterling (Commerce, Treasury & Trade)**
   - **Councilor:** [[merchant-prince-cedric-sterling]]
   - **Portfolio:** Imperial mint, banking reserves, taxation, tariffs, domestic trade guilds, and overseas trade contracts with [[the-ring-city]].
5. **House Indoril (Religion, Faith & Culture)**
   - **Councilor:** [[archbishop-aldwin-indoril]]
   - **Portfolio:** The state clergy, temple networks, civil ceremonies, public relief, and spiritual guidance for the realm.
6. **House Bellator (Law, Judiciary & Internal Security)**
   - **Councilor:** [[lord-sylus-bellator]]
   - **Portfolio:** Imperial magistrates, the legal penal code, high courts, correctional bastions, and joint oversight of domestic security.

## Constitutional Military Balance
To prevent another autocratic tyrant from ever seizing the throne, the Cyrodiil constitution explicitly dictates that **no noble house may hold direct command over the armed forces**:
- **Supreme Armed Command:** The ultimate operational leader of all Cyrodiil armed forces is **General [[general-valorian-thorne]]**.
- **Oversight of House Aurelius:** Thorne's command is strictly regulated and scrutinized by House Aurelius under **[[lord-meridian-aurelius]]**, whose constitutional charge is to guarantee national defense does not falter while preventing any military dictator from emerging.
- **Internal Security:** Domestic peace and garrison enforcement are managed jointly between Thorne's legions and House Bellator's judicial watch under **[[lord-sylus-bellator]]**.
- **Frontier & Covert Commands:** Northern border defenses are anchored by decorated siege veteran **General [[general-cassandra-vale]]**, while tactical prodigy **General [[general-kael-stormbringer]]** operates undercover in the west.
