## Overview
The governing body of [[cyrodil]], comprised of thirteen noble houses united in governance since the overthrow of the tyrannical Emperor in 1505 PA. Each house specializes in a distinct sphere of government, economy, or civil service. United by solemn oath and imperial patriotism, they nonetheless engage in intricate political maneuvering behind closed doors.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the high domed amphitheater of the Council Tower, thirteen semicircular marble benches encircle the central seal of Cyrodil. Under the watchful gaze of sculpted past statesmen, the lords and ladies of the realm debate policy with measured words that carry the fate of millions.

## The Thirteen Noble Houses & Councilors

1. **House Redoran (Engineering & Infrastructure)**
   - **Councilor:** [[thaddeus-redoran]]
   - Specializes in civil engineering, road networks, aqueducts, and mining.
2. **House Moth (Arcane Knowledge & Scholarship)**
   - **Councilor:** [[lady-vivienne-moth]]
   - Oversees historical archives, imperial libraries, and arcane intelligence.
3. **House Septim (Military & National Defense)**
   - **Councilor:** [[lord-meridian-septim]]
   - Directs the standing imperial legions and border fortification garrisons.
4. **House Tharn (Trade & Commerce)**
   - **Councilor:** [[merchant-prince-cedric-tharn]]
   - Controls mercantile shipping, tariffs, trade treaties, and merchant caravans.
5. **House Indoril (Religion & Spiritual Guidance)**
   - **Councilor:** [[archbishop-aldwin-indoril]]
   - Guides the state clergy, temples, moral courts, and historical rites.
6. **House Dren (Agriculture & Food Supply)**
   - **Councilor:** [[matron-lleraya-dren]]
   - Manages the breadbasket heartlands, grain reserves, and farm estates.
7. **House Bruma (Northern Border & Defense)**
   - **Councilor:** [[lady-astrid-bruma]]
   - Protects the frigid northern frontier passes against wild clans and raids.
8. **House Hlaalu (Treasury & Finance)**
   - **Councilor:** [[lord-nalvus-hlaalu]]
   - Directs the imperial mint, taxation, banking reserves, and state investments.
9. **House Anvil (Naval Affairs & Ports)**
   - **Councilor:** [[commodore-marcellus-anvil]]
   - Commands maritime defense fleets, coast guards, and commercial docks.
10. **House Telvanni (Arcane Anomalies & Planar Research)**
    - **Councilor:** [[lord-arvyn-telvanni]]
    - Investigates planar rifts, forbidden phenomena, and ancient relics.
11. **House Armorer (Industry, Mining & Crafting)**
    - **Councilor:** [[lady-gwynora-armorer]]
    - Manages foundries, arms manufacture, and master craft guilds.
12. **House Cheydinhal (Law, Judiciary & Internal Security)**
    - **Councilor:** [[lord-sylus-cheydinhal]]
    - Administers imperial courts, magisterial law, and the civilian watch.
13. **House Choral (Culture, Diplomacy & Foreign Affairs)**
    - **Councilor:** [[lady-elenya-choral]]
    - Coordinates diplomatic envoys, foreign treaties, and the arts.

## Military Leadership of Cyrodil
The council relies on three distinguished generals to execute military decisions:
- [[general-cassandra-vale]]: Master of siege operations and the northern fortresses.
- [[general-valorian-thorne]]: Veteran conservative leader of the central infantry legions.
- [[general-kael-stormbringer]]: Young tactical prodigy currently dispatched in disguise to [[daggerfall]].
