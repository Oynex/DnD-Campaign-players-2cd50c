## Overview
The planetary guild regulating all private armies, sellsword companies, bounty hunters, and adventuring charters on [[rubicon]], headquartered within the colossal iron citadel of the Outer Ring in **[[the-ring-city]]**. Led by Warmaster **[[korren-blood-shield]]**, the Brotherhood ensures that private warfare remains orderly, contracts are legally binding, and mercenaries do not turn into uncontrollable bandit hordes.

> [!read-aloud]
> Shouting drill-masters, clashing steel blades, and the clatter of armored boots echo across the massive cobblestone drill squares of the Mercenary Bastion. Hundreds of foreign sellswords—Nordic berserkers, desert duelists, and human skirmishers—sign blood-inked indenture charters under the cold gaze of veteran guild captains.

## The Business of Blood
- **The Contract Seal:** When an empire or merchant house hires private sellswords through the Brotherhood, both sides deposit gold and collateral. Breaching a contract results in a global bounty placed on the offender by the guild.
- **Supplying the Aegis:** Rotates thousands of veteran shock-marines into the rosters of **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]** for dangerous tours along the southern blockade.
