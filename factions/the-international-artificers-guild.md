## Overview
The supreme guild of master builders, structural engineers, and stone-shapers, headquartered within the volcanic basalt halls of the Middle Ring of **[[the-ring-city]]**. Commanded by the legendary dwarven patriarch **[[torvin-stone-cutter]]**, the Guild oversees the construction of colossal harbor seawalls, sky-aqueducts, and fortress bastions across [[rubicon]].

Combining ancient dwarven rune-masonry with human mechanical engineering, the Guild built the very concentric rings of the Ring City itself.

> [!read-aloud]
> The thunder of massive trip-hammers and the roar of smelting furnaces shake the stone floor of the Great Foundry. Master masons carve cyclopean granite blocks with runic chisels, while engineers assemble towering cranes powered by counterweighted pulleys and water-turbines capable of hoisting fifty-ton seawall stones with ease.

## Influence & Power
- **Public Works Monopoly:** No city wall, imperial aqueduct, or mega-bridge (including the maintenance contracts on [[ductus-bridge]]) can be built without Guild certification.
- **Defending the Ring City Walls:** Maintains the three concentric perimeter seawalls of the Ring City against abyssal tidal waves.
