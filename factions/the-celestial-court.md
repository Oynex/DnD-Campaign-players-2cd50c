## Overview
The sovereign imperial dynasty of the **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay]]**, comprising the last true **Loong (serpentine flying dragons)** in existence on [[rubicon]]. 

While serpentine dragons once ruled the skies and purged the world's dragons ten millennia ago, today only the imperial family carries this pure primordial bloodline, possessing the divine power to assume both serpentine and human forms.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the Jade Throne Room of Nan-Gau, incense coils around titanic pillars sculpted with winding golden dragons. Serpentine courtiers in robes of spun silk sit beneath the high dias, their eyes glowing with draconic majesty amidst the tense, hushed whispers of an impending succession war.

## The Imperial Dynasty
- **The Sovereign:** **[[celestial-dragon-emperor]]** - Revering ruler of Cathay, currently ailing.
- **The Guardian of the Wall:** **[[the-thunder-dragon]]** - Sister of the Emperor and supreme custodian of [[hells-gate]].
- **The Heir-Designate:** **[[princess-mei-ling]]** - The newborn infant daughter of the Emperor's favorite concubine, officially declared the next Empress.
- **The Contenders:**
  - **[[dragon-princess-zhao]]** (Governor of [[hana-no-miyako]])
  - **[[dragon-prince-xian]]** (Governor of [[ryuko]])
  - **[[dragon-prince-longwei]]** (Governor of [[yunshan-isles]])
- **The Black Sheep:** **[[prince-yin-xiao]]** - The third son, widely dismissed as a drunken, frivolous fool.
