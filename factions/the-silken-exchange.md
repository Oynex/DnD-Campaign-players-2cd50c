## Overview
The planetary guild regulating the trade in high-value antiquities, rare gems, enchanted musical instruments, exotic beasts, and haute couture, headquartered within the opulent Crystal Arcades of the Middle Ring in **[[the-ring-city]]**. Led by Madame **[[lysandria-silk-bloom]]**, the Exchange caters to the extravagant desires of royal courts and archmages across [[rubicon]].

While other guilds trade in raw steel, grain, and gold, the Silken Exchange trades in vanity, status, and ancient cultural wonders.

> [!read-aloud]
> Sunlight filters through stained-glass domes, illuminating cascading waterfalls of Cathayan silk, velvet tapestries woven with glowing fey thread, and velvet display pedestals showcasing cut star-sapphires and bottled sirens' songs. The air smells of sweet gardenias and distilled rosewater, filled with the gentle melodies of harps played by masked bards.

## Power of Culture
- **The Antiquities Vaults:** Authenticates, auctions, and safeguards legendary artifacts and relics recovered from ancient ruins across the world.
