## Overview
A legendary, dreaded mercenary company clad in silent, non-reflective black iron plate armor that materializes without warning at key, catastrophic battles across [[rubicon]]. 

To military scholars and grizzled veterans, **The Warpath** is not a conventional army—they are a living battlefield nightmare. They fly no heraldry, pledge no fealty, and hold no interest in gold, land, or politics. They appear exclusively when a battle has become utterly one-sided, slaughter is imminent, and the despair of the losing side has reached a fever pitch.

> [!read-aloud]
> A suffocating chill sweeps across the blood-soaked earth, swallowing the roar of combat in unnatural silence. Emerging through the smoke of burning siege engines, silent figures clad in jagged, shadow-drinking iron advance in perfect, horrific lockstep. They carry no banners and speak no battlecries, moving with a dread, almost non-human precision that freezes the blood of kings and generals alike.

## The Currency of Despair
The Warpath cannot be hired by ambassadors or purchased with coin:
- **The Bargain:** When total defeat is guaranteed, one of the Warpath's terrifying dread commanders materializes directly before the leader of the despairing forces.
- **The Price:** The commander is offered a grim, absolute choice: *Give up everything you cherish—your life, your bloodline, your soul, your legacy, and all you hold dear.*
- **The Aftermath:** If the leader accepts the pact, the Warpath descends upon the opposing army with supernatural, devastating savagery. In minutes, the triumphant invaders are routed, broken, and slaughtered until it is *they* who drown in absolute despair. But the contractor never enjoys victory—the Warpath always claims its price down to the last drop, leaving the leader dead, their reputation forever tarnished, and their heirs cursed.

## Historical Intervention: The First Siege of Stormwind
The most famous recorded manifestation of the Warpath in modern history occurred during the **First Invasion of [[stormwind]]** by [[noxus]]:
- The Noxian vanguard had shattered the coastal fortifications, and the fall of Stormwind was hours away.
- In absolute agony, the former King of Stormwind called upon the Warpath, agreeing to forfeit everything he cherished.
- The black-clad army crashed into the Noxian flank with cataclysmic fury, driving the imperial legions entirely out of [[east-fall]] and saving the kingdom for another generation.
- **The Total Price:** The Warpath claimed its pound of flesh: the king died, his royal reputation was completely hollowed out so that his own people now revile his memory as a failure, and his surviving son, **[[prince-liam-of-stormwind]]**, was cursed never to return to his homeland.
- It was only years later, after the kingdom sat leaderless and broken by the curse, that Noxus returned in the *Second Invasion* to annex the land.

## The Legend of Darius
During the bloody chaos of the First Siege of Stormwind, **Grand General [[darius]]** faced one of the Four Paths of the Warpath in single combat. 

Darius fought with monstrous ferocity and survived the encounter—earning him a mythic status across Rubicon as the **only mortal in recorded history** known to have crossed blades with a commander of the Warpath and lived to tell the tale.
