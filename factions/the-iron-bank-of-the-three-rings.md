## Overview
The supreme financial institution of [[rubicon]], headquartered within the colossal subterranean granite vaults of the Inner Ring of **[[the-ring-city]]**. Commanded by High Chancellor **[[aurelius-vance]]**, the Iron Bank holds the gold reserves, sovereign debt bonds, and currency minting monopolies of every civilized kingdom in the world.

Whether a king requires legions to invade a neighboring province or an merchant fleet to explore the outer reaches, the gold almost invariably flows from the Iron Bank. 

> [!read-aloud]
> Beneath soaring vault arches of black granite reinforced with enchanted Adamantine bands, hundreds of clerks in cloth-of-gold robes weigh foreign bullion on gigantic counterweighted brass scales. Deep beneath the counting halls, three concentric rings of submerged vault doors protect mountains of minted platinum, promissory ledgers, and the mortgage deeds of entire continents.

## Financial Hegemony
- **Sovereign Debt:** Holds the national debt bonds of Cyrodiil, Piltover, and the independent merchant realms. If an empire defaults, the Iron Bank finances its enemies.
- **The Aegis Tithe Vaults:** Manages the multi-million gold reserve earmarked for **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]**, frequently clashing with naval admirals over defense budget audits.
