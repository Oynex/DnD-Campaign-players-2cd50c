## Overview
The planetary guild of international magistrates, treaty scholars, and maritime arbiters, headquartered within the marble Basilica of Law in the Inner Ring of **[[the-ring-city]]**. Led by Chief Justiciar **[[elias-vane]]**, the Synod presides over the International Court of Arbitration.

Whenever two sovereign empires dispute maritime fishing lanes, trade tariffs, or treaty breaches, their legal cases are tried before the robed magistrates of the Synod, whose binding rulings are backed by the threat of global commercial embargoes.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the echoing, cavernous basilica of polished white granite, forty-nine magistrates in black samite robes sit upon high judicial tiers. Before them, golden scales suspended from the high ceiling sway gently in the draft, while clerks record arguments in iron-bound tomes of international statutory law.

## Juridical Power
- **Treaty Guarantor:** Stores the original ratified parchments of every international pact on Rubicon, including the 1,500-year-old Covenant of the Southern Blockade.
