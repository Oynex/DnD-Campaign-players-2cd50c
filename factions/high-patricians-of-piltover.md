## Overview
The ancient, landed nobility of **[[piltover]]**, representing the "Old Money" families whose ancestry, coastal estates, and municipal charters existed centuries before the discovery of the radiant crystals in the Great Crater. 

Operating from the grand marble colonnades of the **Municipal Senate High Chambers**, the High Patricians serve as the constitutional counterweight to **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]**. While the corporate conglomerates boast billions in magitech revenue, the Patricians hold the historical land titles, water rights, and constitutional court appointments that legally permit those corporations to exist.

> [!read-aloud]
> Beneath soaring frescoed ceilings depicting the founding merchant lords of the coast, aristocrats in tailored silk waistcoats, ermine stoles, and family signet rings sip spiced vintages from crystal flutes. Behind their polished ancestral poise and disdain for corporate upstarts, their eyes are sharp, calculating every shift in municipal stock and Senate voting blocs.

## The Four Great Noble Houses
Four illustrious patrician dynasties form the heart of the aristocratic resistance:
1. **House Delacroix:** The oldest and most prestigious bloodline in Piltover, holding the hereditary High Magistracy of the Constitutional Court. Led by **[[julian-delacroix|Patriarch Julian Delacroix]]**, they control the deep harbor berths and historical real estate beneath the upper cliffs.
2. **House Valmont:** Grand agricultural and forestry barons whose sprawling coastal vineyards and timber groves supply the specialized woods used in airship framing. Led by **[[genevieve-valmont|Lady Genevieve Valmont]]**, a ruthless political operator who holds massive mortgage debts over several Council directors.
3. **House Beauchamp:** Masters of banking, ancestral vaults, and continental treasury loans. Led by **[[tristan-beauchamp|Lord Tristan Beauchamp]]**, they finance international merchant trade and covertly back privateer fleets along the western sea lanes.
4. **House Castillon:** Renowned for their aristocratic officer lineage, running the city's ceremonial High Guard and the Admiralty Guild. Led by **[[roland-castillon|Commodore Roland Castillon]]**, who fiercely resists corporate attempts to replace human guards with automaton sentinels.

## The Tri-Cornered Shadow Games
- **Contempt for Corporate "Nouveau Riche":** The Patricians view the corporate executives of the Council as vulgar, greedy tradesmen who lack breeding, culture, and long-term vision. They use Senate filibusters and regulatory injunctions to strangle corporate overreach.
- **Underground Alliances:** While outwardly horrified by the filth of the subterranean sumps, several noble scions—most notably in House Delacroix and House Beauchamp—covertly finance **[[the-sump-syndicate]]**, laundering underworld chem-profits through offshore banks in exchange for clandestine assassination contracts against corporate board members.
- **The Peasant Blindspot:** Oblivious to the true desperation of the working class, the Patricians dismiss **[[the-broken-cycle]]** as petty gutter rabble, unaware that the worker cult has already infiltrated their domestic servant quarters and carriage stables.
