## Overview
The supreme governing executive of **[[piltover]]**, comprised of the chief executives and board chairmen of the **Five Great Conglomerates** that dominate the city-state's economy, technology, and military production. 

While the Council officially holds constitutional authority over municipal laws, trade tariffs, and the planetary export of [[magitech]], it is locked in a vicious, high-stakes **three-way power struggle** for the soul of the city against the **[[high-patricians-of-piltover]]** (the old landed aristocracy) and **[[the-sump-syndicate]]** (the underground chem-barons).

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the soaring, gold-and-glass dome of the Council Spire, five high-backed chairs of polished walnut and brass face outward across the city skyline. Below, crystal-powered automaton sentinels guard the marble dais while councilors in tailored velvet coats debate multi-million gold-piece patent disputes with the cold, measured precision of ledger clerks.

## The Five Corporate Conglomerates
The council is comprised strictly of the heads of Piltover's five regulated monopolies:
1. **Aethelgard Magitech-Refining Ltd.:** Monopolizes the chemical processing, cutting, and stabilization of raw arcane crystals dredged from the Great Crater basin through advanced **[[crystal-refinement]]**. Commanded by Chief Executive **[[vanderbilt-ferros|Vanderbilt Ferros]]**.
2. **Krom & Sons Locomotive (KSL):** Dominates the manufacture of heavy pressurized rail engines, armored rolling stock, and long-range sky-freighters (**[[krom-and-sons-locomotives]]**) connecting Piltover to [[rivendel]]. Commanded by Baroness **[[mireille-krom|Mireille Krom]]**.
3. **The Aether-Weave Munitions:** Produces the high-yield **[[aether-weave-firearms]]**, automated cannon batteries, and energy barrier generators that defend the western passes against Noxian expansion. Commanded by Director **[[kaelen-voss|Kaelen Voss]]**.
4. **Bio-Synthetics (Bio-Synthetica):** Synthesizes industrial stimulants, chemical lubricants, and healing elixirs (**[[synthetic-chem-enhancements]]**); notoriously compromised by illicit underworld laboratories. Commanded by **[[dr-corin-reve|Dr. Corin Reve]]**.
5. **Trans-Continental Crystal Network (TCCN):** Operates the planetary telegraph relays, crystal power-grid cables, and communication towers spanning western Aethelgard. Commanded by Madame **[[helena-vane-dupont|Helena Vane-DuPont]]**.

## The Tri-Cornered Web of Leverage
- **The Aristocratic Veto:** The corporate directors constantly clash with the **[[high-patricians-of-piltover]]**, who use their ancestral control of the Municipal Senate to block corporate rezoning laws and patent seizures.
- **The Factory Floor Crisis:** The Council relies heavily on **[[the-sump-syndicate]]** to keep the subterranean workforce submissive. However, rising discontent has birthed **[[the-broken-cycle]]**, an armed worker cult that threatens to blow the assembly lines to scrap.
