## Overview
A sweeping expanse of rolling golden prairies and rich river plains situated in west-central [[aethelgard]], directly west of the central mountain spine. Dotted with whitewashed windmills, grain elevators, and agricultural settlements centered on [[gold-shire]], Westfall is celebrated as **the grain basket of the world**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Endless fields of amber grain sway under the warm afternoon breeze like an ocean of liquid gold. Massive timber windmills slowly turn against the horizon, their grinding stones humming as horse-drawn wagons heap bushels of fresh grain destined for river barges and market halls.

## Strategic Target of Noxus
Westfall's agricultural output feeds not only local farmsteads, but also supplies the dense urban populations of [[piltover]] and shipping lines to the central sea. 

The expansionist war machine of [[noxus]] has long lusted after Westfall's bountiful soils to feed its massive military legions, making its capture the ultimate objective behind the siege at [[rivendel]].

## Nordic Incursions
Because Westfall's river valleys lead out toward the western ocean, reaver longships from the **[[skyrim|Northern Jarldom of Skyrim]]** periodically sail down through the coastal delta to launch seasonal pillaging raids on outer grain silos.
