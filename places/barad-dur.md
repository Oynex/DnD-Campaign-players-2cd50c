## Overview
A legendary, cyclopean mountain fortress hidden deep within the frozen interior ranges of [[northrend]]. Originally the grand sanctuary of **[[the-knights-of-the-silver-dawn]]**, Barad-dûr was sealed from the inside two millennia ago when the thousand-strong knightly brotherhood withdrew from the world in despair.

Today, it stands as the ultimate white whale for explorers, tomb-raiders, and arcane antiquarians across [[rubicon]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Looming through the violent whiteout blizzard, jagged black spires of dark granite and frozen iron rise like a crown of thorns above glaciated mountain crags. A colossal frost wyrm circles the high watchtowers, while adamantine gates hundreds of feet high remain entombed in sheets of ancient, unmelting ice.

## Expeditions & Doom
- **The Lure of Relics:** Rumors persist that the fortress houses two thousand years of accumulated chivalric treasures, masterwork armaments, and ancient magical archives from the era of the Silver Dawn.
- **Unbroken Failure:** Scores of well-funded expeditions from Skyrim, Cyrodiil, and the Ring City have set out across the ice caps to locate the citadel. None have breached the walls, and the vast majority freeze to death or vanish without a trace before catching sight of the spires.
