## Overview
A colossal stone-and-iron megastructure bridge connecting the eastern mainland of [[cyrodil]] across the oceanic strait to the rugged mountainous island of the twin dwarven strongholds.

> [!read-aloud]
> An awe-inspiring wonder of ancient stonework and dwarven masonry stretching miles across the churning ocean strait. Colossal granite pillars descend hundreds of fathoms into the seabed, supporting a multi-lane highway fortified with fortified watchtowers, aqueducts, and iron toll-gates.

## Strategic Importance
The Ductus Bridge serves as the sole overland conduit between human civilization and the deep subterranean mining empires of [[khazad-dum]] and [[gundabad]]. Heavy caravans carrying raw metals, gemstone tribute, and [[steam]]-forged machinery travel across it day and night.
