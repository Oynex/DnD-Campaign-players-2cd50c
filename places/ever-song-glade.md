## Overview
A vast, primeval emerald rainforest blanketing the south-central expanse of [[aethelgard]]. Ever-Song Glade is the ancestral homeland of the **Wood Elves**, distant, reclusive cousins of the Asur of [[summerset-isle]]. 

Unlike their high elven kin who sail pristine marble galleons, the Wood Elves dwell high within monumental canopy redwoods, fiercely attuned to the untamed spirit of the forest.

> [!read-aloud]
> Sunlight filters through colossal emerald canopies in shafts of golden light. Ancient moss-draped ruins and hanging ropeways weave seamlessly between colossal trunk perches, while the haunting melody of elven woodland flutes drifts through the mist like birdsong.

## Bitterness Toward Piltover
The Wood Elves harbor intense anger toward [[piltover]] for the aggressive industrial clear-cutting and deforestation along the western forest fringes to fuel Piltover's runaway crystal-refining foundries.

## Reluctant Guardians of Rivendel
Despite their hatred of Piltover's industrial greed, the Wood Elves harbor no illusions about [[noxus]]:
- They know that if Noxian legions break through the central mountains, Noxus will not merely cut timber—they will enslave or exterminate the elves and clear-burn the entire glade.
- Consequently, the Wood Elves provide **reluctant but indispensable military aid to the defense of [[rivendel]]**. 
- Because the Wood Elves know every hidden animal trail, razor crag, and mountain pass better than anyone alive, their scouts act as the eyes and ears of the bottleneck garrison, intercepting Noxian vanguard infiltrators before they reach the bridge.
