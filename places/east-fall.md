## Overview
A fertile agricultural expanse of gentle hills and farmsteads situated on the eastern coastline of [[aethelgard]], south of the great fortress of [[noxus]] and adjacent to [[stormwind]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Paved military roads cut straight through sprawling turnip and barley farms, lined with iron wayposts and Noxian supply depots. Farmers work the soil under the watchful eyes of mounted legion patrols, their harvest requisitioned directly for the western siege trains.

## Role
East Fall serves as the primary eastern breadbasket for the Noxian imperial army, feeding the garrisons of [[the-immortal-bastion]] and the legions garrisoning [[stormwind]].
