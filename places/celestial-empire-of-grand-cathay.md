## Overview
The oldest civilization with recorded history on [[rubicon]]—revered as **The First Lands**. Occupying the entire northern and eastern expanse of the continent of [[cathay]] behind the colossal barrier of [[hells-gate]], Grand Cathay was founded at the dawn of the world when the High Elves still dwelled in raw woods and humans were scattered nomadic tribes.

Grand Cathay is a realm of profound spiritual balance where mortals, benign nature spirits, and fey live in harmony under the reign of the immortal serpentine dragons.

> [!read-aloud]
> Endless terraces of jade-green rice fields cascade down misty mountain valleys, framed by crimson-lacquered pagodas and ancient arched bridges. High in the clouds, celestial serpentine dragons glide gracefully without wings, their scales glistening like opals in the morning sun as dragon-prowed river barges drift along four great serpentine rivers.

## The Loong Dragons & The Dragon God
The ruling dynasty of Cathay are **Loong dragons**—divine, sentient serpentine flying dragons capable of assuming human form:
- **Extra-Planar Origin:** Ancient myths suggest the Loong dragons originally crossed over from another plane before mortal memory began.
- **The Dragon Purge (10,000 Years Ago):** Over ten millennia ago, the primordial Loong dragons purged chromatic and metallic dragons across Rubicon. Today, the surviving chromatic and metallic dragons are scattered or in hiding, while true serpentine dragons have dwindled until only the imperial family remains (just 3 to 4 true dragons alive today).
- **The Third Dragon God:** The primordial Dragon Emperor proved so unimaginably powerful that he contested the draconic deities **Bahamut** and **Tiamat**, establishing himself as the revered **Third Dragon God** before ascending into celestial godhood.
- **Degenerate Wyverns:** The winged wyverns and bestial dragons found across the rest of Rubicon are merely devolved, animalistic descendants of these ancient beings.

## Major Provinces & Cities
- **[[nan-gau]]:** The ancient capital and the first recorded mortal settlement in world history, situated where four great serpentine rivers diverge.
- **[[hana-no-miyako]]:** The breathtaking northern mountain province shrouded in pink cherry blossoms, governed by [[dragon-princess-zhao]].
- **[[ryuko]]:** The sun-bleached western port on the **Rune Desert**, governed by [[dragon-prince-xian]].
- **[[yunshan-isles]]:** The eastern floating mountain archipelago on the Dread Sea, governed by [[dragon-prince-longwei]].
- **[[hells-gate]]:** The monumental red Great Wall raised 1,500 years ago by [[the-thunder-dragon]] to seal off the southern volcanic horrors.

## The Looming Succession Crisis
The current [[celestial-dragon-emperor]] is gravely ailing. Defying imperial precedent, he recently declared his newborn daughter, **[[princess-mei-ling]]**, as the rightful Crown Princess. This sudden decree has outraged his three older dragon offspring, setting the stage for an explosive civil war among the provinces of Hana no Miyako, Ryuko, and the Yunshan Isles.
