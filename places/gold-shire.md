## Overview
A bustling, prosperous agricultural market town situated in the heart of [[westfall]] on [[aethelgard]]. Surrounded by thousands of acres of tilled grain and orchard groves, Gold Shire serves as the commercial epicenter for the agrarian heartlands.

> [!read-aloud]
> Cobblestone crossroads bustle with farmers hawking seasonal produce, livestock drovers, and flour merchants bartering beside timber-framed taverns and thatched grain storehouses. The comforting scent of baked bread and brewing ale fills the air.

## Role
Gold Shire functions as the central logistical nexus where farmers from across Westfall pool their grain harvest into bulk convoys bound for river transit to [[piltover]] and export harbors.
