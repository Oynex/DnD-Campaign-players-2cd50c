## Overview
The northern anchor of the twin dwarven mountain strongholds situated on the rugged island sub-continent directly east of [[cyrodil]], connected to the mainland via the [[ductus-bridge]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Carved directly into the heart of jagged basalt cliffs, colossal dwarven king statues flank gigantic bronze gates that disappear into the abyssal dark of the mountain. Far below, the ceaseless thud of magma-powered forge hammers vibrates through the bedrock.

## Subterranean Splendor
Khazad-dûm is renowned for deep-vein mithril mining, vast subterranean highways, and master architectural craftsmanship. It maintains an ancient alliance with its southern twin, [[gundabad]].
