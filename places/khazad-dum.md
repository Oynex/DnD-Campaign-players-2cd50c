## Overview
The northern anchor of the twin dwarven mountain strongholds, situated on the rugged island sub-continent directly east of [[cyrodil]] and linked to the mainland via the colossal [[ductus-bridge]]. 

Unlike its martial sister fortress to the south, Khazad-dûm is **open, vibrant, and welcoming to outsiders**, serving as the primary commercial and cultural embassy of the dwarven clans to the mortal world.

> [!read-aloud]
> Carved directly into towering basalt cliffs, monumental statues of dwarven forebears flank colossal bronze gates that open into brightly illuminated subterranean boulevards. Crystal chandeliers and glowing magma channels cast golden light across bustling multi-tiered markets, where the clinking of gold coins mingles with the cheerful roar of dwarven taverns and the ringing of silversmith hammers.

## The Great Delving Bazaar
Khazad-dûm is universally renowned for its open trading halls:
- **World-Renowned Crafts:** Foreign merchants, guild traders, and collectors flock from across [[rubicon]] to purchase famous dwarven craftworks—intricate mithril filigree, precision-cut gemstones, rune-carved jewelry, and unbreakable architectural masonry.
- **The Mainland Trade Accord:** Khazad-dûm maintains an enduring, centuries-old bilateral trade pact with [[cyrodil]]. Imperial merchant caravans haul hundreds of tons of grain, cured meats, fresh fruit, fine wines, and timber across [[ductus-bridge]], returning loaded with masterwork tools, refined ores, and mechanical components.
- **Kinship with Gundabad:** While Khazad-dûm handles commerce and diplomacy, it stands in unbreakable lockstep with its southern twin, **[[gundabad]]**, sharing deep subterranean road networks and mutual defense oaths.
