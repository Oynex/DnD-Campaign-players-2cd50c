## Overview
The known world and overarching campaign setting of **Rubicon**. A world defined by colliding eras, diverse continental civilizations, emerging steam and crystalline magitech industries, high politics, and ancient primordial mysteries.

> [!read-aloud]
> Beneath an azure sky, vast oceans separate ancient empires and rising powers. From the frozen spires of the north to the jade rivers of the east and the crystal towers of the west, the world of Rubicon balances on the threshold between industrial revolution and ancient arcane war.

## Continents & Major Geographies
1. **[[tamriel]] (North-West):** Ancient human and Nordic realm; home to the [[council-of-cyrodil]], the ancient [[imperial-city]], the cliffside harbor of [[daggerfall]], the holy city of [[holy-city-of-vivec]], and the warrior holds of [[skyrim]].
2. **[[northrend]] (Far North):** An inhospitable, glaciated arctic continent dominated by permafrost, wyrms, and the dark spires of Barad-dûr.
3. **[[summerset-isle]] (West / South-West):** An ancient elven archipelago centered around the pristine prismatic [[crystal-tower]], the capital [[alinor]], and the naval super-fortress of [[sky-watch]].
4. **[[aethelgard]] (Central Continent):** The crossroads continent, divided by a mountain range into the expansionist empire of [[noxus]] in the east and the technological powerhouse of [[piltover]] in the west, locked in standoff at the [[rivendel]] pass.
5. **[[cathay]] (South & East):** A sweeping crescent continent cupping the central sea. Divided by the monumental wall of [[hells-gate]] into the volcanic southern ash wastes and the sprawling, prosperous [[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay]].
6. **[[the-ring-city]] (The Central Isle):** The independent, triple-walled commercial metropolis governing international trade and merchant guilds, situated at the nexus of all continental shipping lines.
7. **[[the-serpentine-abyss]] & [[the-world-tree]] (North-East Waters):** The treacherous, leviathan-filled sea and the millions-of-years-old primordial World Tree, held under strict High Elven naval embargo.
