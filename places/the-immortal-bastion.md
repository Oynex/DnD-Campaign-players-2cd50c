## Overview
The colossal, obsidian-walled capital citadel of [[noxus]], looming menacingly over the jagged plateau of eastern [[aethelgard]]. 

Far older than the empire itself, the Immortal Bastion was not built by Noxus. It was erected millennia ago by the legendary warlord **Sahn-Uzal** (remembered in dark folklore as **Mordekaiser**, the Iron Revenant) during the dawn era when eastern Aethelgard was a savage wilderness of squabbling, nomadic war tribes.

> [!read-aloud]
> An oppressive obsidian colossus towering hundreds of spans above the jagged city streets. Its gargantuan bastions, cyclopean ramparts, and jagged iron spikes loom like a predator crouched over the surrounding empire. The very stone seems to drink in ambient light, echoing with the rhythmic thunder of marching legionaries and the ancient, unsettling whisper of millions of souls bound into its foundations.

## The Age of Sahn-Uzal
Before Noxus was forged, the eastern lands were divided among dozens of brutal, warring barbarian clans:
- **The Iron Conqueror:** Sahn-Uzal conquered, slaughtered, and subjugated every tribe under an unyielding iron fist, uniting the scattered peoples for the first time in history through absolute bloodshed.
- **Raising the Bastion:** Declaring himself the eternal ruler of the living and the dead, he commanded the construction of the Immortal Bastion. According to legend, dark sorcery and the very souls of the conquered were forged into its mortar, making the fortress virtually indestructible.

## The Pale Lady's Treachery & The Anti-Monarchy Vow
Sahn-Uzal's horrific reign of terror seemed eternal until his own inner circle turned against him:
- **The Backstab of LeBlanc:** The enigmatic sorceress **[[leblanc]]** orchestrated a masterwork conspiracy from within his court. At the height of his power, she stabbed the tyrant in the back, unraveling the sorcerous anchors that bound his soul to his dread iron shell and casting him into the abyss of death.
- **Never Again a King:** Standing before the towering, empty iron citadel, the liberated tribes celebrated their deliverance. United by the shared trauma of tyranny, the tribal elders swore an eternal, sacred blood oath: **The people would never again bow to a king, a crown, or a single absolute master.**
- **The Birth of Noxus:** The tribes established their new empire around the empty stronghold, christening themselves **Noxus** and making the Immortal Bastion the eternal seat of collective strength.

## Present Day
Today, the Immortal Bastion houses the assembly halls of the **[[noxian-trifarix]]**, imperial war arsenals, the gilded high guest quarters where valuable diplomatic captives like **[[prince-liam-of-stormwind]]** are held, and deep subterranean catacombs whose ancient, forgotten vaults are sealed under high state secrecy.
