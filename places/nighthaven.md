## Overview
A grand, forbidding gothic stone city and colossal fortress crowning a jagged basalt outcrop at the center of [[duskwood]] on eastern [[aethelgard]]. 

For centuries, Nighthaven was the sovereign capital of the **Kingdom of Nighthaven**, an ancient feudal realm ruled by the dark sorcerers of the **Malakor Dynasty**. Today, it has been liberated and annexed by [[noxus]], serving as the vital provincial capital and northern military command bastion of the empire.

> [!read-aloud]
> Soaring basalt spires and vaulted archways rise through the perpetual twilight of Duskwood, overlooking a sprawling walled city of dark stone and slate roofs. High atop the royal battlements, the spiked crimson standards of Noxus ripple in the cold wind. Streets that once sat empty in terror now bustle with vibrant market stalls, where imperial legionaries and local woodsmen trade peacefully under the warm glow of municipal lantern-posts.

## The Fallen Kingdom of Nighthaven
Before its fall, the Kingdom of Nighthaven held absolute dominion over all of Duskwood:
- **The Royal Court of Sorcerers:** Under **[[king-victor-malakor]]**, the royal family practiced horrific dark arts behind aristocratic refinement. Corrupt vassal lords—such as Duke Cassian Vane of Blackmire and Count Lucian Graves of the Bleakwoods—enforced the crown's will, rounding up serfs and political dissidents for blood sacrifices to the hidden demon beneath the keep.
- **The Good Vassals:** Honorable regional lords like **[[baroness-evelyn-crowe]]** secretly resisted where they could, ultimately providing the intelligence and tunnel maps that allowed Noxus to breach the city’s outer gates.

## The Duel of Kings & Sorcery
When Grand General **[[swain|Jericho Swain]]** led the imperial assault, the siege culminated in an epic sorcerous duel between Swain and King Victor Malakor within the high throne room:
- Both wielded catastrophic high magic, rattling the foundations of the castle.
- King Victor's dying curse severed and disintegrated Swain's left arm, but Swain struck the fatal blow, executing the king and wiping out the cultist aristocracy.
- Having unraveled the True Name of the demon **Raum**, Swain subjugated the fiend into his severed limb, transforming his injury into his all-seeing demonic hand.

## Civic Governance under Lady Eleanor
To restore peace to the traumatized populace, Swain appointed his bride, **[[lady-eleanor-malakor]]** (the sole surviving, kind-hearted daughter of the royal line), as the **Caretaker and Civil Governor of Nighthaven City**:
- While the citadel houses the Noxian military garrison, Eleanor oversees the civilian city—rebuilding housing, establishing public dispensaries, and winning the deep love of her people.
- The common folk view Noxus not as ruthless conquerors, but as **liberators** who freed them from centuries of royal nightmare.
