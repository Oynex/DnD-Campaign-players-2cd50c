## Overview
A forbidding gothic castle fortress rising from a rocky prominence within the heart of [[duskwood]]. Formerly the palace-seat of a tyrannical dark mage ruler, Nighthaven was besieged and captured by [[noxus]], who executed the sorcerer and converted the castle into a forward imperial command redoubt.

> [!read-aloud]
> Jagged spires of dark basalt rise from the gloomy tree line of Duskwood, their narrow arched windows lit by cold blue witchfire. Black iron chains and spiked drawbridges guard the gatehouse, where Noxian garrisons maintain a permanent watch over the eerie woodland roads.
