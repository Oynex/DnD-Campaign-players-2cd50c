## Overview
A fortified stone river fortress situated at the northern border of [[duskwood]], marking the **absolute northernmost stronghold of [[noxus]]** on [[aethelgard]]. 

> [!read-aloud]
> Heavy granite battlements and iron catapult batteries line the southern shore of the massive Lake of Nurnen. Yet just a few hundred yards out over the water, a solid wall of impenetrable, glowing pearlescent fog completely obscures the northern horizon.

## The Impassable Barrier
Noxus’s blitzing northern expansion has been brought to a complete standstill at Mist's Edge:
- The empire cannot cross the **Lake of Nurnen** to strike the lake-city of [[esgaroth]] or reach the northern mountains.
- The lake is perpetually blanketed by a dense, magical mist that disorients compasses, chokes navigation, and forces any ship entering it to endlessly circle back to where it began.
- Noxian warmasters know the mist drifts down from the mountain valley of **[[imladris]]**, but imperial scouts and arcanists have found no way to pierce, disperse, or explain the living ward.
