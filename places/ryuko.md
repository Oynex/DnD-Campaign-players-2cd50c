## Overview
A legendary, sun-drenched coastal port metropolis situated on the western fringe of the **Rune Desert** on [[cathay]], where rolling golden dunes meet crystal-blue ocean waters. Governed by the Emperor's eldest son, **[[dragon-prince-xian]]**, Ryuko is a breathtaking realm of desert splendor, opulent sandstone palaces, and labyrinthine silk markets.

Renowned as Rubicon's premier trading crossroads of the exotic, Ryuko is where oddities, relics, and treasures from every corner of the world inevitably wash ashore.

> [!read-aloud]
> Soaring turquoise and lapis lazuli minarets rise above a labyrinth of sun-bleached sandstone palaces and billowing silk pavilions. The air is intoxicating, heavy with the scents of cinnamon, burning frankincense, roasted desert spices, and salty sea spray. Merchant galleons with crimson sails dock beside fleets of enchanted dune-skiffs, while bustling open-air bazaars showcase levitating carpets, caged chimera cubs, bottled djinn wisps, and glittering glass curiosities under a blazing azure sky.

## The Grand Bazaar of Oddities
The markets of Ryuko are unmatched in variety and luxury:
- **Treasures of the World:** Merchants from [[the-ring-city]], alchemists from Piltover, and wanderers from across Rubicon barter side-by-side. If a relic is lost, forgotten, or strictly forbidden in other kingdoms, it will eventually surface in the shaded alleyways of Ryuko's deep bazaars.
- **Desert Dune-Skiffs:** Armadas of specialized skiffs equipped with enchanted sand-keels glide across the sweeping dunes of the interior, ferrying spice caravans, glass ingots, and desert gems between isolated oasis citadels.
- **Mercenary Hub:** Prince Xian maintains an iron grip on the city through a vast network of desert nomad cavalry, foreign sellsword companies, and privateer fleets, funneling astronomical customs tariffs into his bid for the imperial throne.
