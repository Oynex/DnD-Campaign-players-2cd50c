## Overview
A legendary, sun-drenched coastal port metropolis situated on the western fringe of **[[the-rune-desert]]** on [[cathay]], where rolling golden dunes meet crystal-blue ocean waters. Governed by the Emperor's eldest son, **[[dragon-prince-xian]]**, Ryuko holds administrative and military dominion over the entire desert expanse.

Renowned as Rubicon's premier trading crossroads of the exotic, Ryuko is where oddities, relics, and treasures from every corner of the world inevitably wash ashore. It is also the operational staging ground for licensed archaeological expeditions into the sand-buried ruins of the ancient runic civilization.

> [!read-aloud]
> Soaring turquoise and lapis lazuli minarets rise above a labyrinth of sun-bleached sandstone palaces and billowing silk pavilions. The air is intoxicating, heavy with the scents of cinnamon, burning frankincense, roasted desert spices, and salty sea spray. Merchant galleons with crimson sails dock beside fleets of enchanted dune-skiffs, while bustling open-air bazaars showcase levitating carpets, caged chimera cubs, bottled djinn wisps, and glittering glass curiosities under a blazing azure sky.

## Domain Over the Rune Desert & Runic Trade
- **Dominion Over the Sands:** Ryuko exercises sole imperial jurisdiction over **[[the-rune-desert]]**, patrolling caravan tracks with enchanted dune-skiffs and maintaining desert outposts.
- **Runic Sponsorship:** While the central court strictly bans civilian runic experimentation, Prince Xian actively sponsors certified masters from the **[[guild-of-imperial-runesmiths]]**, outfitting his elite desert nomads with authorized **[[runic-magic-crafting|runic weaponry]]** and siphoning ancient relics unearthed from pre-cataclysm desert ruins.
- **The Black Bazaar:** In the shaded deeper alleys of the port, illegal pre-ban runestones, uncertified glyph daggers, and dormant crystal matrices smuggled from the desert regularly exchange hands for astronomical sums.
