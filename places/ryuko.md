## Overview
A vibrant, sun-drenched maritime trading port situated on the western edge of the **Rune Desert** on [[cathay]]. Built from sandstone and blue ceramic tile, Ryuko is governed by the Emperor's eldest son, **[[dragon-prince-xian]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Sun-bleached sandstone docks bustle with desert caravan traders, foreign sea merchants, and dragon-crested galleons. Towering minarets and domed markets overlook azure waters, while sand-skiffs glide across the sweeping dunes stretching out into the desert interior.

## Governance & Economic Might
Ryuko acts as the primary western commercial gateway of Grand Cathay, receiving maritime merchants from [[the-ring-city]]. Prince Xian leverages the vast customs tariffs, merchant alliances, and desert mercenary guilds under his command to fund his claim to the imperial throne.
