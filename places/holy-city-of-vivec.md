## Overview
A monumental, waterborne religious stronghold located at the southernmost tip of [[cyrodil]] on [[tamriel]]. Vivec is the spiritual heartbeat of the continent, serving as the supreme pilgrimage site and high sanctuary for the dominant religion of Tamriel.

> [!read-aloud]
> Massive stepped ziggurats and gilded temple cantons rise majestically from the tranquil azure waters of the southern bay. Grand canal gondolas glide beneath arched stone sky-bridges hung with holy incense censers, while hundreds of thousands of pilgrims climb the sacred marble steps in quiet prayer.

## Spiritual Center
- **The High Cantons:** Multi-tiered stone sanctuaries housing religious monastic orders, sacred reliquaries, and the supreme ecclesiastical courts.
- **Ecclesiastical Authority:** Closely tied to the spiritual leadership of House Indoril and [[archbishop-aldwin-indoril]], making Vivec an autonomous bastion of faith within the empire.
