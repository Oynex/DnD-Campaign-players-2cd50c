## Overview
A monumental, waterborne religious metropolis rising majestically from the azure waters of the southern bay of [[cyrodil]] on [[tamriel]]. Regarded as the **holiest city in the western hemisphere**, Vivec is the spiritual capital and administrative seat of **[[orthodox-malorism]]**, drawing hundreds of thousands of pilgrims annually from every province of the empire.

Constructed as an archipelago of colossal stepped stone ziggurats known as **Cantons**, Vivec is an architectural wonder where grand holy processions take place aboard gilded canal gondolas beneath towering marble sky-bridges hung with brass censers.

> [!read-aloud]
> Soaring stepped ziggurats of brilliant white granite and gilded bronze rise like divine mountains directly from the tranquil waters of the bay. Grand canal gondolas draped in cloth-of-gold glide beneath multi-tiered sky-bridges, where incense smoke hangs thick in the sea air. The harmonic ringing of a hundred temple bells echoes across the water, mingling with the synchronized hymns of thousands of white-robed pilgrims ascending sacred marble steps.

## The Miracle of the Barren Shore & The Sacred Trials
Vivec was not built upon ancient ruins or fertile plains; it was born of a legendary miracle concluding a continental odyssey:
- **From North to South:** Born in the deep, icy crags of northern [[skyrim]], **Prophet Malor** journeyed the entire breadth of Tamriel, preaching along frozen fjords, scorching ashlands, and plague-ridden trenches before finally reaching the southern sea.
- **The Forty-Day Vigil:** Thirty-five centuries ago, the southern bay was a desolate, salt-crusted marsh. Malor walked into the stagnant sea and fasted in prayer for forty days until a miraculous subterranean geyser of pure, sweet freshwater erupted through the sea floor.
- **The City on the Waves:** Proclaiming the spot sacred, the Prophet’s disciples drove cedar and stone pilings into the bay, gradually quarrying granite to raise the colossal High Cantons over centuries.
- **The Vault of Relics:** Beneath the High Temple lie the holy artifacts of Malor’s trials: his weathered cedar travel staff, the iron cell door of King Vaelen that unlocked on its own, and the parchment fragments of the *Book of Trials*.

## The Five High Cantons & Ecclesiastical Power
Each massive ziggurat canton functions as a semi-autonomous district governed by holy law:
- **The High Temple Canton:** Seat of supreme liturgical authority, housing the sacred relics of Prophet Malor and the gilded council chambers of the Five Warrior-Bishops of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**.
- **The Canton of the Prophet:** Massive residential ziggurat housing monastic orders, copying scribes, and the grand library of southern theology.
- **The Pilgrim Cantons:** Thronged with hostels, sacred bathhouses, relic bourses, and open-air chapels catering to visiting pilgrims.
- **The Martial Cantons:** Fortress bastions housing the grand headquarters, armories, and training academies of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**, who command the standing holy army across Tamriel.

## The Gilded Veil: Piety and Corruption
Beneath the scent of incense and glorious choral hymns lies a complex, morally gray institution:
- **The Holy Tithe Machine:** Every merchant, shipmaster, and farmer under imperial law pays a compulsory tithe to the church. While vast sums fund magnificent hospices, public relief clinics, and food distribution, corrupt prelates quietly divert immense wealth into private luxury.
- **The Holy Army's Inquisitions:** Commanded by the Five Warrior-Bishops, **[[the-knights-of-malor]]** maintain active crusader regiments that march across Tamriel to hunt witches, burn swamp hags in the Blackwood fens, and cleanse ancient curses, ensuring that commoners view the church as an indispensable shield against supernatural evil.
- **The Schismatic Shadow:** While southern pilgrims kneel in tears before the altars, northern raiders from **[[skyrim]]** view Vivec with profound disgust, adhering to the **[[new-malors]]** and viewing the city's gold-plated ziggurats as monuments to southern greed and apostasy.
