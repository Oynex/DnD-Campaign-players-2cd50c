## Overview
The capital of [[cyrodil]] and the seat of the [[council-of-cyrodil]]. A sprawling, ancient metropolis founded two thousand years ago at the geographic heart of the realm. For fifteen hundred years, the city served as the throne-seat of the Emperors; five hundred years ago, following the Great Revolt that ousted the monarchy, the central palace was converted into the **Council Tower**, where the Six Great Houses now govern.

> [!read-aloud]
> A circular marvel of white stone, tiered palaces, and soaring marble arches rising from the central heartland. At its core, the Council Tower pierces the clouds, casting a monumental shadow over the plazas where senators, guildmasters, and diplomats convene—a city bearing the weight of two thousand years of unbroken civilization.

## The Six Quarters
Reflecting the governance of the Six Houses, the city is arranged in grand districts:
- **The Corvan Quarter:** Hub of engineering guilds, foundries, and public works.
- **The Astellion Archive District:** Colossal libraries, magical scriptoriums, and intelligence archives.
- **The Aurelius Citadel:** High garrison barracks, parade grounds, and legionary armories.
- **The Sterling Exchange:** Imperial mint, trading bourses, and international banking houses.
- **The Temple District:** Grand cathedrals and gardens administered by [[archbishop-aldwin-indoril]].
- **The Magisterial Precinct:** High courts, legal halls, and watch garrisons overseen by [[lord-sylus-bellator]].
