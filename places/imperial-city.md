## Overview
The ancient capital of [[cyrodil]] and the seat of the [[council-of-cyrodil]]. A sprawling metropolis built around the monolithic Council Tower, where the thirteen noble houses have governed for over five hundred years since the fall of the empire.

> [!read-aloud]
> A circular marvel of white stone, tiered palaces, and soaring marble arches rising from the central heartland. At its core, the Council Tower pierces the clouds, casting a monumental shadow over the plazas where senators, guildmasters, and diplomats convene.

## Political Center
The city is divided into aristocratic quarters governed by each of the thirteen houses:
- The treasury reserves of House Hlaalu.
- The engineering colleges of House Redoran.
- The Grand Cathedral overseen by Archbishop [[archbishop-aldwin-indoril]].
- The vast military parade grounds maintained by House Septim.
