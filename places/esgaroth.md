## Overview
A picturesque, waterborne trading town constructed entirely on massive timber pilings over the tranquil waters of the **Lake of Nurnen**, nestled in the shadow of the northern mountain range of [[aethelgard]]. 

> [!read-aloud]
> Wooden walkways, multi-tiered shingled houses, and bustling market docks sit elevated over crystal-clear lake waters. Fishing canoes and merchant skiffs dart between timber pilings, safely sheltered behind a veil of glowing protective mist that keeps the war fleets of Noxus at bay.

## Origins & The Dwarven Tunnel
Esgaroth was originally founded at the lakeside terminus of the colossal tunnel dug by the ancient dwarves of **[[erebor]]**. When **[[the-archmage-dragon]]** placed the magical forest of **[[imladris]]** across Erebor's front gate, the stubborn dwarves excavated their way out to the lake, establishing the trade quays that grew into modern Esgaroth.

## Sanctuary on the Lake
Protected from Noxian conquest by the mysterious, supernatural fog rolling down from the heights of [[imladris]], Esgaroth serves as the vital trade gateway to the northern peaks and the unyielding barrier against the legions stationed at **[[mists-edge]]**.
