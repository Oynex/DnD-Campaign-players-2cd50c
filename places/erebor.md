## Overview
The crown jewel of dwarven civilization and the **most ancient mega-stronghold of the dwarven race**, carved into the highest northern peak of the **Mountain Range of Nurnen** at the absolute crest of [[aethelgard]]. 

One thousand years ago, the dwarves were forced to abandon the monumental halls during an ancient, catastrophic calamity. Today, the giant gates stand silent in the high mountain snows.

> [!read-aloud]
> High amidst the jagged, snow-swept crags of Mount Nurnen, colossal stone statues of ancient dwarf kings sit carved into the mountain flank, flanking adamantine gates sealed by frost and time. Deep within the mountain's frozen dark lie miles of vaulted highways, colossal treasure vaults, and silent forges awaiting the hammer of their rightful heirs.

## The Twin Entrances of Erebor
Due to an ancient encounter with **[[the-archmage-dragon]]**, Erebor possesses a unique architectural history:
1. **The Great Front Gates:** The original ancestral gate facing the southern foothills. Several thousand years ago, the dragon sprouted the surreal, purple crystalline forest of **[[imladris]]** directly across this threshold as an elaborate practical joke against the magic-hating dwarves.
2. **The Back Gate to Esgaroth:** Rather than submit to walking through a whimsical magical wonderland, the infuriated dwarves quarried a colossal subterranean highway through the eastern flank of the mountain, emerging on the shores of Lake Nurnen where **[[esgaroth]]** now stands.

## The Dwarven Secret
Outside dwarven circles, the other races believe the dragon of Imladris died long ago. Only the dwarven clans remember the deeper truth: that their gruff ancestors and the eccentric dragon eventually became close friends after centuries of stubborn banter.

## The Pan-Dwarven Dream
Every dwarf across [[rubicon]]—from the miners of [[khazad-dum]] to the blacksmiths of [[gundabad]]—grows up on the songs of Erebor. Reclaiming the ancestral throne of the Lonely Mountain is the supreme, unifying ambition of all dwarven clans. Even though abandoned for ten centuries, the mountain remains unviolated by [[noxus]], protected from the south by the impenetrable magical mists shrouding Lake Nurnen.
