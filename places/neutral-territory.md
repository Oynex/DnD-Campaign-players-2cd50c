## Overview
The vast oceanic expanses, contested border frontiers, and unaligned island chains that lie between the borders of [[cyrodil]], [[noxus]], and [[summerset-isle]]. 

## Major Sites
- [[the-ring-city]]: The independent, sovereign commercial capital of the world.
- [[vespers-rest]]: A sunken island settlement swallowed by the sea fifteen years ago.
