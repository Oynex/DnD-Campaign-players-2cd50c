## Overview
A colossal, treacherous ocean expanse dominating the northeastern waters of [[rubicon]], separating the civilized continents from the mythical sanctuary of **[[the-world-tree]]**. Shrouded in eternal banks of supernatural fog, the Serpentine Abyss is notorious for violent ocean squalls, abyssal whirlpools, and ancient aquatic leviathans.

It has long been deemed impassable by mortal navigators, and has devolved into half-forgotten maritime folklore.

> [!read-aloud]
> Titanic banks of cold, iridescent sea mist loom like fortress walls over ink-black ocean swells. Beneath the churning surface, colossal serpentine shadows measuring hundreds of fathoms glide silently through the deep, their glowing emerald frills and fanged jaws breaking the waves in eerie, hypnotic cadence.

## The Skywatch Embargo & Perimeter Patrols
Due to the sheer lethality of the abyss and the dangerous cosmic nature of what lies beyond, the High Elves of **[[sky-watch]]** have placed a **worldwide naval embargo** on venturing into the Serpentine Abyss:
- War galleons under **[[fleet-admiral-aislyn]]** actively patrol the perimeter boundaries of the fog banks.
- Foreign explorers, merchant privateers, and thrill-seeking navigators caught sailing toward the abyss are forcefully turned back or impounded.
- Asur patrol ships routinely conduct search-and-rescue operations along the perimeter, dragging back waterlogged wreckage and half-drowned survivors who ventured too close to the leviathan hunting grounds.

## The Riddle of the Ruins
Despite the strict blockade, archaeological excavations in ancient ruins across [[tamriel]], [[aethelgard]], and [[cathay]] periodically turn up ancient glyphs, logs, and artifacts referencing successful historical expeditions to the World Tree. This confirms that across the millennia, a legendary few have managed to navigate the abyss and return alive.
