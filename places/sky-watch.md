## Overview
A colossal maritime super-fortress anchoring the eastern cliffs of [[summerset-isle]]. Skywatch is the naval hammer of the **Asur (High Elves)**—an impregnable coastal citadel from which the High Elven navy dominates the oceanic trade corridors and sea lanes of the known world.

Within Skywatch, the singular faith of **[[kynarism]]** burns brightly: here, officers, captains, and marines view the eternal defense of mortal civilization against the southern ash horrors as their sacred covenant with their ancestors—preserving the world so that elven civilization may endure.

> [!read-aloud]
> Tiered granite-and-crystal seawalls plunge vertically into the surging ocean surf, ringed with heavy dragon-headed coastal ballistas. Within the sheltered deepwater lagoon, dozens of majestic elven dragon-ships and soaring white galleons ride at anchor under the steely gaze of the High Citadel.

## Naval Hegemony & The Dual Mandate
Commanded directly by **[[fleet-admiral-aislyn]]**, the highest military commander of the Asur. Skywatch oversees two supreme maritime missions:
1. **The Southern Containment Line:** In military alliance with **[[the-ring-city]]**, maintaining a permanent naval cordon against the horrors emerging from the southern volcanic ash wastes.
2. **The Serpentine Abyss Embargo:** Enforcing a strict, worldwide maritime quarantine around **[[the-serpentine-abyss]]**, deploying dragon-ship squadrons to intercept unauthorized explorers and rescue voyagers who drift too close to the leviathan hunting grounds.
