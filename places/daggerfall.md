## Overview
A prosperous port city on the eastern coast of [[cyrodil]], built into towering ocean cliffs. Known as a magical marvel due to its innovative architecture, mechanical funiculars, and floating crystalline structures. Recently, however, severe magical disturbances and seismic tremors have shaken the city.

> [!read-aloud]
> White stone battlements and towering crystalline spires crest the jagged seaside cliffs. Floating blue crystals hum in harmonic resonance along the skyline, though several now flicker with alarming violet static. Down in the deep harbor, ships rock violently against unnatural harbor swells.

## Key Districts & Locations

### The Main City & Harbor
Carved directly into the cliffside, connected by water-powered elevators and spiraling stairways. The harbor accommodates merchant galleons from [[the-ring-city]] and military cruisers under [[commodore-marcellus-anvil]].

### [[academy-of-daggerfall|The Academy of Daggerfall]]
The human pinnacle of magical research and magitech engineering. Dominated by the **Resonance Tower**, an impossibly high crystalline needle. Recent exterior fractures along the tower suggest a catastrophic magical breach occurred within its laboratories.

### Harbor Keep
A heavily fortified citadel carved into the lowest granite shelf of Daggerfall's harbor. It currently holds recovered arcane artifacts under guard—mysterious crystalline relics showing catastrophic signs of planar collision.

## Current Tension
The elite mages of the [[order-of-the-ethereal-sigil]] under [[archmage-aldwyn]] maintain a strict lockdown around the Academy, while undercover Cyrodilian general [[general-kael-stormbringer]] monitors the escalating crisis.
