## Overview
A colossal red-brick and iron-reinforced Great Wall and fortified bastion spanning the narrow continental neck of [[cathay]]. Built **1,500 years ago** during the Cataclysm by **[[the-thunder-dragon]]**, the sister of the Celestial Emperor, Hells Gate completely seals off the peaceful empire from the monster-infested volcanic ash wastes to the south.

> [!read-aloud]
> A cyclopean barrier of red granite and bronze ramparts stretches as far as the eye can see, towering hundreds of feet above the charred ash plains. At the center gatehouse, crackling blue lightning arcs continuously along dragon-carved storm rods, where the Thunder Dragon keeps perpetual watch over the nightmare wastes.

## The Northern Shield
Unlike the political courts of Nan-Gau, Hells Gate is a place of unending martial vigil. Under the personal command of the Thunder Dragon, elite Cathayan storm-legions and dragon-blooded sentries repulse constant probes from mutated abominations and ash-wastes horrors.
