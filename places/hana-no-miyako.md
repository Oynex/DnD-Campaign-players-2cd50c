## Overview
A breathtaking northern province and mountain metropolis within the **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay]]**, celebrated as the City of Flowers. Built upon cascading mountain ridges shrouded in permanent pink and crimson cherry blossom canopies, it is governed by the Emperor's eldest daughter, **[[dragon-princess-zhao]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Fragrant pink petals drift across tiered curved tile roofs and tranquil koi ponds. Ancient torii gates and hillside shrines wind through blooming cherry-wood groves, where courtly scholars and spirit mages practice amidst the timeless beauty of the blossoms.

## Political Center
Hana no Miyako is both a cultural capital and the political bastion of Princess Zhao. From its mountain heights, she commands elite dragon-guard battalions, consolidating imperial court support in her bid to succeed her ailing father on the Celestial Throne.
