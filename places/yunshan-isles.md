## Overview
A mystical archipelago of towering, mist-shrouded karst peaks and **floating mountain islands** rising along the eastern coast of [[cathay]], bordering the Dread Sea. Governed by the Emperor's second son, **[[dragon-prince-longwei]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Colossal moss-covered stone pillars and floating mountain crags hover weightlessly amidst sea mist, linked by swaying rope bridges and silk aerial funiculars. Serpentine dragons and celestial cranes wheel effortlessly through the clouds around mountain-top hermitages and aerial temples.

## Airborne Stronghold
The Yunshan Isles serve as the primary aerial bastion of the empire. Prince Longwei commands the celestial sky-fleets and wind-dancers, using his remote, impenetrable aerie to build an unassailable military power base.
