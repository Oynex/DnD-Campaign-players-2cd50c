## Overview
A breathtaking, colossal region of soaring mist-shrouded karst peaks and **gigantic floating mountain islands** drifting weightlessly across the eastern skies of [[cathay]]. Governed from atop the central floating monolith by the Emperor's second son, **[[dragon-prince-longwei]]**.

Beyond their natural wonder, the Yunshan Isles serve as the primary aerial bastion of Grand Cathay and the home base of the legendary **Cathayan Air Force**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Colossal moss-draped mountain peaks float suspended amidst swirling sea mists, defying gravity like islands adrift in an ocean of sky. Swaying suspension bridges of braided silk and iron chain link soaring cliffside pagodas. Suddenly, the sky erupts with color—flocks of agile, iridescent wyverns wheel through the clouds, escorting massive armored war balloons adorned with dragon crests, while spear-wielding dragoon knights soar effortlessly alongside them on shimmering wings of flight-magic.

## Stronghold of the Cathayan Air Force
Prince Longwei commands Rubicon's most formidable aerial military host:
- **Armored War Balloons:** Colossal hot air airships fitted with fire-lances, alchemical flame-pots, and dragon-head bombards that rain devastation upon ground armies.
- **Wyvern Vanguard:** A disciplined legion of small, swift flying wyverns trained for aerial dogfighting, strafing runs, and intercepting enemy skyships.
- **The Dragoon Knights:** An elite knightly order of aerial warriors who channel wind and flight magic. Armed with heavy runic polearms, they leap fearlessly from war balloon gondolas, gliding through the clouds to impale aerial beasts or board enemy vessels in mid-air.

## The Celestial Royal Observatory
At the absolute apex of the highest floating mountain island—soaring thousands of feet above the cloud layer where the air is thin and freezing—sits the **Celestial Royal Observatory**:
- **Divine Sky-Sanctuary:** An ancient, jade-and-adamantine astronomical observatory housing celestial lenses and star-charts dating back to the primordial Loong dragons.
- **Royal Bloodline Law:** Access to the observatory is strictly and inviolably **restricted exclusively to the royal dragon family**. Unauthorized mortals who set foot on its steps are instantly executed, as it is here that the dragons gaze directly into celestial alignments and cosmic planar shifts.
