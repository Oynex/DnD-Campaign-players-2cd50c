## Overview
A once-peaceful island fishing community that sank beneath the waves fifteen years ago under violent, unexplained circumstances. Today it lies submerged beneath coastal reefs, surrounded by an unnatural perpetual magical storm.

> [!read-aloud]
> Howling winds whip lightning-shot waves around jagged reefs that break the surface. Below the churning gray waters, the ghost-like outlines of stone cottages, drowned towers, and snapped harbor pilings are split down the middle by a glowing abyssal trench.

## Mystery of the Ruin
- **Dual Magical Damage:** Salvage explorers and scouts note that the sunken ruins are cleanly divided into two halves showing distinctly different schools of catastrophic magical destruction—one side warped by arcane detonation, the other torn by impossible physical force.
- **Escalating Maelstrom:** The magical tempest that perpetually enshrouds the island has grown exponentially more violent over the past twelve months.
