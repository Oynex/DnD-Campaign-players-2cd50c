## Overview
The massive crescent continent originating south of the central continent and curving gracefully eastward to cup the central sea. Revered as the home of **The First Lands**, Cathay contains the most ancient recorded history on [[rubicon]].

The continent is cleanly bifurcated by the monumental Great Wall of **[[hells-gate]]**:
1. **The Ash Wastes & Volcanic South:** A scorched, monster-infested nightmare realm of basalt crags, volcanic fissures, and dragon roosts, guarded at the western land bridge by **Dragon's Maw**.
2. **The Eastern Empire:** The lush, prosperous realm on the northern and eastern side of the wall, entirely governed by the **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> To the southwest lies a jagged crescent of volcanic ash and smoking craters, ending abruptly at a colossal wall of red brick and bronze gates. Beyond that monumental boundary lies an entirely different world—a realm of terraced jade hills, blossoming cherry-wood mountains, and sparkling river highways leading toward the Dread Sea.
