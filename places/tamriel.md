## Overview
The primary continent of the world of [[rubicon]], home to diverse civilizations, ancient magical sites, and warring political factions.

## Continental Powers
- **[[cyrodil]]:** Central human nation governed by the [[council-of-cyrodil]].
- **[[summerset-isle]]:** Ancient elven archipelago to the southwest, guided by the [[sapiarchs-of-the-crystal-tower]].
- **[[noxus]]:** Aggressive expansionist empire in the north and east centered on [[the-immortal-bastion]].
- **[[the-ring-city]]:** Independent island city-state regulating global commerce.
