## Overview
The great northwestern continent of [[rubicon]], characterized by a stark cultural and geopolitical divide between the harsh northern highlands and the sophisticated imperial south.

> [!read-aloud]
> From the glaciated peaks of Skyrim where dragon carved longships sail the Sea of Ghosts, down to the sunlit wheat fields of Cyrodiil and the grand ziggurat cantons of Vivec, Tamriel is a continent of ancient feuds, profound magic, and monumental architecture.

## Geopolitical Landscape

### The North-South Divide
- **The North:** The **[[skyrim|Northern Jarldom of Skyrim]]**, home to fiercely independent warrior clans, maritime reavers, and the legendary Throat of the World.
- **The South:** The ancient **[[cyrodil|Cyrodil Empire]]**, an aristocratic republic governed by the [[council-of-cyrodil]] from the 2,000-year-old **[[imperial-city]]**.

### Western Arcane Bastion
- **[[daggerfall]]:** The fortified cliffside academy city of High Rock, representing the pinnacle of human arcane mastery and linked via ocean leyline to the **[[crystal-tower]]**.

### Southern Holy Land
- **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]:** The waterborne ziggurat metropolis serving as the supreme religious sanctuary of Tamriel.

### The Eastern Dwarven Sub-Continent
Directly east of mainland Cyrodiil lies a rugged, mountainous island sub-continent connected to the mainland via the colossal **[[ductus-bridge]]**. This rocky realm is the domain of ancient dwarven clans and houses the twin mountain strongholds of **[[khazad-dum]]** and **[[gundabad]]**.
