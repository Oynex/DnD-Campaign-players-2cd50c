## Overview
The magnificent capital city of [[summerset-isle]] and the supreme cultural, spiritual, and political seat of all **Asur (High Elves)** across [[rubicon]]. Built from gleaming alabaster stone and mother-of-pearl glass, Alinor is an architectural marvel that has stood untouched by foreign conquest for thousands of years.

Alinor is also the spiritual core of **[[kynarism]]**, housing the legendary **Great Ledger of Ancestors** in its crystalline archives, where every enduring masterpiece created by High Elven artisans, poets, and mages across four thousand years is permanently recorded.

> [!read-aloud]
> Shimmering spires of polished alabaster rise into the coastal clouds, catching the ocean sunlight like crystallized starlight. Gilded balconies overlook tranquil garden plazas filled with flowering silverwood trees, while the royal banners of the Phoenix King flutter above the High Palace.

## Sovereign Governance
Alinor is the throne-seat of dual elven sovereignty:
- **The Phoenix King:** Crowned ruler and high martial sovereign of the Asur, currently **[[alith-anar|Phoenix King Alith Anar]]**.
- **The Everqueen:** Spiritual matriarch, chosen avatar of elven divinity, and mother of the realm.
Together, they govern High Elven society with serene wisdom, directing the defense of the realm and the ancient traditions of their people.

## The Archival Sanctuary of Kynarism
Within the central plaza stands the Hall of Continuance, where young Asur come upon reaching their second century to present their *Parapet* (their enduring masterwork) before the Elder Council. Inscribing a new achievement into the city's crystal scrolls is considered the highest religious honor in High Elven life.
