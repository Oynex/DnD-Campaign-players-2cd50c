## Overview
The southern twin fortress of [[khazad-dum]], crowning the crags of the rocky island sub-continent east of [[cyrodil]]. Gundabad is the martial anvil of the dwarven clans, housing gargantuan foundries, weapon arsenals, and military bastions.

> [!read-aloud]
> Plumes of volcanic soot and sparks rise from the high chimney flues of Mount Gundabad. Iron-reinforced battlements command the southern straits, while armored dwarven sentries with rune-etched halberds stand ceaseless vigil over the lower gates.
