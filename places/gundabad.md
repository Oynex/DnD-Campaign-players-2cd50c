## Overview
The southern twin fortress of [[khazad-dum]], crowning the jagged volcanic crags of the dwarven island east of [[cyrodil]]. 

Gundabad is the **iron fist and supreme military fortress of the dwarven race**. Unlike the bustling markets of Khazad-dûm, Gundabad is **strictly closed to casual outsiders and travelers**, functioning as a colossal subterranean fortress-arsenal, military academy, and weapon-proving ground.

> [!read-aloud]
> Plumes of volcanic soot, black smoke, and orange embers belch from the high chimney flues of Mount Gundabad, casting an eerie glow over iron-plated battlements. Massive adamantine gates remain heavily barred, flanked by grim dwarven sentries in rune-etched full plate holding runic halberds, their cold glares leaving zero doubt that uninvited guests will be met with immediate iron and fire.

## The Martial Anvil & The Cyrodiil Treaty
- **Volcanic Armories:** Within the mountain's magma chambers lie the greatest forge works in the western world, continuously turning out runic armor, enchanted broadaxes, and heavy siege ballistae.
- **Military Alliance with Cyrodiil:** Gundabad stands on excellent diplomatic terms with the Council of Cyrodiil. Under defense accords overseen by House Aurelius under **[[lord-meridian-aurelius]]** and Supreme Commander **[[general-valorian-thorne]]**, Gundabad exclusively supplies the Cyrodiil armed forces with heavy plate armor, shields, and siege ballistae.
- **The Lifeblood Exchange:** In exchange for this elite martial weaponry, Cyrodiil shipping lines provide the dwarven military city with continuous convoys of grain, livestock, and essential staples to sustain their legions.
