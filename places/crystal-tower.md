## Overview
The legendary crystalline monument at the heart of [[summerset-isle]], representing the **pinnacle of elven magical civilization**. Led by **[[high-mage-teclis]]**, brother of [[alith-anar|Phoenix King Alith Anar]], the tower serves as the supreme archive of elven arcane knowledge and planar research.

> [!read-aloud]
> Crafted from translucent crystalline glass that refracts the morning sun into rainbows of pure arcane energy, the Crystal Tower stands without joint or mortar. It pulses in rhythm with the great ocean leyline reaching northward across the sea toward Daggerfall.

## The Trans-Oceanic Leyline
The Crystal Tower sits upon the southern anchor of a primordial **Magical Leyline** dating back millions of years to the dawn of the cosmos, stretching across the open ocean directly northward to **[[daggerfall]]**. The harmonic feedback between these two monumental academies enables archmages to monitor planetary mana flows, dimensional stability, and planar shifts across the world.

## The Silent Warning
In recent months, High Mage [[high-mage-teclis]] and the elven mages of the tower have detected dangerous, asynchronous oscillations rippling along their leyline anchor. Arcane readings suggest an enormous, unnatural drain is siphoning off primal mana somewhere along the eastern frequency, yet elven isolationism and suspicion have kept them from conferring openly with outside civilizations.
