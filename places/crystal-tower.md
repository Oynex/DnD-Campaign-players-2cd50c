## Overview
The legendary monument at the heart of [[summerset-isle]]. It serves as an archive of all elven knowledge, a bastion of high magic, and the seat of the [[sapiarchs-of-the-crystal-tower]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Crafted from translucent crystalline glass that refracts the morning sun into rainbows of pure arcane energy, the Crystal Tower stands without joint or mortar. It pulses in rhythm with the planar leylines of the world.

## Functions & Arcane Research
- **Planar Anchoring:** Monitored by [[sapiarch-liriel]], keeping the fabric between mortal realms and the outer planes stable.
- **Leyline Regulation:** Directed by [[sapiarch-fenorel]], harmonizing environmental mana.
- **Historical Vaults:** Maintained by [[sapiarch-thalien]], guarding forbidden magical history.
