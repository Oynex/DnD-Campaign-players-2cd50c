## Overview
The treacherous, icebound northern sea separating [[tamriel]], the frozen continent of [[northrend]], and northern [[aethelgard]]. Named for the dense, mist-shrouded icebergs, sunken exploration fleets, and strange spectral lights that flicker across its frigid waters during the long winter nights.

> [!read-aloud]
> Jagged blue-green icebergs drift like silent ghosts through freezing gray waters. Thick coastal fogs cling to the waves, carrying the distant, hollow sound of howling polar winds and cracking sea-ice.

## Maritime Routes
Despite the bitter cold and roaming sea predators, daring merchant navigators brave the southern fringes of the Sea of Ghosts to maintain shipping lanes connecting the northern holds of Skyrim (*Solitude, Windhelm*) to the lake ports of northern Aethelgard (*Esgaroth*).
