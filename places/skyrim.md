## Overview
The rugged, mountainous northern realm of [[tamriel]], comprised of fiercely independent warrior holds. Governed by a council of regional Jarls, Skyrim is locked in an enduring ideological and martial cold war with the [[cyrodil|Cyrodil Empire]] to the south.

While Cyrodiil's nobility dismisses the Northerners as uncultured barbarians, the Jarls view the imperial south as soft, decadent, and corrupt.

> [!read-aloud]
> Snow-capped pine peaks tower over mist-filled fjords. Carved dragon-headed longships line the rocky coves, their shields bearing the heraldry of fierce northern clans who prize honor, steel, and maritime plunder above imperial parchment.

## Coastal Raiding & The Fae Taboo
Nordic longships regularly launch daring amphibious reaver raids across the coasts:
- **Imperial Coasts:** Striking Cyrodiil's western shipping lines and coastal estates.
- **Cross-Sea Raids:** Sailing daringly across the central channels to raid the rich farmlands of *Westfall* on [[aethelgard]].
- **The Fae Taboo of Imladris:** Despite their legendary fearlessness, Skyrim raiders will **never** set foot near the purple crystal valley of *Imladris* on Aethelgard, holding a superstitious terror that its soil is cursed by volatile, soul-stealing fae magic.

## Major Holds
- **Solitude:** The fortified western port hold guarding the northern sea approaches.
- **Windhelm:** Ancient eastern stronghold steeped in traditionalist martial pride.
- **Whiterun:** Central trade hold nestled in the shadow of the **Throat of the World**.
- **Riften:** Shrouded southeastern hold nestled amidst autumn birch forests.
