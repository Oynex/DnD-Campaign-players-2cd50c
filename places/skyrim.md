## Overview
The rugged, mountainous northern realm of [[tamriel]], comprised of fiercely independent warrior holds. Governed by a council of regional Jarls, Skyrim is locked in an enduring ideological, cultural, and martial cold war with the [[cyrodil|Cyrodil Empire]] to the south.

While Cyrodiil's nobility dismisses the Northerners as uncultured barbarians, the Jarls view the imperial south as soft, decadent, and spiritually rotten.

> [!read-aloud]
> Snow-capped pine peaks tower over mist-filled fjords. Carved dragon-headed longships line the rocky coves, their shields bearing the heraldry of fierce northern clans who prize honor, steel, and communal brotherhood above imperial parchment and golden priestly robes.

## The Northern Birthright & The New Malors Reformation
The bitter rivalry between Skyrim and Cyrodiil is rooted not only in politics, but in an ancient battle over spiritual lineage:
- **The Prophet's Northern Blood:** The clans fiercely remember that **Prophet Malor was born in the deep north of Skyrim**, enduring frozen blizzards as a youth before walking southward across Tamriel. To the northerners, Malor is their own son—a rugged barefoot ascetic whose soul belonged to the snow and the clan hearth.
- **The Rejection of Orthodox Vivec:** While Cyrodiil turned Malor into a gilded idol to collect mandatory taxes through **[[orthodox-malorism]]**, the northerners led the **[[new-malors]]** reformation. The clans fiercely reject bishops, cathedrals, and church tithes, outlawing the fortified chapterhouses of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]** from setting foot in Skyrim.
- **Righteous Desecration:** When Nordic longships raid southern coasts, stripping gold leaf and relics from imperial temples is considered an act of holy justice—stripping false idols from corrupt southern priests to honor the barefoot Prophet.

## Coastal Raiding & The Fae Taboo
Nordic longships regularly launch daring amphibious reaver raids across the coasts:
- **Imperial Coasts:** Striking Cyrodiil's western shipping lines and coastal estates.
- **Cross-Sea Raids:** Sailing daringly across the central channels to raid the rich farmlands of *Westfall* on [[aethelgard]].
- **The Fae Taboo of Imladris:** Despite their legendary fearlessness, Skyrim raiders will **never** set foot near the purple crystal valley of *Imladris* on Aethelgard, holding a superstitious terror that its soil is cursed by volatile, soul-stealing fae magic.

## Major Holds
- **Solitude:** The fortified western port hold guarding the northern sea approaches.
- **Windhelm:** Ancient eastern stronghold steeped in traditionalist martial pride.
- **Whiterun:** Central trade hold nestled in the shadow of the **Throat of the World**.
- **Riften:** Shrouded southeastern hold nestled amidst autumn birch forests.
