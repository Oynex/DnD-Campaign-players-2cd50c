## Overview
The massive **Central Continent** of [[rubicon]], serving as the geographic crossroads of the known world. A towering north-south mountain range divides the continent into two starkly polarized geopolitical spheres:
- **The East:** Entirely dominated and subjugated by the expansionist military superpower of [[noxus]].
- **The West:** A patchwork of independent agrarian heartlands (*Goldshire, Westfall*), deep elven valleys (*Imladris, Ever-Song Glade*), and the technologically supreme city-state of [[piltover]].

The entire continent teeters on the brink of total war, centered on the strategic mountain pass of [[rivendel]]—the solitary land choke point connecting east and west.

> [!read-aloud]
> A continent of extreme contrasts. In the west, lush green pastures, golden wheat fields, and gleaming brass airships hum beneath clear skies. To the east, red canyon dust and industrial smoke rise from the iron bastions of Noxian outposts. Between them, jagged gray mountains act as a natural wall, pierced only by the fortified gates of Rivendel.

## Major Regions & Powers
- **[[piltover]]:** The western coastal city-state whose revolutionary magitech is decades ahead of the rest of the world.
- **[[rivendel]]:** The fortified mountain-and-river choke point holding the line against the east, fortified with Piltover's advanced weaponry.
- **[[noxus]]:** The aggressive empire that conquered the eastern territories (*Durotar, Orgrimmar, East Fall, Stormwind*) and seeks to conquer the west.
- **The Heartland Commons:** The bountiful farming villages of *Gold Shire* and *West Fall*.
- **The Sylvan Sanctuaries:** *Ever-Song Glade* in the south and the purple crystalline sanctuary of *Imladris* in the northern foothills.
- **Northern Highland Keeps:** *Erebor*, the lake-town of *Esgaroth*, and the timberlands of *Dusk Wood*.
