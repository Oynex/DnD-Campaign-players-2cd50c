## Overview
The massive, canyon-carved stronghold of the orcish clans in **[[durotar]]**, situated along the red sandstone cliffs of eastern [[aethelgard]]. Carved directly into the sheer stone walls of a natural chasm, Orgrimmar has long served as the heart of orcish blacksmithing, clan gatherings, and martial contests.

Following its brutal conquest by **[[darius|Grand General Darius]]**, Orgrimmar was transformed into the primary staging bastion and shock-troop training ground for the **[[noxus|Noxian Legion]]**. Iron-spiked watchtowers, massive war-gates, and obsidian parade grounds now loom over the red dust, where thousands of armored orc centurions drill under the blood-red banners of the empire.

> [!read-aloud]
> Towering iron-plated gates flanked by colossal tusks and spiked timber palisades guard the entrance to the red stone canyon. Inside, the ringing of hundreds of forge anvils echoes between the canyon cliffs, mingling with the rhythmic war-shouts of marching orc cohorts. Thick black smoke from smelting furnaces billows into the cloudless desert sky, carrying the scent of red clay and hot iron.

## Ancestral Hearth & Imperial Yoke
- **The Ash-Pits of the Unforgotten:** Hidden within the deep subterranean tunnels of the city lies the sacred central ash-pit of **[[the-two-deaths]]**, where clan elders secretly keep the ancestral embers burning despite strict Noxian prohibitions.
- **The Vanguard Legions:** Orgrimmar produces the heaviest siege armor, double-headed war axes, and frontline berserkers that Noxus deploys on the front lines of continental campaigns.
