## Overview
The largest metropolis on [[rubicon]], the **undisputed economic center of global trade**, and the **richest city in existence**. Built as an island fortress within three concentric stone rings, it serves as the global headquarters for all major vocational guilds and the command clearinghouse for the planetary **Containment Fleet**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Soaring white stone terraces and gilded aqueducts rise above a forest of trading ship masts. Three colossal concentric ring walls encircle the island, each grander than the last. The salty ocean air blends with the scents of foreign spices, burning coal, and the ceaseless roar of a million traders bargaining across the greatest market in existence.

## The Origin of Wealth & Naval Might
The Ring City did not achieve superpower status through commerce alone. **Fifteen hundred years ago**, when the world united to fight an existential cataclysm surging from the southern volcanic continent, every sovereign realm chose the neutral Ring City as the centralized hub to route mutual defense tithes, supplies, naval recruits, and warships. 

Over centuries, handling the collective defense budget and maritime logistics of the entire world transformed the city into the premier banking capital and naval armada of Rubicon.

## The Three Rings & Global Guilds
- **The Outer Ring:** Sprawling merchant docks, shipyards, deepwater warehouses, and international bazaars.
- **The Middle Ring:** The world headquarters for every major vocational guild (Merchant, Crafting, Scholar, Healer, and Adventurer Guilds).
- **The Inner Ring:** The seat of supreme authority, housing the grand assembly halls of the **Guild Council**.

## The Waning Containment Fleet
While the Ring City remains formally allied with **[[fleet-admiral-aislyn]]** and the **Asur (High Elves)** of **[[sky-watch]]**, the 1,500-year-old defense pact is unraveling:
- Shorter-lived continental empires (Cyrodiil, Noxus) have begun withholding their mandatory fleet subsidies, viewing the ancient southern threat as distant folklore.
- As funding dwindles, Ring City war galleons sit undermanned or are diverted into lucrative private merchant escorts, accelerating the [[waning-of-the-containment-fleet]].
