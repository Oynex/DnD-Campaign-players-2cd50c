## Overview
The Ring City is the largest metropolis on the known world and the undisputed commercial heart of all civilizations. An island city surrounded by vast harbors, it serves as the nexus point for global trade, commerce, and economic power. No city rivals it in size, prosperity, or influence.

> [!read-aloud]
> Soaring white stone terraces and gilded aqueducts rise above a forest of trading ship masts. Three colossal concentric ring walls encircle the island, each grander than the last. The salty ocean air blends with the scents of foreign spices, burning coal, and the ceaseless roar of a million traders bargaining across the greatest market in existence.

## Geography & Urban Architecture
The city is built in three concentric rings, each representing a distinct tier of importance, commerce, and political authority:

### The Outer Ring (The Commons & Harbors)
A sprawling expanse of markets, warehouses, docks, and merchant stalls. This is the public face of the Ring City, bustling with activity, filled with traders from every nation, sailors unloading cargo, and merchants hawking goods. Caravans arrive daily, ships dock constantly, and fortunes are made and lost in the open air.

### The Middle Ring (The Guild Enclave)
Contains the global headquarters of the world's vocational guilds:
- **Merchant Guild:** Coordinates trade routes and shipping agreements across the known world.
- **Crafting Guilds:** Master metalsmiths, leatherworkers, jewelers, and woodcarvers setting global trade standards.
- **Scholar Guild:** Preserves knowledge, running the greatest libraries and academies outside universities.
- **Healer Guild:** Oversees medical practices and trains physicians worldwide.
- **Adventurer Guild:** Registers mercenary expeditions, dangerous delves, and monster bounties.

### The Inner Ring (The Seat of Power)
The true center of global authority. Here stand the magnificent marble halls of the **Guild Council**, where the heads of all vocational guilds meet to determine policy. Access is strictly restricted to dignitaries and guild masters.

## The Law of Proximity
The closer one's office or residence is to the center ring, the greater one's power and influence. To move closer to the center is to rise in power; to be relegated to the outer ring is to be insignificant.

## Harbors & Commerce
Surrounding the island is the world's greatest deepwater harbor, accommodating thousands of trading galleons, swift couriers, and massive cargo haulers. Every piece of trade and information traveling across the globe passes through this harbor.
