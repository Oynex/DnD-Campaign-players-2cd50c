## Overview
A monumental fortress city built over a roaring river canyon within the central mountain range of [[aethelgard]]. Rivendel is the **vital continental choke point**—the only viable overland transit route connecting the Noxus-conquered eastern lands to the fertile, peaceful western plains.

> [!read-aloud]
> Soaring arched stone bridges span a mist-veiled chasm carved by churning river currents. Yet the serene, graceful architecture is now reinforced with gleaming brass plating, crystal-focused heavy ballistas, and energy-shield projectors bearing the crest of Piltover. Down the canyon, the iron war-horns of Noxus echo against the sheer cliffs.

## The Line of Defense
- **The Bottleneck:** The mountains to the north and south of Rivendel are impassable for mass armies. Anyone seeking to march from east to west must break through Rivendel.
- **The Piltover Alliance:** While Rivendel's native defenders are renowned for archery and guerrilla warfare, they would have fallen to the sheer numerical weight of Noxus long ago without the backing of [[piltover]]. Piltover supplies experimental magitech munitions, energy shielding, and heavy ordnance that continuously turn back Noxian siege assaults.
