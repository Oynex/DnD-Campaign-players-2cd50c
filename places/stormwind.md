## Overview
A grand coastal stone fortress and harbor city nestled on the eastern coast of [[aethelgard]], bordering the Dread Sea. Formerly the sovereign seat of a proud, independent human kingdom, Stormwind was eventually annexed by the legions of [[noxus]] after two separate imperial campaigns.

> [!read-aloud]
> Soaring white stone battlements and blue-shingled rooftops overlook a deepwater harbor, but the golden lion banners of the old kingdom have been torn down. In their place, the spiked red standards of Noxus fly from the battlements, and iron-shod sentries patrol the grand cathedral squares.

## The Two Invasions
- **The First Invasion & The Warpath:** Years ago, when Noxus first besieged Stormwind, the royal army was routed and the kingdom faced total annihilation. In overwhelming despair, the former King of Stormwind made a dark pact with **[[the-warpath]]**—sacrificing everything he cherished, including his own life. The dread black-armored host crashed into the imperial lines and utterly repelled Noxus from [[east-fall]]. It was during this brutal retreat that Grand General **[[darius]]** faced one of the commanders of the Warpath in single combat and miraculously survived.
- **The Second Invasion:** Several years later, after the dark price was fully paid and the old king was dead, Noxus returned in overwhelming force, finally subjugating the leaderless city.

## The Captive Prince
To prevent popular uprisings among the loyalist nobility, the late king's young heir—the **Prince of Stormwind**—was spared and taken to [[the-immortal-bastion]] in Noxus as a high-value "diplomatic hostage," guaranteeing the kingdom's forced compliance.
