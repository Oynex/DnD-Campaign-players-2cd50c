## Overview
The rugged, sun-scorched badlands dominating the southeastern interior of [[aethelgard]], characterized by towering red sandstone mesas, arid dust canyons, and treacherous stone ravines. Durotar is the ancestral homeland of the **Orcs**.

> [!read-aloud]
> A harsh landscape of burnt-orange clay, windswept hoodoos, and sheer canyon walls glowing under an unforgiving desert sun. Spiked iron watchtowers and spiked timber palisades crest the red cliffs, where heavily armored orc legionnaires now march beneath the blood-red banner of Noxus.

## The Three Tribes & The Noxian Subjugation
For generations, Durotar was locked in perpetual blood-feuds between three rival orcish clan strongholds:
- **Orgrimmar:** The grand fortress carved into the sheer canyon cliffs.
- **Razor Hill:** The central clan encampment and staging grounds.
- **Khand:** The harsh southern desert bastion guarding the frontier.

The clans spent centuries bashing each other in territorial warfare. Seeing their division, the military empire of **[[noxus]]** ruthlessly exploited their blood-feuds. Led by **[[darius|Grand General Darius]]**, Noxian forces played the chieftains against each other, crushed the defiant holds with overwhelming discipline, and absorbed the ferocious orcish warriors directly into the frontline shock legions of the Noxian army.
