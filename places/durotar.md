## Overview
The rugged, sun-scorched badlands dominating the southeastern interior of [[aethelgard]], characterized by towering red sandstone mesas, arid dust canyons, and treacherous stone ravines. Durotar is the ancestral homeland of the **Orcs**.

> [!read-aloud]
> A harsh landscape of burnt-orange clay, windswept hoodoos, and sheer canyon walls glowing under an unforgiving desert sun. Spiked iron watchtowers and spiked timber palisades crest the red cliffs, where heavily armored orc legionnaires now march beneath the blood-red banner of Noxus.

## Ancestral Faith: The Two Deaths
Before the coming of Noxus, the spiritual soul of Durotar was guided by **[[the-two-deaths]]**:
- **The Core Creed:** Orcs believe mortals die twice—first when the body perishes, and second when the last person forgets their name. Remembering the deeds and bloodlines of the fallen is therefore considered an act of supreme religious devotion.
- **Noxian Suppression:** Noxian commissars have outlawed tribal ancestor chanting and forced fallen orcs into unmarked mass trenches. In response, orc legionnaires secretly bury carved iron tokens beneath roadside cairns, defying the empire to inflict the Second Death upon their brothers.
