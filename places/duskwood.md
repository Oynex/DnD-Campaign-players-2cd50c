## Overview
A sprawling, ancient temperate forest spanning the northeastern wilderness of [[aethelgard]], nestled south of the Lake of Nurnen and bordering [[east-fall]]. Shrouded beneath a perpetual canopy of towering, black-barked ironwood trees, Duskwood earned its name from the twilight gloom that settles over the forest floor even at midday.

Historically the feudal domain of the **Kingdom of [[nighthaven]]**, the entire province was annexed by the **[[noxus|Noxian Empire]]** and has since become one of its most fiercely loyal territories.

> [!read-aloud]
> Towering black-pine canopies weave together overhead, filtering the sunlight into long rays of smoky silver mist. Paved imperial stone highways now cut straight through the brooding timber, lined with iron lanterns and fortified waystations. Along the road, local woodsmen haul heavy wagons of prized dark ironwood timber, singing tavern songs in the shadow of mounted Noxian ranger patrols whose silver wolfhounds keep the woodland predators at bay.

## The Feudal Past & The Malakor Shadow
For centuries, Duskwood was divided into a network of subservient duchies, counties, and baronies that answered to the royal throne of **[[king-victor-malakor]]** in Nighthaven:
- **The Aristocratic Terror:** The common folk, lumberjacks, and charcoal-burners lived in constant dread of royal abductions and demonic tithes. Corrupt vassal nobles enforced the king's cruel edicts, while serfs were forbidden from traveling between baronies without royal seals.
- **The Noxian Deliverance:** When Grand General **[[swain|Jericho Swain]]** marched into the woods, the royal army was shattered, the corrupt cultist aristocracy was eradicated, and the ancient kingdom was dismantled.

## Pax Noxiana & Regional Leadership
The transformation of Duskwood into a thriving imperial province is one of the greatest triumphs of Noxian integration:
- **Civilian Leadership:** While **[[lady-eleanor-malakor]]** cares for the capital city of Nighthaven, regional baronies are governed by trusted local leaders such as **[[baroness-evelyn-crowe]]**, who helped liberate the province.
- **Imperial Infrastructure:** Noxian engineers paved stone roads through the bogs, drained haunted marshes, and cleared the packs of shadow-beasts that once terrorized traveling merchants.
- **Duskwood Ironwood:** The prized rot-resistant timber of the forest flows continuously south to fuel Noxian war engines and shipyards, providing well-paid labor for thousands of local foresters.
