## Overview
A foreboding, gloomy forest spanning the northeastern wilderness of [[aethelgard]], situated south of the Lake of Nurnen. So dense and choked with twisted black-barked pines that sunlight rarely penetrates to the mossy forest floor, Duskwood has long been shrouded in sinister mists and supernatural dread.

> [!read-aloud]
> Twisted, gnarled trees with weeping black lichen lean over overgrown dirt roads. A thick, clammy fog clings to the forest floor, muffling sound and hiding the glowing eyes of nocturnal predators that prowl the shadowed timber.

## History & Noxian Control
For centuries, Duskwood was the isolated fiefdom of a ruthless cabal of dark mages who ruled from the gloomy castle fortress of [[nighthaven]]. Noxian legions under [[swain|Jericho Swain]] laid siege to the forest, extinguished the cabal, and annexed the region under imperial command.
