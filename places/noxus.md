## Overview
A brutal, militaristic expansionist empire situated on the harsh plains and crags of [[tamriel]]. Noxus values raw strength, military discipline, and pragmatism above birthright or nobility.

> [!read-aloud]
> Black iron battlements and blood-red standards crest rugged volcanic hills. The clang of smithies and the steady march of armored legionnaires echo through dark granite roads leading toward the obsidian monolith at the empire's heart.

## Societal Structure
Anyone who proves their utility to the war machine can rise to high office in Noxus. They utilize heavy infantry, brutal siege machines, and dark sorceries to expand their frontiers.

## Key Stronghold
- [[the-immortal-bastion]]: The gargantuan ancient citadel around which the Noxian capital was constructed.
