## Overview
A colossal, militaristic expansionist empire that has rapidly risen to become the undisputed superpower of eastern [[aethelgard]]. Driven by a brutal meritocratic philosophy where strength, cunning, and utility to the state dictate one's standing, Noxus respects neither ancient nobility nor peaceful borders.

The empire is ruled from [[the-immortal-bastion]] by the **Noxian Trifarix**, a triumvirate of three supreme leaders representing the core pillars of imperial power:
- **Vision:** Grand General **[[swain|Jericho Swain]]**, master tactician possessing demonic foresight.
- **Might:** Grand General **[[darius]]**, the Hand of Noxus and supreme field commander.
- **Guile:** **[[leblanc]]**, the ageless Pale Lady of the Mirrored Veil.

> [!read-aloud]
> Blood-red standards bearing the iron crest of Noxus ripple above monumental stepped ziggurats and obsidian parade grounds. Centurions in spiked black iron armor direct endless columns of legionnaires, war beasts, and heavy siege engines, all marching under the shadow of the colossal warlord statue guarding the harbor.

## The Founding Oath: Freedom from Tyrants
The foundational culture of Noxus is rooted in ancient rebellion:
- **The Warlord Sahn-Uzal:** Thousands of years ago, the scattered ancestral clans of eastern Aethelgard were brutally conquered and enslaved by **Sahn-Uzal** (Mordekaiser), who built [[the-immortal-bastion]].
- **The Overthrow of the Iron King:** When **[[leblanc]]** betrayed and felled the tyrant from behind, the freed clans took possession of the citadel and swore an eternal covenant: **Noxus would never suffer a king again.** 
- **The Meritocratic Creed:** Because power must never belong to hereditary crowns or lone despots, Noxus elevated meritocracy above all else—anyone, regardless of low birth, species, or origin, can rise to command if they possess the strength and will to serve the empire.

## Total Eastern Conquest
Noxus has conquered and annexed **every single territory east of the central mountain range** on [[aethelgard]]:
- **[[durotar]]:** Exploited the blood-feuds between the three warring orc tribes of *Orgrimmar*, *Razor Hill*, and *Khand*, subjugating them under [[darius]] and turning the orcs into frontline shock troops.
- **[[stormwind]] & [[east-fall]]:** Conquered the small coastal kingdom of Stormwind with ease, seizing its farmlands and holding the young Prince of Stormwind hostage in the capital.
- **[[duskwood]] & [[nighthaven]]:** Overthrew the local dark mage ruler, seizing the gothic castle of Nighthaven.
- **The Halt at [[mists-edge]]:** Their northern expansion has been brought to a standstill along the Lake of Nurnen, unable to penetrate the magical mists shielding [[esgaroth]].

## The Western Ambition
With the east secured, Noxus’s full military might is focused on breaking through the fortified mountain pass of **[[rivendel]]** to conquer the fertile grain basket of **[[westfall]]** and plunder the magitech secrets of **[[piltover]]**.
