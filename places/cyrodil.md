## Overview
Cyrodil is the premier human realm on [[tamriel]], an expansive nation known for grand stone cities, fertile heartlands, and an influential governing council of noble houses. Since the overthrow of the tyrannical Emperor in 1505 PA, Cyrodil has been governed not by a monarch, but by the [[council-of-cyrodil]], an oligarchy of thirteen noble houses.

> [!read-aloud]
> Paved imperial highways cut through golden wheat fields and thick temperate forests, linking heavily fortified garrisons to sparkling coastal harbors. Everywhere the banner of Cyrodil flies, an emblem of five centuries of united aristocratic rule.

## Major Settlements & Regions
- [[imperial-city]]: The sprawling seat of the Council Tower and imperial bureaucracy.
- [[daggerfall]]: Prosperous cliffside port and home of the [[academy-of-daggerfall]].
- **Redoran Woodlands:** Vast pine forests managed by House Redoran, currently disturbed by strange goblin incursions and supernatural anomalies.

## Governance & Military
Cyrodil boasts three legendary generals commanding its armies:
- [[general-cassandra-vale]]: Master of siege craft commanding the northern garrisons.
- [[general-valorian-thorne]]: Traditionalist supreme commander of the standing legions.
- [[general-kael-stormbringer]]: Young tactical prodigy currently dispatched in disguise to [[daggerfall]].
