## Overview
Cyrodil is the premier human realm on [[tamriel]], an ancient and storied empire occupying the central and southern territories of the continent. Founded two millennia ago and governed since the revolution 500 years ago by the [[council-of-cyrodil]] (the Six Great Houses), Cyrodiil is characterized by grand stone cities, fertile heartlands, and an aristocratic political system.

> [!read-aloud]
> Paved imperial highways cut through golden wheat fields and thick temperate forests, linking heavily fortified garrisons to sparkling coastal harbors. Everywhere the banner of Cyrodil flies—an emblem of two thousand years of history and five centuries of united aristocratic council rule.

## North vs. South Conflict
Cyrodiil is locked in a bitter historical conflict with the **[[skyrim|Northern Jarldom of Skyrim]]**:
- The imperial court dismisses the Nordic clans as uncivilized barbarians.
- Skyrim longships frequently raid Cyrodiil's western coastlines and trade convoys.
- Northern garrisons under General [[general-cassandra-vale]] remain on permanent alert along the rugged mountain passes.

## Key Settlements & Landmarks
- **[[imperial-city]]:** The 2,000-year-old circular island capital on Lake Rumare, seat of the Council Tower.
- **[[daggerfall]]:** Fortified academy port on the western cliffs, the pinnacle of human magic.
- **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]:** Grand waterborne temple city and spiritual stronghold in the south.
- **[[ductus-bridge]]:** Colossal trans-oceanic highway bridge leading east to the dwarven island of [[khazad-dum]] and [[gundabad]].
- **Corvan Woodlands:** Vast pine forests managed by House Corvan, source of timber, coal, and recent reality tremors.
