## Overview
The tallest mountain peak in all of [[tamriel]], soaring tens of thousands of feet above the snowy pine valleys of the **[[skyrim|Northern Jarldom of Skyrim]]**. Shrouded in perpetual blizzards and howling sub-zero gale winds, the Throat of the World is considered by both northern clans and southern theologians to be the most sacred natural landmark in the mortal world.

It was here that young **[[prophet-malor|Prophet Malor]]** undertook his legendary *Winter Fast*, scaling the sheer ice walls without cloak or flame and praying for seven weeks until the mountain frost spirits bowed in respect, acknowledging him as the Brother of Frost.

> [!read-aloud]
> A colossal, jagged spire of glacial ice and black granite pierces the upper stratosphere, its summit lost in a maelstrom of silver clouds and glittering snow-dust. The winds that howl down its sheer precipices sound like ancient voices chanting in forgotten tongues, chilling the blood of even the bravest Nordic warriors.

## Spiritual Importance
- **The Pilgrimage of Stone:** Young warriors of the **[[new-malors]]** undertake solitary ascents along the lower ridges, returning with a carved stone rune to prove they possess the resilience of Prophet Malor.
- **High Hrothgar:** An ancient monastery of silent grey monks perched seven thousand steps up the mountain face, dedicated to studying the cosmic resonance of the sky and preserving the oldest oral songs of the north.
