## Overview
A colossal stone-and-iron megastructure bridge connecting the eastern coastline of [[cyrodil]] across the churning oceanic strait to the mountainous island of the twin dwarven strongholds. 

Constructed through ancient joint engineering between human masons and dwarven rune-smiths, Ductus Bridge stands as one of the greatest architectural wonders of [[rubicon]].

> [!read-aloud]
> An awe-inspiring wonder of ancient stonework and dwarven masonry stretching miles across the churning ocean strait. Colossal granite pillars descend hundreds of fathoms into the seabed, supporting a multi-lane highway fortified with watchtowers, aqueducts, and iron toll-gates. Heavy cargo wagons and steam-driven haulers cross day and night, their iron wheels rumbling like rolling thunder above the waves.

## The Artery of Two Civilizations
Ductus Bridge serves as the indispensable overland conduit between humanity and the dwarven clans:
- **Eastbound Caravans:** Thousands of wagons and steam carts organized by **[[merchant-prince-cedric-sterling]]** haul grain, livestock, vintage wines, and timber into the open gates of **[[khazad-dum]]** and the military storehouses of **[[gundabad]]**.
- **Westbound Caravans:** In return, endless convoys of refined mithril, gemstone jewelry, masterwork plate armor, and siege weapons flow directly into Cyrodiil's markets and imperial legion depots.
- **Maintenance & Defenses:** Jointly garrisoned by Cyrodiil legionaries and dwarven shield-bearers, with structural maintenance supervised by House Corvan under **[[thaddeus-corvan]]**.
