## Overview
A vast, shimmering inland freshwater sea located in northeastern [[aethelgard]], nestled between the misty shores of **[[mists-edge]]** and the mountain foothills of **[[erebor]]**. 

Fed by subterranean aquifers and glacial runoff, the Lake of Nurnen is renowned for its tranquil sapphire waters and the legendary floating settlement of **[[esgaroth|Esgaroth (Lake-Town)]]**. More importantly, the lake serves as the immovable natural barrier that halted the relentless northern advance of the **[[noxus|Noxian Legion]]**, due to the impenetrable, disorienting magical mists that drift across its waters.

> [!read-aloud]
> Stagnant, glassy water stretches to the horizon like a colossal sheet of polished obsidian. Eerie banks of silver-white mist drift lazily across the surface, concealing the distant spires of the lake-town. The air is deathly silent save for the occasional splash of colossal freshwater sturgeons rising from the unfathomable depths below.

## Strategic & Magical Significance
- **The Northern Wall of Noxus:** Noxian war galleons attempting to sail across the Lake of Nurnen have repeatedly vanished into the mists, emerging weeks later on distant southern shores with mad, starving crews.
- **The Secret Dragon Tunnel:** Beneath the lake floor lies the subterranean waterway connecting the deep cellars of the Lake-Town directly into the volcanic under-caverns of [[erebor]], secretly excavated centuries ago by **[[the-archmage-dragon]]**.
