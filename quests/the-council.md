## Quest Overview
While tracking goblin raiders threatening timber settlements in the Redoran forests, the party discovers an otherworldly spatial anomaly that defies known laws of magic.

> [!read-aloud]
> The goblin tracks end abruptly at the edge of a shattered clearing. In the center, space itself seems warped, light bending around an invisible fracture in the air that hums in eerie unison with the newcomer's pulse.

## Objectives
- [x] Track goblin raiders into the deep Redoran woodlands.
- [ ] Investigate the reality fracture and recover crystal remnants.
- [ ] Determine the connection between the anomaly and [[anirudh-b]]'s manifestation.
