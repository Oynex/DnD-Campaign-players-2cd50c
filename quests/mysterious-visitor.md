## Quest Overview
Following the disturbances in the Redoran woods and escalating tremors across the realm, the [[council-of-cyrodil]] issues an urgent imperial summons to the Council Tower in the [[imperial-city]].

> [!read-aloud]
> An imperial courier flanked by two armored legionnaires bears a platinum scroll case stamped with the thirteen seals of the Council. The lords of Cyrodil request an immediate, private audience.

## Objectives
- [ ] Travel to the [[imperial-city]] Council Tower.
- [ ] Navigate political inquiries from [[merchant-prince-cedric-tharn]] and [[lord-meridian-septim]].
- [ ] Meet privately with [[lady-vivienne-moth]] to discuss the planar breaches.
