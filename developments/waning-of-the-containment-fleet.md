## Overview
Fifteen hundred years ago, a catastrophic, world-threatening evil surged from the volcanic ash wastes of the southern continent. In response, every mortal nation of [[rubicon]] united under a historic covenant, pooling their ships, wealth, and warriors to establish a permanent oceanic cordon: **The Containment Fleet**. 

Support, maritime tithes, and replacement vessels from all nations were routed through **[[the-ring-city]]**, turning the neutral island into the supreme economic clearinghouse and naval colossus of the world, operating in tandem with the high elven armada of **[[sky-watch]]**.

Today, that grand coalition is crumbling.

> [!read-aloud]
> Down in the salt-crusted docks of the Ring City, rows of colossal war galleons sit undermanned and awaiting overdue timber from the mainland. While elven sea-captains scan the southern horizon with mounting dread, the bustling merchant houses debate tariffs and continental border disputes, treating the ancient covenant as little more than a bothersome tax.

## The Lifespan Divide & Generational Amnesia
- **The Asur Living Memory:** The High Elves of [[summerset-isle]] possess extraordinarily long lifespans; senior commanders and elders were either alive during the cataclysm 1,500 years ago or only a generation removed. For the elves, the terror of the southern horrors is a living, visceral reality.
- **The Short-Lived Amnesia:** For humans, orcs, and other shorter-lived races, fifteen centuries represent dozens of generations. The apocalyptic war has degraded into distant myth and exaggerated bedtime stories.
- **Waning Commitments:** Mainland nations (Cyrodiil, Noxus, the Jarldoms) increasingly withhold their mandated naval subsidies, diverting gold and manpower to their own border conflicts.
