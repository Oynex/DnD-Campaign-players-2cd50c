## Overview
The ancient, state-sanctioned religion of [[cyrodil]] and the southern provinces of [[tamriel]], practiced by millions of citizens and administered directly from the sacred waterborne ziggurats of the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]**. Its followers and clergy identify as **Orthodox Malorists**, preserving what they hold to be the continuous, unbroken ecclesiastical line of **Prophet Malor**.

Originating 3,500 years ago from the miraculous revelations, trials, and wanderings of Prophet Malor, Orthodox Malorism is a magnificent, liturgical faith characterized by colossal marble cantons, sacred myrrh and frankincense censers, gold-woven liturgical vestments, an intricate priestly hierarchy, and a formidable standing holy army known as **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Great bronze bells reverberate across the salt waters as clouds of sweet myrrh billow from massive golden censers swung by ivory-robed choir monks. Tens of thousands of kneeling worshippers recite synchronized canticles beneath soaring vaulted arches lined with gold-leaf mosaics of Prophet Malor, bowing their heads as robed prelates sprinkle sanctified water from crystal chalices.

## Birth in the Deep North & The Continental Pilgrimage
A fundamental truth acknowledged by both North and South is that **Prophet Malor was born in the deep, frozen crags of northern [[skyrim]]**:
- **Northern Birthright:** Malor was born into a humble clan steading amidst the blizzard-blasted glaciers of the northernmost reaches. As a youth, he communed with the ancient frost winds and lived among the reindeer herders, enduring winters that claimed whole villages.
- **The Call Southward:** Guided by divine visions of an eternal light meant to civilize all mortal races, Malor left his clan behind as a young man, embarking on a lifelong journey that traversed the length of Tamriel from the arctic north to the southern sea.
- **The Clashing Claims:** 
  - **The Northern Claim:** The people of Skyrim hold that Malor was born of their own blood, bred by northern frost and hearthstone values, and that his true spirit remained in the wild mountain air.
  - **The Southern Claim:** The Orthodox church argues that while his mortal vessel was born in the north, his true prophetic awakening and divine fulfillment occurred only when he reached the south, where his greatest miracles were performed and where he chose to lay his ultimate resting place.

## The Legendary Feats & Wanderings
The *Book of Trials* documents the seventy years of Malor’s pilgrimage across Tamriel:
- **The Winter Fast on the Throat of the World:** As a young seeker in the deep north, Malor scaled the frozen precipice during the Great Ice Winter, fasting without cloak or fire for seven weeks until the mountain frost spirits acknowledged his sanctity.
- **The Crucible of the Ashlands:** Crossing the scorched volcanic wastes of central Tamriel where no water flowed, Malor struck his cedar staff into obsidian rock, bringing forth a bubbling spring that quenched his followers for forty leagues.
- **The Mercy to the Red Knife Bandits:** Ambushed in the Crag of Weeping by thirty cutthroats who beat him and took his cloak, Malor knelt and washed the mud from their boots when they were done. Struck by his utter lack of fear and radiating calm, their chieftain threw down his daggers; twelve of the bandits renounced violence on the spot to follow him as lifelong disciples.
- **The Tyrant’s Iron Cage:** Imprisoned for three years in the subterranean oubliette of King Vaelen the Cruel for preaching against human sacrifice, Malor was offered gold, concubines, and freedom if he would recant. He emerged three years later untouched by madness or pestilence, walking through heavy iron cell doors that miraculously unlocked themselves on the night the tyrant choked on his own wine.
- **The Walking of the Black Plague:** When the Crimson Rot laid waste to three provincial valleys, Malor walked into quarantine trenches without mask or protective herbs. He cradled the dying, smoothed their brows, and whispered blessings; those he touched passed in peaceful slumber, while hundreds miraculously woke with cool skin, cured of the plague.
- **The Crossing of the Ghostly Seas:** Sailing aboard a battered skiff across the storm-torn Sea of Ghosts, monstrous rogue waves tore the sail to ribbons and washed the oars overboard. Malor stood at the prow, lifted his hands into the howling gale, and commanded the tempest into silence, calming the tempestuous waves into glassy obsidian.
- **The Miracle of the Barren Shore:** Thirty-five centuries ago, having journeyed across the entire continent, Malor reached the salt-crusted marshes of the southern bay. He walked alone into the brine, kneeling in prayer and fasting for forty continuous days until a miraculous subterranean geyser of sweet, crystal-pure freshwater erupted through the sea floor, hallowing the waters where the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]** was founded.

## Churches of Malor Across Tamriel
Unlike local pagan shrines, the Orthodox church established a ubiquitous architectural and spiritual presence across the continent:
- **Ubiquitous Shrines & Cathedrals:** In nearly every fortified city, trade hub, and provincial crossroad across [[tamriel]], a **Church of Malor** stands tall. Built of white stone with stained glass and bronze bell-towers, these churches provide sanctuary, distribute daily bread, host weddings and funerals, and maintain healing cloisters.
- **Chapterhouses of the Knights:** Attached to every major Church of Malor is a chapterhouse of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**, housing paladins, inquisitors, and clerks who enforce canon law and defend settlements from monsters.

## The Great Schism (2,000 Years Ago)
For fifteen centuries, the faith of Malor was unified. However, around the founding of Cyrodiil and the Imperial City, the southern synods codified the **Doctrine of the Anointed Vessel**:
- **The Orthodox Mandate:** Southern scholars argued that the Prophet’s divine revelations demanded permanent, ordained custodians—*"The divine light must be gathered into a vessel of consecrated marble and gold, guided by anointed hands to sustain civilization against primeval chaos."*
- **Institutional Splendor:** This doctrine cemented **Orthodox Malorism**: an opulent, centralized church governed by archbishops, funding sprawling public works, and establishing ecclesiastical tribunals.
- **The Northern Rebellion:** When the northern holds of [[skyrim]] broke away to adopt the radical, decentralized **[[new-malors]]** movement, the Orthodox high synod condemned them as heretical rebels who discarded holy canon for barbarian campfire paganism.

## The Gilded Veil: Splendor and Rot
Orthodox Malorism is deeply cherished by the common people, who see its grand cathedrals as sanctuaries offering daily breadlines, orphanages, and medical care under benevolent figures like **[[archbishop-aldwin-indoril]]**. Yet beneath this veil of holiness lies deep institutional corruption:
- **The Mandatory Holy Tithe:** Every business, guild, and freehold under Cyrodiil's banner is legally required to pay a hefty spiritual levy to the church in Vivec. While it maintains the empire's grand monuments, high-ranking bishops quietly siphon colossal fortunes into private villas and political favors.
- **Plenary Indulgences:** Wealthy patricians and merchant princes routinely purchase confidential pardons and liturgical dispensations for illicit crimes, cloaked beneath pious Latinate rites.
- **The Holy Crusade:** Enforced by **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**, the church commands an invincible standing army that cleanses witch covens in the marshes, banishes dark curses, and aggressively guards the border passes against northern raiders.
