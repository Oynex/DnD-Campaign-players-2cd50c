## Overview
The ancient, once-glorious solar religion that held spiritual dominance across the kingdoms, river valleys, and coastal plains of eastern **[[aethelgard]]** for more than two thousand years. Known to historians as **The Radiant Covenant** (and its followers as *Covenantors* or *Solar Brethren*).

Centuries before the rise of the black iron war-engine of **[[noxus]]**, the Radiant Covenant was the spiritual bedrock of eastern civilization. It taught that mortal life was sustained by the benevolent warmth of **Aethel-Sol**, revering solar dawn rites, compassionate hospital cloisters, and solemn covenants of hospitality and mercy. Today, the religion is on the verge of total extinction—its grand marble basilicas pulled down, its golden solar crests melted into legionary coins, and its clergy hunted to near annihilation by the expansionist legions of Noxus.

> [!read-aloud]
> Amongst the cracked, weed-choked flagstones of a ruined solar cathedral in the eastern plains, soot stains mar the white granite columns where Noxian siege fire tore through stained-glass sunbursts. The grand gold-leaf altar has been chipped away by iron chisels, and atop the pediment where the sun-disk once stood, the blood-red iron crest of Noxus has been hammered directly into the consecrated stone.

## The Systematic Purge by Noxus
When the Noxian Trifarix launched its total conquest of eastern Aethelgard, the Radiant Covenant was targeted not merely as an enemy faction, but as the supreme cultural obstacle to imperial expansion:
- **Ideological Anathema:** Noxus preaches that mercy is a fatal poison and that only personal strength and utility to the empire matter. The Covenant's doctrines of universal compassion, sanctuary for the weak, and submission to divine will were branded by Grand General **[[swain|Swain]]** and **[[darius|Darius]]** as a paralyzing mental illness that kept eastern kingdoms weak.
- **The Melting of the Relics:** Upon annexing **[[stormwind]]**, **[[east-fall]]**, and **[[duskwood]]**, Noxian shock legions systematically sacked every Cathedral of Solace. Golden altar chalices, solar crowns, and ancient reliquaries containing the bones of first-era saints were carted to the foundry furnaces of **[[the-immortal-bastion]]** and melted into iron-alloy armor plating.
- **The Execution of the High Patriarchs:** Priests and nuns who refused to sign the Imperial Loyalty Oath or renounce divine law were declared enemies of the state and sentenced to the gladiator pits or public gallows.

## The Clandestine Remnant (The Ember Cloistered)
While the magnificent stone churches have been reduced to ashes or converted into Noxian cavalry barracks, the Radiant Covenant has not entirely perished:
- **Catacomb Whispers:** In the subterranean crypts beneath ruined villages and within the deepest tangled groves of Duskwood, small pockets of faithful still gather in darkness, lighting solitary wax candles beneath hidden cloth sun-emblems.
- **The Smuggled Scriptures:** Covenant monks, disguised as travelling tinkerers or grain peddlers, carry parchment fragments of the *Book of Dawn* sewn into the linings of their coats, secretly baptizing the children of subjugated farmers.
- **The Hope of the West:** The surviving Covenantors pray fervently toward the western mountain wall, believing that if the western bastions of **[[rivendel]]** and **[[piltover]]** can halt the Noxian advance, the light of Aethel-Sol may one day return to cleanse their ancestral lands.
