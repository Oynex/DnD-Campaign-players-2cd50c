## Overview
The volatile, morally hazardous alchemical science developed in the toxic borderland between corporate laboratories and the lawless subterranean sumps of **[[piltover]]**. Pioneered legitimately by **Bio-Synthetica** under Dr. Corin Reve to treat industrial lung rot and enhance physical labor endurance, the technology was rapidly corrupted, reverse-engineered, and weaponized by **[[the-sump-syndicate]]** into potent narcotic combat stimulants known colloquially on the streets as **Shimmer**.

Chem-enhancements utilize discarded crystal leaching runoff mixed with heavy organic stimulants. When injected into the bloodstream via subcutaneous brass injectors or pressurized vapor masks, the chemical triggers instantaneous cellular mutation, deadening pain, accelerating adrenaline by tenfold, and granting ordinary factory workers temporary, monstrous physical strength.

> [!read-aloud]
> Inside a grime-encrusted glass ampoule, a viscous, luminescent violet serum pulses with an unnatural, sickly internal glow. When pumped through subcutaneous brass conduits into the neck of a syndicate bruiser, his veins bulge with purple fire, his pupils dilate into pitch-black pools, and his muscles tear and expand with terrifying, frenzied velocity.

## Applications & Double-Edged Blade
- **Industrial Stimulants:** In corporate factories, heavily diluted micro-doses of Bio-Synthetica stimulants are pumped through ventilation shafts to keep laborers awake and working through fourteen-hour double shifts without collapsing.
- **Combat Mutagens:** The Sump Syndicate equips elite enforcers and pit-fighters with high-concentration Shimmer harnesses, turning expendable thugs into terrifying berserkers capable of ripping through iron doors and shrugging off rifle bullets.
- **Catastrophic Degradation:** The price of chem-enhancement is horrific. Prolonged usage causes irreversible neurological degradation, brittle bones, violent psychotic episodes, and agonizing cellular necrosis once the serum wears off.

## Weapon of Rebellion
While the crime barons use Shimmer to enforce street control, radical factions within **[[the-broken-cycle]]** have begun stealing stockpiles of refined violet injectors, intending to distribute them to subterranean shock cadres for the prophesied day when they pull the Great Lever against the upper spires.
