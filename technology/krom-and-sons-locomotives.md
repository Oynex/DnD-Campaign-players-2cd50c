## Overview
The pinnacle of continental transportation, designed and manufactured exclusively by **Krom & Sons Locomotive (KSL)** under the direction of Baroness Mireille Krom. This technology encompasses both heavy armored rail dreadnoughts and soaring, rigid-keeled dirigible airships that connect **[[piltover]]** to the strategic fortress pass of **[[rivendel]]** and the agricultural heartlands of western [[aethelgard]].

Unlike primitive coal-fired steam engines found in outlying territories, Krom locomotives utilize sealed magitech boilers where pressurized water is superheated instantly by submerged, high-frequency crystal arrays. This produces infinite, sootless steam pressure that drives multi-thousand-ton iron behemoths across steep mountain inclines at unprecedented speeds.

> [!read-aloud]
> A colossal leviathan of riveted iron and polished brass rolls into the terminal, its massive driving wheels taller than two men. Blue crystalline light glows intensely through heavy glass ports along the boiler flank, illuminating plumes of pristine white steam that hiss from venting chimneys. Above the station, a three-decked sky-freighter maneuvers into its mooring tower, its keel-mounted repulsion vanes thrumming with deep harmonic vibration.

## Key Deployments
- **The Western Iron Artery:** The reinforced double-track railway running from Piltover's harbor directly east through the mountain tunnels to **[[rivendel]]**, transporting tons of munitions, grain, and troop cohorts daily.
- **The Sky-Freighter Fleet:** Armored airships equipped with crystal-charged lifting bladders and directional brass propellers, capable of bypassing treacherous mountain passes and scouting troop movements across the central divide.

## Vulnerabilities & Sabotage
The reliance on high-speed rail has made KSL lines a prime target:
- **Noxian Infiltration:** Noxian saboteurs actively seek to plant explosive charges along the Ductus canyon trestles to sever the supply line to Rivendel.
- **Worker Militancy:** Subterranean rail hands affiliated with **[[the-broken-cycle]]** frequently tamper with switching junctions, warning that the "Iron Levers" will be the first things seized when the uncoupling begins.
