## Overview
The revolutionary personal weaponry and heavy defensive artillery designed by **The Aether-Weave Munitions Trust**, commanded by Director Kaelen Voss. This line of weaponry is the primary military deterrent ensuring that the expansionist shock legions of **[[noxus]]** cannot easily breach the western passes of [[aethelgard]].

Moving entirely past gunpowder and flintlocks, Aether-Weave arms utilize standardized, rechargeable crystal capacitors loaded directly into the stock or breech of the weapon. When the trigger is pulled, a magnetic coil accelerates a bolt of hyper-concentrated radiant energy or superheated metal slugs that punch through heavy steel plate and monster hide with devastating armor-piercing kinetic force.

> [!read-aloud]
> The crystal rifle rests in the marksman's hands with perfectly balanced, machined grace. Through slotted vents along the blued-steel barrel, a vibrant cerulean beam charges with an audible, high-pitched whine. Upon discharge, there is no smoke or sulfur stench—only a blinding flash of blue lightning and a crack of thunder that leaves a vaporized crater through two feet of solid granite.

## Models & Tactical Classes
1. **The Voss-Pattern Carbine:** Standard-issue marksman rifle supplied to the defenders of **[[rivendel]]**, capable of accurate lethal fire out to six hundred yards with zero bullet drop.
2. **Aether-Cannon Batteries:** Massive static defense cannons mounted atop the granite battlements of Rivendel, designed to vaporize approaching Noxian siege towers and armored war-drakes at extreme range.
3. **Personal Hex-Pistols:** Compact, ornate sidearms carried by corporate executives, high patricians, and wealthy bounty hunters across Piltover.

## Counter-Proliferation
The Council classifies the internal blueprints of Aether-Weave firearms under supreme military secrecy. Anyone caught smuggling crystal cartridges or weapon schematics out of Piltover is sentenced to life imprisonment or quiet execution by corporate enforcers.
