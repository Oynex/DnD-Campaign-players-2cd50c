## Overview
An ancient, highly sophisticated magical crafting discipline originating five millennia ago in **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay|Grand Cathay]]**. Runic magic is the art of engraving specialized celestial ideograms, geometric glyphs, and metaphysical matrices directly into stone, metals, weapons, armor, and fine jewelry. 

Unlike fleeting verbal incantations cast by mages, runic inscriptions permanently hard-code specific magical effects into physical matter. By infusing the engraved channels with powdered magic crystals and celestial inks, a master smith can impart unbreakable durability to a blade, grant armor resistance against dragonfire, or cause an architectural keystone to levitate indefinitely without ongoing concentration from a spellcaster.

> The hammer strikes the cold silver chisel with a ringing, musical chime. As the runesmith carves the final sweeping stroke of the warding ideogram into the breastplate, a flash of azure light sparks along the groove. Liquid lapis-and-crystal ink is poured into the recess, sinking into the steel until the entire glyph pulses with a warm, steady, rhythmic heartbeat of contained arcana.

## History & The Great Ban
- **The Ancient Golden Age:** Five thousand years ago, runic crafting was the cornerstone of Cathayan prosperity. Cities were powered by levitating runic towers, fields were irrigated by perpetual runic water-stones, and legionaries carried weapons that never dulled.
- **The Cataclysm of Over-Exploitation:** The insatiable demand for magic crystals to hard-code ever-larger workings led to reckless strip-mining and unstable crystalline shortcuts. A catastrophic harmonic chain reaction shattered the regional leylines, permanently draining the surrounding land into what is now **[[the-rune-desert]]**.
- **The Imperial Proscription:** In the aftermath of the catastrophe, the **[[celestial-dragon-emperor]]** issued an imperial edict banning the study, practice, and civilian ownership of runic crafting tools across the empire under pain of death.

## Modern Regulation & Certified Runesmithing
Because runic crafting represents thousands of years of invaluable cultural heritage and military technology, the Dragon Court did not completely destroy the knowledge:
- **Imperial Monopoly:** The Dragon Court created the **[[guild-of-imperial-runesmiths]]** to strictly regulate, license, and oversee the trade.
- **Strict Quotas & Safe Practices:** Certified smiths are bound by sacred oaths, restricted to using small, stabilized crystal allowances, and forbidden from creating self-sustaining feedback loops or monumental enchantments.
- **Comparison to Magitech:** While Piltover's **[[magitech]]** relies on mechanized gears, conduits, and replaceable battery cells, Cathayan runic crafting is permanent, metaphysical, and structural—becoming an intrinsic property of the forged material itself.
