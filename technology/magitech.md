## Overview
Magitech represents the sophisticated synthesis of arcane sorcery and precision mechanical engineering. Rather than relying on physical fuel, magitech devices channel raw magical energy through carved crystal matrices, resonating conduits, and gear-driven housings.

> [!read-aloud]
> Polished brass casings and filigreed silver rods house glowing azure crystals that hum with contained harmonic vibration. As the gears click into place, a steady, heatless blue luminescence courses through the apparatus, defying gravity and friction.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Crystal Matrices:** Arcane crystals cut along specific geometric facets to focus and regulate spells without mortal spellcasting fatigue.
- **Harmonic Resonance:** Pioneered at the [[academy-of-daggerfall]] through the **Resonance Tower**, enabling city-scale wards and floating architectures.
- **Applications:** High-precision chronometers, levitation elevators, ward generators, and experimental planar communicators.
