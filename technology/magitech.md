## Overview
Magitech represents the sophisticated synthesis of raw arcane crystal matrices and precision mechanical engineering. Rather than relying on physical combustion or steam, magitech devices channel intense magical energy through cut crystal capacitors, resonating conduits, and gear-driven automaton housings.

> [!read-aloud]
> Polished brass casings and filigreed silver rods house glowing azure crystals that hum with contained harmonic vibration. As the gears click into place, a steady, heatless blue luminescence courses through the apparatus, defying gravity and friction.

## Global Distribution & Technological Divide
- **The Vanguard of Progress:** The city-state of [[piltover]] on [[aethelgard]] is the undisputed world leader in magitech, operating **decades ahead of all other civilizations**. Their crystal-harnessed weaponry, autonomous defenses, rail locomotives, and dirigible airships are technological wonders unrivaled elsewhere.
- **Human Research:** The [[academy-of-daggerfall]] in [[cyrodil]] pioneers magitech on [[tamriel]], focusing on architectural resonance and large-scale defensive warding.

## The Secret Instability
While magitech has enriched Piltover and reinforced Daggerfall, artificers and mages across both regions independently report alarming developments: raw crystals are becoming increasingly volatile, humming with harmonic feedback that resists standard containment, suggesting that the underlying energy reservoir is fracturing.
