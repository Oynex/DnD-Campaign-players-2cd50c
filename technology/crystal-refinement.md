## Overview
The foundational industrial discipline that powers the entire technological miracle of **[[piltover]]**. Pioneered by **Aethelgard Magitech-Refining Ltd.** under Chief Executive Vanderbilt Ferros, crystal refinement is the specialized chemical and mechanical process of transforming volatile, raw mana crystals dredged from the Great Crater into stabilized, standardized power cells.

Without refinement, raw crater crystals detonate violently when exposed to friction, sonic vibrations, or ambient heat. Through precision diamond-edge cutting, mineral bath stabilization, and pressurized copper casing, Piltover produces standardized energy batteries capable of powering everything from street lamps and telegraph spires to heavy locomotive engines.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the high, glass-domed distillation floor of the refinery, massive iron centrifuges spin with a deafening, harmonic hum. Streams of cooled vitriol bath wash over glowing azure crystal clusters, hissing as excess static electricity discharges along heavy copper lightning rods. Masked artificers in rubberized leather peer through quartz viewports, adjusting micrometric brass clamps that carve the crystals into flawless geometric dodecahedrons.

## Industrial Production & Grades
Piltover classifies refined crystals into three distinct industrial grades:
1. **Grade-A (Resonance Core):** Flawless, pure azure gems reserved strictly for the **[[council-of-piltover]]**, military defense barriers at [[rivendel]], and sky-galleon flight engines.
2. **Grade-B (Industrial Cell):** The commercial workhorse powering **[[krom-and-sons-locomotives|KSL rail engines]]**, automated factory conveyor lines, and telegraph repeater towers.
3. **Grade-C (Slag Shards):** Unstable byproduct and cut-offs. Officially slated for disposal, Grade-C crystal shards are routinely stolen or siphoned by **[[the-sump-syndicate]]** to fuel black-market chem-weapons and illicit underworld stimulants.

## The Cracking Phenomenon
In recent months, refinery engineers have documented an alarming threshold anomaly: newly dredged crystals from deeper strata within the crater are displaying erratic, unpredictable harmonic frequency spikes. When refined, several cells have begun "cracking" spontaneously, discharging localized reality-distortion pulses that dissolve organic matter into fine blue ash.
