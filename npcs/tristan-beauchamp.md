## Overview
The financial master of House Beauchamp and Chancellor of the High Patrician Banking Vaults in **[[piltover]]**. Lord Tristan Beauchamp controls the oldest private banking network in western [[aethelgard]], holding the mortgages, municipal war bonds, and ancestral gold vaults of both noble families and corporate conglomerates.

While corporate CEOs boast about their high-margin crystal profits, Tristan quietly reminds them that their factories sit on leased patrician land and their overseas expansion is financed by Beauchamp bank bonds. Elegant, ruthless, and possessing an encyclopedic knowledge of international trade law, Beauchamp is the quiet puppet master who keeps the Corporate Council from completely swallowing the Municipal Senate.

> [!read-aloud]
> Sitting behind a table of polished black obsidian inside the subterranean Beauchamp vaults, Lord Tristan Beauchamp examines a ledger bound in dragon-scale leather. He wears an impeccably tailored charcoal coat with brass filigree buttons, his raven hair brushed back from a pale, aristocratic brow. He smiles with chilling politeness, tapping a silver quill against a stack of defaulted corporate promissory notes.

## International Banking & Dirty Gold
- **Debt Leverage:** Holds massive debt notes over multiple corporate board members, allowing him to quietly influence Council decisions from behind the scenes.
- **The Sump Conduit:** Works covertly with **[[madame-rouge]]** and **[[silas-the-red]]**, laundering illicit chem-revenues through offshore trade ventures in exchange for lucrative interest cuts and private protection details.
