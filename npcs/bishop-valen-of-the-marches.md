## Overview
The veteran quartermaster, field marshal, and political anchor of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**, Bishop Valen commands the border fortresses, supply lines, and garrison legions from the western bastions of the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]**. A grizzled veteran of forty campaigns with graying stubble and a broken nose that never set straight, Valen looks more like an old legionary sergeant than a prince of the church.

Valen represents the weary pragmatism at the core of the military church. He knows full well that the high clergy in Vivec is greedy and that [[high-prelate-lucian-vane]] siphons gold; yet he also knows that without the iron discipline, castle garrisons, and supply lines of the holy army, barbarian warlords and demonic curses would overwhelm the southern provinces. He compromises his morals daily and handles the required dirty work so that the younger generation of paladins can remain uncorrupted.

> [!read-aloud]
> Hunched over a war-table covered in dispatch ribbons, supply manifests, and perimeter maps, Bishop Valen gnaws on a hard tack biscuit. Battered, notched steel plate clanks softly as he leans forward, his heavy, scarred hands pointing out troop movements along the northern passes. A weathered wolf-fur cloak hangs over his shoulders, and a heavy bastard sword sits bare on the table beside his tankard.

## Background & Forty Years of Iron
Recruited from a frontier garrison at eighteen, Valen spent his entire adult life fighting in the trenches. He has defended against northern reaver longships, fought plague ghouls in the fens, and led cavalry charges that broke border rebellions. He was promoted to Bishop not because of theological acumen, but because the holy army’s supply logistics would collapse without his peerless organizational mind.

He sleeps on a straw mattress in the canton barracks rather than the plush featherbeds of the episcopal palace, sharing bread and watered beer with his common recruits.

## The Surrogate Daughter: Protecting Alyssa's Soul
When **[[holy-commander-alyssa-thorne]]** first arrived in the cloisters as a grieving highborn novice, Valen saw in her a rare, incandescent purity that the cynical church usually crushes within a year. 
- **The Mentor:** Valen personally took Alyssa under his wing, training her in tactical formations, martial discipline, and how to command the respect of battle-hardened knights on the frontline.
- **The Father's Shield:** Valen loves Alyssa like his own daughter. He deliberately stepped back from frontline glory, shifting his focus to quartermaster logistics and behind-the-scenes political maneuvering specifically to handle the dark compromises, extortion threats, and dirty work of the church. By absorbing the filth himself, he ensures Alyssa's innocence and moral conviction remain completely unsullied.
- **Mutual Reverence:** Alyssa views Valen with unwavering filial devotion, trusting his strategic advice unconditionally.

## The Puppet Master of the Bishop Council
Beneath his gruff soldier exterior, Valen wields total, unbroken control over the council of Five Warrior-Bishops:
- **Outvoting Lucian Vane:** [[high-prelate-lucian-vane]] constantly tries to steer holy crusades for commercial profit and tithe extortion, but Valen routinely plays him like a battered fiddle. Lucian despises being outmaneuvered by an unwashed trench soldier, yet he can **never** outvote him: whenever a vote is called, Alyssa votes with her mentor out of trust, and the zealot **[[inquisitor-general-malachai]]** votes with Valen out of martial respect for the old veteran. With three guaranteed votes, Valen dictates council policy.
- **Malachai’s Leash:** Even the terrifying fanatic Malachai respects Valen’s combat pedigree, treating the old general with quiet deference and keeping his inquisitorial fanatics far away from Alyssa's hospital brigades.
- **Balthazar’s Silent Nod:** True to his cold nature, [[arch-justiciar-balthazar-karr]] remains indifferent to council bickering, yet privately admires how smoothly and quietly the old veteran neutralizes political crises before they violate canon law.
