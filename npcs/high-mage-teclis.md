## Overview
The supreme **Leader of the [[crystal-tower]]** and undisputed master of elven high magic on [[rubicon]]. He is the brother of **[[alith-anar|Phoenix King Alith Anar]]**. Where his brother commands armies and statecraft from [[alinor]], Teclis commands the arcane wisdom of the Crystal Tower and the defense of the planar veil.

> [!read-aloud]
> Robed in flowing prismatic silks that shift with ambient magical currents, High Mage Teclis studies an enormous glowing projection of the planetary leylines. An ornate staff topped with a flawless glowing crystal rests in his hand, radiating absolute mastery over the weave.

## Legendary Arcane Power
- **Master of the Crystal Tower:** Oversees all elven magical research, dimensional wards, and planetary planar monitoring.
- **The Trans-Oceanic Conduit:** Actively maintains the planetary leyline linking the Crystal Tower directly northward to **[[daggerfall]]**, keeping human and elven magical frequencies aligned.
