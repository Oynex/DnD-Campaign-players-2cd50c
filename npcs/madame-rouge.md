## Overview
The shadowy queen of Piltover’s illicit narcotics and vice trade, Madame Rouge is the co-ruler of **[[the-sump-syndicate]]**. Operating from *The Velvet Cistern*—a magnificent, subterranean pleasure palace carved into an ancient cistern hall beneath the central canal—she controls the synthesis, dilution, and distribution of the narcotic combat stimulant known as **[[synthetic-chem-enhancements|Shimmer]]**.

Never seen without an ornate porcelain mask decorated with weeping crimson tears, Madame Rouge is an alchemical prodigy and master blackmailer. While Silas the Red commands the brute muscle, Rouge commands the minds, vices, and secrets of Piltover's elite, counting corporate executives, high judges, and noble patricians among her private addicts.

> [!read-aloud]
> Reclining amidst plush crimson velvet cushions behind a screen of perfumed chemical smoke, Madame Rouge holds an ivory cigarette holder glowing with amber embers. Her face is concealed behind a lacquered porcelain mask featuring a painted smile and weeping red tears. At her side rests a polished brass injector filled with luminescent violet Shimmer, her voice purring with dangerous, seductive menace.

## The Web of Vice & Blackmail
- **The Shimmer Monopoly:** Controls the illicit refining laboratories that turn **[[dr-corin-reve|Dr. Corin Reve's]]** chemical runoff into the addictive violet serum flooded into the streets.
- **Noble Addicts:** Hosts clandestine, masked masquerades in the sumps where decadent noble scions from **[[high-patricians-of-piltover|House Valmont and House Beauchamp]]** indulge in forbidden alchemical euphoria, unknowingly signing away their financial leverage to her ledger.
- **Lethal Alchemist:** Wears concealed spray-rings capable of delivering paralytic nerve toxins in a single polite handshake.
