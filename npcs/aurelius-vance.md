## Overview
The supreme master of global finance and High Chancellor of **[[the-iron-bank-of-the-three-rings]]**, sitting on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Known simply across royal courts as "The Sovereign of Coin," Vance is arguably the single most influential civilian on [[rubicon]].

A razor-sharp financial tactician with silver-streaked hair and tailoring of dark samite trimmed with gold leaf, Vance views wars, famines, and imperial alliances strictly as variable interest rates on a grand planetary balance sheet.

> [!read-aloud]
> Wearing an austere velvet coat devoid of gaudy jewels save for a single heavy ring bearing the iron key of the Bank, High Chancellor Aurelius Vance writes in an ivory ledger with a golden quill. His expression is serene and unreadable, his pale eyes studying loan agreements with the dispassionate focus of a predator that has already calculated every possible outcome.

## Personality & Power
- **The Collector:** Famous for his terrifying maxim: *"Kings bleed; empires crumble; but the Bank always collects."*
- **Council Play:** Uses debt leverage to bend sovereign ambassadors to his will, frequently reminding Noxian and Cyrodilic envoys that their armies only march because his bank honors their promissory scrip.
