## Overview
The hereditary ruler of Blackpine Barony in the southern hills of **[[duskwood]]**. During the reign of the Kingdom of **[[nighthaven]]**, Baroness Crowe was one of the few honorable nobles who refused to participate in the dark rites practiced by **[[king-victor-malakor]]** and his corrupt royal court. Forced to watch in helpless horror as her serfs were subjected to arbitrary levies and abductions, she quietly resisted from the shadows.

When Grand General **[[swain|Jericho Swain]]** led the imperial legions into Duskwood, Baroness Crowe secretly allied with Noxus, providing crucial intelligence, regional scouts, and subterranean tunnel maps that allowed the legions to bypass Nighthaven's outer perimeter.

> [!read-aloud]
> A handsome, sharp-eyed woman in her late forties wearing dark green wool riding coats reinforced with polished boiled leather. A hunting bow rests at her shoulder, her hands calloused from years of working alongside foresters. Her warm demeanor carries an underlying iron resolve born from enduring decades of royal oppression.

## Provincial Governor & Imperial Champion
Today, Baroness Crowe is one of the most respected regional administrators in eastern Noxus:
- **Provincial Steward:** Confirmed by Swain as the senior civil governor of Duskwood's outer baronies, she oversees the ironwood logging syndicates and maintains close cooperative ties with **[[lady-eleanor-malakor]]** in the capital.
- **Unapologetic Loyalist:** She is a fervent advocate for the Pax Noxiana, frequently reminding foreign travelers and skeptical diplomats that while outsiders view Noxus as ruthless conquerors, it was Noxian legions that shattered the chains of the Malakor dynasty and gave her people peace.
