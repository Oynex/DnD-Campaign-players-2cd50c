## Overview
The supreme mistress of information in [[piltover]], Madame Helena Vane-DuPont is President of the **Trans-Continental Crystal Network (TCCN)** and the senior diplomat on **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]**. 

Uniquely positioned between both worlds, Helena is the daughter of an old noble house who married into the new corporate tech elite, making her the primary diplomatic bridge—and spymaster—between the corporate directors and **[[high-patricians-of-piltover|The High Patricians]]**. TCCN controls the crystal telegraph relays, transatlantic cable conduits, and cryptographic signal spires that connect western [[aethelgard]] to the outside world.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in high-fashion silk brocade laced with woven gold fiber, Madame Helena Vane-DuPont reclines upon a gilded chaise in her private communications salon. Overhead, brass pneumatic mail tubes hiss and drop sealed canisters containing encrypted telegrams from across the continent. Her hazel eyes are cool and amused, holding the secrets, blackmail, and financial records of every prominent figure in the city.

## Information Warfare & Intelligence
- **The Global Ear:** Through TCCN’s monopoly on crystal-wave communication, Helena intercepts international dispatches from Noxus, Cyrodiil, and the Ring City before they reach anyone else.
- **The Arbiter of Compromise:** Uses confidential financial intelligence to mediate disputes between the corporate council and patrician senators, quietly profiting from insider stock speculation on every compromise.
