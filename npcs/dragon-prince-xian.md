## Overview
The eldest son of the [[celestial-dragon-emperor]] and governor of the desert port metropolis of **[[ryuko]]**. Shifting into a fiery, copper-scaled serpentine dragon, Prince Xian controls the empire's western maritime trade routes, customs bourses, and desert outposts.

> [!read-aloud]
> Draped in golden desert robes and jewel-encrusted chainmail, Prince Xian laughs heartily amidst an entourage of foreign merchant captains and mercenary commanders. Yet beneath his lavish hospitality lies a calculating warlord who views gold as the ultimate weapon of statecraft.

## Succession Ambition
Prince Xian believes that economic wealth and mercenary armies will decide the future of Grand Cathay. He refuses to acknowledge a newborn infant as his sovereign and is using his vast treasury in Ryuko to buy off imperial bureaucrats in [[nan-gau]].
