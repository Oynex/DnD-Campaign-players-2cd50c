## Overview
The eldest son of the [[celestial-dragon-emperor]] and governor of the exotic desert port metropolis of **[[ryuko]]**. Shifting into a colossal, copper-and-gold scaled serpentine dragon, Prince Xian controls the empire's western maritime trade routes, desert caravan networks, and foreign merchant bourses.

He lives like an opulent desert emperor, surrounding himself with lavish luxury, rare oddities, and foreign commanders.

> [!read-aloud]
> Draped in golden desert robes of spun silk and jewel-encrusted chainmail, Prince Xian laughs heartily amidst an entourage of foreign merchant captains and sellsword commanders. A curved scimitar with a ruby pommel rests at his hip. Yet behind his lavish hospitality and booming laughter lies a cunning predator who views gold and exotic commerce as the ultimate weapons of statecraft.

## Succession Ambition & Desert Power
Prince Xian firmly believes that economic wealth and mercenary armies will decide the future of Grand Cathay:
- **The Wealth of Ryuko:** Channels immense customs revenue from the Great Bazaar of [[ryuko]] into his war chest.
- **Mercenary Armies:** Employs thousands of desert dune-skiff raiders, foreign sellswords, and privateers who answer to gold rather than imperial edicts.
- **Defying the Baby Empress:** Disdains his father's decree declaring the infant [[princess-mei-ling]] as heir, actively using his wealth to buy off high bureaucrats in [[nan-gau]] while preparing to march on the capital when his father falls.
