## Overview
The reigning **Phoenix King** of the **Asur (High Elves)**, ruling from the high throne of [[alinor]] alongside the Everqueen. A monarch of fierce martial discipline, strategic intellect, and unyielding dedication to the protection of elven civilization.

> [!read-aloud]
> Crowned with a circlet of white gold and phoenix feathers, the Phoenix King stands before his war council. His silver-chased armor is adorned with sapphire crests, his eyes sharp and calculating as he reviews overseas fleet reports from Skywatch.

## Family & Reign
- **Brother of the High Mage:** Blood brother of **[[high-mage-teclis]]**, the supreme master of the [[crystal-tower]]. Together, the two brothers command the martial and arcane destiny of all High Elves.
- **Foreign Neutrality:** Maintains a strictly neutral posture toward continental squabbles between Cyrodiil and Noxus, directing all imperial Asur forces toward holding the southern seas against the horrors of the ash wastes.
