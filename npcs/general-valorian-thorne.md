## Overview
The supreme battlefield commander and **ultimate leader of all armed forces of [[cyrodil]]**. A battle-tested veteran of fifty campaigns, General Thorne is a legendary military figure renowned for unyielding composure, mastery of heavy infantry shield walls, and encyclopedic knowledge of imperial defensive strategy.

Under the constitution, no noble house is permitted direct military command. Thorne holds the high baton of the legions, answering to the Council while undergoing constant regulatory oversight by **[[lord-meridian-aurelius]]** and House Aurelius.

> [!read-aloud]
> A towering, barrel-chested warlord in heavy steel plate armor bearing the crest of the Imperial High Command. A battered red cloak falls from his broad pauldrons, his granite-jawed face weathered by sun, wind, and old blade scars. He speaks without flourish or pretense, his voice resonating through the war room like a heavy war horn.

## Supreme Command & Internal Security
- **Operational Command:** Directs all standing infantry legions, cavalry cohorts, and garrison armies stationed throughout Cyrodiil.
- **Internal Security:** Partners with **[[lord-sylus-bellator]]** to coordinate military garrisons with the civilian magistracy, safeguarding the imperial highways and rapidly stamping out provincial revolts.
- **Constitutional Dynamic:** While respectful of Lord Aurelius's supervisory authority, Thorne fiercely protects his officers from aristocratic political meddling, insisting that battlefield tactics must be decided by soldiers, not politicians.
