## Overview
The supreme military commander of the **Piltover High Guard** and Grand Commodore of the Harbor Admiralty, representing the martial lineage of House Castillon among **[[high-patricians-of-piltover|The High Patricians]]**. 

Unlike the private corporate mercenaries hired by the Council, Commodore Castillon commands the hereditary, human-staffed municipal guard tasked with defending Piltover's harbor, royal gates, and historical senate halls. A proud, decorated veteran of naval skirmishes along the western channels, Castillon is a fierce traditionalist who violently opposes Director **[[kaelen-voss|Kaelen Voss's]]** schemes to replace living soldiers with unfeeling crystal-powered automaton sentinels.

> [!read-aloud]
> Standing tall upon the seawall ramparts overlooking Piltover’s grand harbor, Commodore Roland Castillon cuts an imposing, statuesque figure in an azure admiral's frock coat trimmed with heavy gold bullion epaulettes. A ceremonial naval sabre hangs at his hip, and his hawk-like gaze sweeps across the water, assessing the readiness of his patrol galleons with unbending naval pride.

## Martial Stand & Ethical Line
- **Defender of Human Valor:** Castillon fiercely refuses to deploy crystal automaton sentries in patrician districts, arguing that machines have no conscience and will turn on their masters the moment a crystal frequency oscillates.
- **Harbor Protection:** Operates the coastal artillery forts and marine boarding cohorts that keep pirates and foreign privateers away from Piltover’s lucrative shipping lines.
- **Tension with Voss:** Locked in an ongoing jurisdictional cold war with Director Kaelen Voss and the private *Aether-Sec* forces over who holds supreme martial jurisdiction in the event of an attack by Noxus.
