## Overview
The legendary, primordial warlord-sorcerer of eastern [[aethelgard]], also feared across ancient texts as the **Iron Tyrant** or **Mordekaiser**. Thousands of years ago, before the realm of [[noxus]] was ever conceived, Sahn-Uzal united the barbarian tribes through savage conquest, butchery, and forbidden necromantic bloodcraft.

To cement his eternal dominion over the mortal plane, Sahn-Uzal raised the colossal obsidian ziggurats of **[[the-immortal-bastion]]**, using the souls of slain kings as mortar. His reign of terror only ended when his closest sorceress-counselor, the Pale Lady **[[leblanc]]**, orchestrated his assassination from behind, binding his soul into the death realm.

> [!read-aloud]
> A colossal, iron-clad shadow towering twelve feet tall, crowned in jagged spikes and gripping a monolithic black iron mace. Within the empty visor of his horned helm, twin burning emerald flames flare with the cold, eternal wrath of a conqueror who refused to submit even to death itself.

## The Shadow Over Modern Noxus
- **The Root of the Noxian Creed:** When Sahn-Uzal was felled, the liberated tribes swore an eternal covenant that eastern Aethelgard would never suffer a single monarch again—giving birth to the meritocratic triumvirate of the **[[noxian-trifarix]]**.
- **The Death Realm Bastion:** Secret texts preserved in the deepest vaults of the Immortal Bastion reveal that Sahn-Uzal did not perish in the void; instead, he conquered the afterlife, turning the souls of the dead into iron bricks to construct an unholy duplicate citadel in the underworld.
- **LeBlanc's Secret Dread:** The ageless sorceress LeBlanc secretly maneuvers empires, legions, and black magic specifically to prevent the Iron King from breaking through the veil and reclaiming his mortal throne.
