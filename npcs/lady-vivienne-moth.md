## Overview
Head of House Moth and the Council's keeper of magical knowledge and imperial archives. Brilliant, highly observant, and seemingly always aware of more than she reveals in open assembly. She has taken a profound, personal interest in the welfare of [[anirudh-b]] since his appearance a year ago.
