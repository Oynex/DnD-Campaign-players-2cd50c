## Overview
The youngest of the three generals commanding Cyrodil's armed forces—a military prodigy and battle-line commander. He is famously one of the only mortals in living history to have survived direct single combat against one of the Ten Roses of [[the-order-of-the-graveyard-rose]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Beneath a frayed traveler's cloak and simple sellsword leathers, his posture is that of a coiled panther. Pale gray eyes dart across the harbor crowd, analyzing choke points and escape routes with instinctive lethal precision.

## Current Mission
Dispatched undercover into [[daggerfall]] in common sellsword garb under secret orders from the Council to investigate the magical catastrophe at the Academy and provide covert reinforcement to the party.
