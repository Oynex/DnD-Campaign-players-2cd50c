## Overview
The architect of Piltover’s military might, Director Kaelen Voss commands **The Aether-Weave Munitions Trust** and oversees municipal defense on **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]**. A former artillery officer who lost both legs to a cannon breach twenty years ago, Voss now walks upon magnificent brass-and-crystal prosthetic limbs of his own design.

Voss is convinced that total war with **[[noxus]]** is inevitable. While other councilors squabble over civilian profits, Voss channels the lion's share of his conglomerate’s research into developing devastating military weaponry—specifically **[[aether-weave-firearms]]** and heavy anti-siege cannon batteries capable of vaporizing Noxian war-drakes at extreme range.

> [!read-aloud]
> Seated rigidly in a high-backed council chair, Director Kaelen Voss cuts a fearsome figure in a high-collared black tunic trimmed with silver wire. He leans upon a cane carved from blued rifle steel, his mechanical prosthetic legs clicking with whisper-quiet hydraulic precision as he shifts. His pale, hawk-like face is utterly devoid of mirth, fixated entirely on troop readiness charts along the eastern passes.

## Defense Policy & The Iron Wall
- **The Arsenal of Rivendel:** Voss's foundries supply the high-yield crystal rifles, automated turrets, and kinetic energy shields that make the fortress pass of **[[rivendel]]** virtually impregnable to conventional assault.
- **Zero Tolerance for Leaks:** Commands the private security division *Aether-Sec*, executing any worker or clerk suspected of smuggling weapons blueprints to **[[the-sump-syndicate]]** or foreign agents.
