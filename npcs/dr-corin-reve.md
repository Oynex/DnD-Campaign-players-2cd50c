## Overview
The brilliant, ethically compromised Chief Alchemist of **Bio-Synthetica** and the health minister on **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]**. Dr. Corin Reve is the world’s foremost pioneer in synthetic biology, cellular regeneration, and chemical stimulants.

While publicly celebrated for developing respirators and antitoxins that allow miners to survive Piltover’s toxic quarries, Dr. Reve is secretly the most compromised man on the Council. Desperate to fund his illegal genetic experiments and find a cure for his own degenerative neural disease, Reve covertly licenses chemical runoff and experimental mutagen formulas to **[[the-sump-syndicate]]**, inadvertently creating the street narcotics and combat mutagens known as **[[synthetic-chem-enhancements|Shimmer]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Wearing a high-collared lab coat of white velvet protected by a stained rubber apron, Dr. Corin Reve adjusts his thick wire-rimmed lenses with trembling fingers. Vials of swirling luminescent solutions click softly inside the fitted pockets of his vest. His pale skin has a sickly, translucent sheen from decades of working over volatile chemical vats, and his bloodshot eyes dart nervously toward the doors at every unexpected sound.

## Double Life & Underworld Ties
- **Corporate Face:** Directs the production of medical supplies, synthetic lubricants, and industrial stimulants that keep Piltover's workforce alive.
- **The Shimmer Secret:** In deep financial and criminal debt to **[[madame-rouge]]** and **[[silas-the-red]]**, Reve secretly provides the chemical catalysts used to distill illicit combat mutagens in the subterranean sumps.
- **Fear of Exposure:** Lives in constant terror that Director Voss or Chief Executive Ferros will discover his treason, which would result in his immediate execution for high corporate treason.
