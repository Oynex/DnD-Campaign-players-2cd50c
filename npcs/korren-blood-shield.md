## Overview
The supreme commander of all chartered private armies on [[rubicon]] and Guildmaster of **[[the-mercenary-brotherhood]]**, sitting on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. A grizzled, heavily scarred human warlord with missing fingers and a jagged scar across his jaw, Korren rose from a penniless pike-mercenary in the border wars to lead the global guild.

Unlike the aristocratic ambassadors around him, Korren has zero patience for delicate court etiquette, keeping a massive bastard sword strapped bare across his back during council sessions.

> [!read-aloud]
> Slumped back in his high-backed council seat with heavy armored boots propped up on the marble ledge, Warmaster Korren Blood-Shield drinks watered rum from a dented iron tankard. His heavy plate armor bears the notched trophies of thirty campaigns, his steel-gray eyes surveying the polished room with the cynical amusement of a killer surrounded by clerks.

## Council Stand
- **The Grunt's Voice:** Fiercely defends the rights and hazard pay of frontline sellswords, ensuring that when nobles wage war, the common warriors who bleed for them are compensated in gold rather than false promises.
