## Overview
The sleek trade consul representing the city-state of **[[piltover]]** and **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]** on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. The charismatic younger brother of Chief Executive **[[vanderbilt-ferros]]**, Alaric is the polished, international face of Piltover’s technological miracle.

Alaric spends council sessions marketing the wonders of [[magitech]], aggressively pitching crystal-powered automated steering engines and energy telegraphs to foreign delegates while lobbying to reduce trade tariffs on Piltover’s manufactured goods.

> [!read-aloud]
> Wearing an immaculate, double-breasted coat of charcoal wool trimmed with brass filigree and sporting a glowing blue crystal lapel pin, Consul Alaric Ferros smiles with dazzling corporate charm. A sleek brass-and-leather briefcase sits at his feet, his fingers deftly adjusting a crystal-powered chronometer on his wrist as he slides lucrative export contracts across the council table.

## Corporate Maneuvering
- **Tech Diplomacy:** Convinces foreign admirals to equip their warships with Piltover's crystal navigation lenses in exchange for long-term supply monopolies.
- **Bitter Guild Feuds:** Locked in constant jurisdictional combat with Master Torvin Stone-Cutter and Grand Magistra Veridiana Hex, who view his synthetic devices as cheap, soul-less industrial garbage.
