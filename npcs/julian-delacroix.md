## Overview
The undisputed leader of **[[high-patricians-of-piltover|The High Patricians]]**, Patriarch Julian Delacroix is the hereditary head of House Delacroix—the oldest and most distinguished noble bloodline in [[piltover]]. Serving as the High Magistrate of the Municipal Senate, Delacroix presides over the city’s constitutional court.

Delacroix despises the "vulgar mechanical tradesmen" of **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]**, viewing their smoky factories and crystal boom as an ugly blight on the pristine coastal heritage of the city. He uses his supreme constitutional authority to veto corporate rezoning petitions, tie up mining patents in multi-year court injunctions, and defend the ancestral privileges of the landed gentry.

> [!read-aloud]
> Standing upon the marble rostrum of the Municipal Senate, Patriarch Julian Delacroix wears a midnight-blue velvet robe edged with white ermine fur. His silver signet ring bears the crest of the sea-gryphon, an emblem older than the founding of the Council. His chiseled, austere face remains haughtily unamused as corporate representatives present their tax relief petitions below.

## Aristocratic Power & Secret Sump Ties
- **The Constitutional Gatekeeper:** Holds hereditary legal vetoes over harbor navigation rights, municipal land sales, and aristocratic estates.
- **Hypocritical Corruption:** While publicly railing against the moral decay and filth of the subterranean sumps, Delacroix covertly launders millions in illicit chem-profits for **[[the-sump-syndicate]]** through private offshore banking trusts, using the underworld to arrange "unfortunate industrial accidents" for corporate rivals.
