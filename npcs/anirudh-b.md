## Overview
A stranger who arrived in [[cyrodil]] one year ago under impossible circumstances. He remembers virtually nothing of his existence prior to waking up in this realm. His very physical presence seems to generate subtle ripples throughout the arcane weave, and his link to escalating magical disturbances across [[tamriel]] appears far from coincidental.

> [!read-aloud]
> A warrior of imposing bearing and unreadable eyes. While he bears the rugged simplicity of a barbarian wanderer, there is an eerie, unnatural calm about him—as if echoes of another universe linger just beneath his skin.

## Background & Connections
- **Party Dynamic:** Travels alongside [[maximillian-redoran]], with whom he shares an unspoken bond of mutual discovery.
- **Council Interest:** Has drawn the intense, personal curiosity of [[lady-vivienne-moth]] of House Moth and several archmages across the continent.
