## Overview
The matriarch of House Valmont and the wealthiest landowner among **[[high-patricians-of-piltover|The High Patricians]]**. Lady Genevieve Valmont commands the sprawling coastal vineyards, ironwood estates, and private shipping berths extending north of **[[piltover]]**.

A master of financial mortgages and municipal politics, Lady Genevieve holds the exclusive monopoly on the cultivation and supply of treated ironwood—the specialized, lightweight timber essential for framing the hulls of **[[krom-and-sons-locomotives|KSL dirigible airships]]**. She uses this monopoly as an economic stranglehold over the corporate council, routinely threatening to halt timber shipments whenever corporate rezoning laws threaten noble property values.

> [!read-aloud]
> Seated in an ornate high-backed chair of carved white oak, Lady Genevieve Valmont wears a dress of pale emerald silk embroidered with gold vine motifs. A fan of peacock feathers and spun silver rests in her ringed fingers. Her sharp green eyes miss nothing, studying municipal contracts with the serene confidence of a woman who owns the very ground the factories stand upon.

## Political Influence & Maneuvers
- **The Timber Stranglehold:** Exercises massive leverage over Baroness Mireille Krom and the locomotive works by dictating timber tariffs for airship keels.
- **Senate Power Broker:** Partners with Patriarch Julian Delacroix to lead the aristocratic conservative voting bloc in the Municipal Senate, blocking corporate expansion into the historic residential wards.
