## Overview
The venerable imperial mandarin representing the **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay]]** on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. A distinguished scholar-statesman from the imperial capital of [[nan-gau]], Minister Chen has represented the Dragon Throne across foreign waters for over sixty years.

Chen embodies the ancient wisdom and enigmatic patience of The First Lands. While western empires squabble over short-term border gains, Chen quietly secures exclusive long-term trade monopolies on silk, jade, and runic curiosities.

> [!read-aloud]
> Robed in cascading green jade silk embroidered with gold celestial clouds and pearl buttons, Minister Chen Long holds a folding paper fan painted with mountain rivers. His long white mustache and kind, crinkled eyes convey a grandfatherly warmth that disarms even the fiercest warlords, yet his gaze misses not a single flicker of deceit in the chamber.

## Subtle Diplomacy
- **Upholding the Covenant:** Because Cathay shares living memory of the southern cataclysm (having built [[hells-gate]] 1,500 years ago), Chen quietly supports Lord Faelorn in maintaining **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]**, faithfully sending alchemical fire and silk sails to the harbor.
- **Succession Anxiety:** Privately watches continental events with mounting concern, knowing that if Grand Cathay fractures into civil war over infant heir [[princess-mei-ling]], the entire eastern trade network will collapse.
