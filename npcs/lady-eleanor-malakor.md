## Overview
The youngest daughter of [[king-victor-malakor]] and the **sole surviving member of the Malakor royal bloodline**. In stark contrast to her sadistic family, Lady Eleanor was born with an innate gentleness and deep empathy that horrified her cruel siblings. As a youth, she abhorred her family's dark sorcery, quietly using her position to smuggle bread and healing draughts to prisoners in the dungeons.

Following the fall of the Kingdom of Nighthaven, she married Grand General **[[swain|Jericho Swain]]** and was appointed the **Caretaker and Civil Governor of Nighthaven City**.

> [!read-aloud]
> A graceful young woman in high-necked dove-gray dresses adorned with silver laurel embroidery walks through the bustling market squares of Nighthaven. Her warm hazel eyes are bright with compassionate focus as she speaks with local orphans and craftsmen. Though her surname once struck terror into the hearts of every citizen, her gentle touch and humble smile now bring reassurance wherever she walks.

## Caretaker of Nighthaven City
While imperial military matters are overseen by the Noxian garrison commander, Eleanor governs the daily civic welfare of the capital city:
- **Rebuilding Trust:** In the early months of her administration, the traumatized populace regarded her with deep suspicion. Through tireless devotion—establishing public apothecaries, subsidizing food stores, and funding relief clinics—the common folk have steadily warmed to her, viewing her as their greatest advocate.
- **Civic Governance:** She limits her authority strictly to the municipal boundaries of Nighthaven City, working cooperatively with regional leaders like **[[baroness-evelyn-crowe]]** to restore prosperity to Duskwood.

## Public Controversy
The high courts of Noxus and foreign nobility were deeply perplexed when Grand General Swain took the last daughter of his bitter enemy as his bride. Spiteful court rumors in the capital whisper that she used forbidden Malakor blood-charms to bewitch the Grand General, though anyone who witnesses them in private sees only mutual devotion and profound respect between two survivors of Nighthaven’s horrors.
