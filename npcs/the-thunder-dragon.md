## Overview
Sister of the [[celestial-dragon-emperor]] and supreme military commander of **[[hells-gate]]**. A colossal serpentine dragon who wields the primordial power of lightning and celestial storms. 

Arguably even stronger in raw martial combat than the Emperor himself, she harbored zero desire to govern or sit on a throne, happily stepping aside so her brother could rule while she took up her true calling: defending civilization at the front lines.

> [!read-aloud]
> Shrouded in crackling blue arcs of lightning, a fierce warrior-maiden in thunder-scale armor surveys the southern ash plains from the ramparts of Hells Gate. A massive storm-spear rests in her calloused grip, her golden eyes blazing with the predatory hunger of an apex dragon eager for battle.

## Creator of the Great Wall
Fifteen hundred years ago during the southern cataclysm, she personally raised the monumental red ramparts of **[[hells-gate]]**, driving back waves of mutated horrors. She cares nothing for the squabbles of her nieces and nephews in the capital, focusing entirely on holding the southern breach.
