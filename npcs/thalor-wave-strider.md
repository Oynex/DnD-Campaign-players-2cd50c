## Overview
The legendary master mariner and Guildmaster of **[[the-oceanic-navigators-league]]**, holding a permanent seat on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Thalor is a half-elf sea captain who has sailed every ocean on [[rubicon]] and survived twelve shipwrecks.

With salt-and-pepper hair whipped by sea spray and eyes that reflect the azure depths of the ocean, Thalor is the most vocal defender of **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]** on the Council, routinely berating short-sighted merchants who want to cut defense funding.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in a heavy sea-wolf fur greatcoat adorned with brass navigation compasses and silver sextants, Grand Navigator Thalor Wave-Strider leans over the council table. His weathered hands are tattooed with ancient star-charts, and a carved whalebone pipe sends fragrant sweet-leaf smoke curling around his sun-darkened brow.

## Passion & Mission
- **Eye on the South:** Has personally witnessed the titanic abyssal horrors rising from the southern trench, treating the containment line as an absolute planetary necessity.
- **Alliance with the Elves:** Maintains close friendship with **[[fleet-admiral-aislyn]]** of Skywatch, coordinating High Elven and human patrol vectors.
