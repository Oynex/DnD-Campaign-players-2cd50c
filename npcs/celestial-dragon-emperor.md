## Overview
The reigning sovereign of the **[[celestial-empire-of-grand-cathay]]**, direct descendant of the primordial Dragon Emperor who ascended to godhood ten thousand years ago as the Third Dragon God alongside Bahamut and Tiamat. 

Though significantly less omnipotent than his deified ancestor, he remains **one of the most powerful entities in existence on [[rubicon]]**, capable of shifting between an ancient serpentine dragon and an august human monarch.

> [!read-aloud]
> Seated upon the Dragon Throne in robes of shimmering gold and jade silk, the Emperor watches his court with timeless, slit-pupiled eyes. Yet beneath the radiant aura of draconic power, faint lines of deep exhaustion and mortal illness betray the quiet frailty of an emperor whose time is running short.

## The Succession Shockwave
Rumors of the Emperor's failing health have ignited an intense succession struggle among his older dragon children. Defying imperial tradition, the Emperor shocked the entire empire by bypassing his ambitious adult offspring and officially declaring his newborn daughter, **[[princess-mei-ling]]**, as the sole heir to the Celestial Throne.
