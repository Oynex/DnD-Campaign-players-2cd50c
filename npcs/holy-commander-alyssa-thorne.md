## Overview
The radiant moral heart of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**, holding the high ecclesiastical rank of Warrior-Bishop under the operational title of **Holy Commander Alyssa Thorne**. Headquartered within the martial cantons of the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]**, she commands the Hospitaler Regiments and Vanguard Paladins across [[tamriel]], widely regarded by common soldiers, sick pilgrims, and peasants as a living saint.

Unlike her cynical peers, Alyssa embodies the true chivalric and protective virtues of **[[orthodox-malorism]]**. Clad in silver plate bearing the sunburst of Prophet Malor and wielding a blessed warhammer whose head glows with dawnlight, she leads defensive sorties to shield frontier hamlets from gnoll bands, goblin raiders, and wilderness horrors.

> [!read-aloud]
> Standing tall upon the chapel ramparts, Holy Commander Alyssa Thorne wears polished silver plate draped with a spotless white cloak. Her auburn hair is tied back in a neat warrior's braid, and her sea-green eyes gaze out across the wounded in the courtyard with profound, sorrowful tenderness. In her right hand she carries a consecrated warhammer whose silver facets reflect the morning sun.

## Bloodline: Niece of General Thorne
Alyssa is the beloved niece of **[[general-valorian-thorne]]**, the Supreme Commander of all armed forces of Cyrodiil.
- **Family Ties:** Her father, Valorian's younger brother, perished in a border skirmish when Alyssa was sixteen. Valorian originally sought to commission his niece into a protected officer role within the imperial legions, hoping to spare her the brutality of war.
- **The Holy Calling:** Refusing aristocratic privilege, Alyssa instead took holy oaths with the Knights of Malor, dedicating her life to defending those who had no legions to protect them. While General Thorne privately worries about the treacherous politics of the church, he maintains immense pride in his niece's tactical brilliance and incorruptible honor.

## Mentorship under Bishop Valen
Recognizing her luminous spirit and fierce natural martial gift, veteran field marshal **[[bishop-valen-of-the-marches]]** took her under his personal wing:
- **The Tactical Pupil:** Valen trained Alyssa in heavy defensive formations, counter-cavalry charges, and how to command thousands of templars under pressure. Her elevation as one of the Five Warrior-Bishops was forced by popular acclaim from the common soldier ranks.
- **The Father She Admires:** Alyssa looks up to Bishop Valen with deep filial devotion, regarding the grizzled old soldier as her mentor and surrogate father. Whenever moral dilemmas arise in council, she trusts Valen's guidance without hesitation, voting alongside him on every decree.
- **Untouched by the Dirt:** Because Valen quietly handles the ugly administrative compromises, blackmail, and political maneuvering of the high church, Alyssa's radiant idealism remains untarnished. She genuinely believes the heavy church taxes are spent entirely on charitable aid, soldier rations, and border fortifications.
- **Untouchable by Zealots:** Even the terrifying [[inquisitor-general-malachai]] refrains from targeting her hospital cloisters; his deep martial respect for Valen keeps the inquisitors at a respectful distance from Alyssa's work.
- **Respect Across the Schism:** Even the hardened northern raiders of **[[new-malors|Skyrim]]** know her name; on more than one occasion, Nordic reavers have spared captured hospital wagons upon learning they flew Holy Commander Thorne's personal heraldry.
