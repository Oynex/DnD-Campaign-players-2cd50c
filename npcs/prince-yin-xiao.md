## Overview
The third son of the [[celestial-dragon-emperor]]. Known universally throughout Grand Cathay as the **Black Sheep of the Dragon Court**, Prince Yin-Xiao is widely considered a hopeless, lazy embarrassment to the imperial bloodline who spends his days lounging in wine parlors and gambling dens in [[nan-gau]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Slumped lazily in a lacquered bamboo chair with his collar undone and a porcelain jug of plum wine dangling from his fingertips, Prince Yin-Xiao chuckles at a poet's bawdy verse. His older siblings pass by with curled lips and cold disdain, treating him as little more than a harmless decorative fixture of the court.

## Public Reputation
Unlike his fiercely ambitious brothers and sister who command provinces and armies, Yin-Xiao has never held a government post, commanded a legion, or expressed the slightest interest in the throne. The entire court dismisses him as a harmless, pampered drunkard.
