## Overview
The fierce military war-envoy representing **[[noxus]]** on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Unlike the silk-robed diplomats of other realms, Centurion Drakos is a scarred, battle-tested frontline veteran of the eastern campaigns, appointed directly by Grand General **[[darius]]** to represent imperial interests.

Drakos openly scoffs at the council’s endless parliamentary debates and velvet-gloved politics, treating his ambassadorial seat like a conquered forward command trench.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in spiked black iron armor bearing the crimson crest of Noxus and a battered scarlet cloak, Centurion Drakos Iron-Fang sits rigidly in his council chair. His weathered face is carved with brutal dueling scars, and his calloused hands rest upon the pommel of a heavy iron war-cleaver leaning against his knees, glaring at foreign diplomats with cold imperial disdain.

## Imperial Agenda
- **Contempt for the Southern Myth:** Loudly declares that Noxus will not bleed gold for "fairy tales of southern sea monsters" while wars must be won on the mainland, boldly justifying imperial subsidy cuts.
- **Black-Market Procurement:** Covertly buys high-grade weapons, rare poisons from the Alchemical Conclave, and smuggled crystal munitions to ship back to the armies preparing to breach [[rivendel]].
