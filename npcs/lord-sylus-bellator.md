## Overview
Head of House Bellator and Chief Justice of the Imperial Magistracy. Uncompromising, razor-sharp, and incorruptible, Lord Sylus views the written law not as a guideline, but as the fragile iron cage preventing mortal society from descending into barbaric anarchy. 

He oversees the imperial magistracy, the high appellate tribunals, and the central penal bastions of Cyrodiil.

> [!read-aloud]
> Wearing tailored robes of stark charcoal wool trimmed in white ermine, Lord Sylus Bellator sits behind stacks of parchment warrants and legal treatises. A silver balance scale hangs behind his high-backed bench. When he speaks, his tone is entirely devoid of warmth or wrath—only the chilling, absolute certainty of the law.

## Domestic Security & The Magistracy
In addition to presiding over the courts, Lord Sylus collaborates closely with **General [[general-valorian-thorne]]** on internal state security:
- **Civilian Watch:** Directs the municipal watch and investigative magistrates who patrol Cyrodiil's cities.
- **Counter-Subversion:** Deeply suspicious of external agitators, Sylus actively investigates smuggling cartels, rogue mages fleeing Daggerfall, and foreign spies from Noxus attempting to infiltrate the imperial capital.
