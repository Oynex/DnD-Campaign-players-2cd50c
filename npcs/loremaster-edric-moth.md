## Overview
Senior historian and archivist of the [[order-of-the-ethereal-sigil]], and a distant cousin of [[lady-vivienne-moth]]. Regarded as the moral conscience of the Academy, Edric firmly argues that certain arcane secrets were buried for the safety of mortal kind and must remain undisturbed.
