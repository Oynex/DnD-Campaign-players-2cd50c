## Overview
The newborn daughter of the [[celestial-dragon-emperor]] and his favored human imperial concubine. Though only an infant in her swaddling cradle, she was officially declared by the Emperor as the **Crown Princess and rightful heir to the Celestial Throne of Grand Cathay**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Nestled within a cradle carved from a single piece of sacred white jade and draped in celestial golden silks, an infant girl sleeps serenely. Above her crib, delicate serpentine motes of golden dragon-light swirl through the air, reacting to her soft, rhythmic breathing.

## The Imperial Target
Her birth and sudden elevation shocked the empire, throwing the succession into chaos. Unable to speak or defend herself, the infant is the central target of covert plots orchestrated by her three elder half-siblings, guarded day and night by the imperial guard within the inner sanctum of [[nan-gau]].
