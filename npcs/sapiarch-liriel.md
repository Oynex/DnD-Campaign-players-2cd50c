## Overview
Supreme authority on planar magic, spatial geometries, and the cosmic veil within the [[sapiarchs-of-the-crystal-tower]]. Cool and deeply analytical, Liriel believes any cosmic anomaly can be deciphered and contained through disciplined mathematics and harmonic spellcraft.

## Research & Concerns
She spends her days in the upper observatories of the [[crystal-tower]], monitoring the thinning dimensional boundaries between the mortal world and the outer planes. The unnatural maelstrom surrounding [[vespers-rest]] and the resonant breach at [[daggerfall]] have heightened her concern that the planar veil is failing.
