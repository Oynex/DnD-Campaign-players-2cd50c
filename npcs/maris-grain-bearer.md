## Overview
The matriarch of global food security and Guildmaster of **[[the-agrarian-grain-cartel]]**, sitting on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Known in diplomatic circles as "The Ceres of the Isles," Lady Maris is a stout, sharp-eyed human woman in her late fifties who grew up on the wheat plains of western Aethelgard.

She treats kings and generals like squabbling farmhands, reminding them with maternal bluntness that a warrior can march without plate armor, but dies in twelve days without her grain sacks.

> [!read-aloud]
> Dressed in elegant homespun wool trimmed with braided amber wheat stalks, Lady Maris Grain-Bearer reviews shipping manifests with razor-sharp focus. Her calloused hands speak to a youth spent behind a plow, and her warm, motherly demeanor hardens into unyielding flint whenever an ambassador threatens to seize grain shipments for an unprovoked war.

## Political Stance
- **Food as Peace:** Has single-handedly stopped two major continental invasions by threatening to place total grain embargoes on the aggressor nations, forcing them to the negotiating table.
