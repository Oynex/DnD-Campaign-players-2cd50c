## Overview
The High Elven Plenipotentiary representing **[[summerset-isle]]** on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Appointed directly by [[alith-anar|Phoenix King Alith Anar]] and the Everqueen, Lord Faelorn is an ancient, five-hundred-year-old Asur noble diplomat who has served in the Ring City for over a century.

Faelorn views his assignment not as an ambassadorial luxury, but as a grueling diplomatic siege. He is fiercely dedicated to sustaining **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]**, relentlessly shaming human and Noxian ambassadors who treat the southern cataclysm as an irrelevant fable.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in high-collared alabaster robes trimmed with sapphire phoenix embroidery and polished silver mail, Lord Faelorn Sun-Treader stands with immaculate, statuesque posture. In his slender hands he holds a stack of delinquent naval balance sheets, his pale golden eyes glaring down the council table at the mainland ambassadors with cold, aristocratic disdain.

## Diplomatic Agenda
- **The Containment Line:** Constantly demands that the Council enforce maritime embargoes on any nation that fails to remit its mandated warships and coin to the Aegis Fleet.
- **Alliance with Thalor:** Regularly votes with Grand Navigator Thalor Wave-Strider to allocate emergency repair funds for southern patrol galleons.
