## Overview
Head of House Sterling and Grand Chancellor of the Imperial Treasury. Charming, impeccably tailored, and widely rumored to be the wealthiest mortal on the continent of [[tamriel]]. 

Cedric treats diplomacy, politics, and warfare strictly as mathematical equations of profit and loss. He maintains that while generals spill blood to claim territory, it is gold and trade ledgers that ultimately determine who rules.

> [!read-aloud]
> Lounging comfortably in a gilded armchair lined with imported Ring City velvet, Merchant-Prince Cedric Sterling turns a pristine gold sovereign across his manicured knuckles. A pleasant, disarming smile graces his handsome features, but his sharp amber eyes track every nuance of expression, calculating the exact value of every person in the room.

## Economic Hegemony
- **The Imperial Mint:** Controls the state banking reserves, currency valuation, and taxation across all Cyrodiil provinces.
- **Overseas Trade Concessions:** Holds lucrative monopolistic shipping pacts with the great mercantile syndicates of **[[the-ring-city]]**, ensuring Cyrodiil remains the primary trading crossroads of the western hemisphere.
- **Council Balancing Act:** Constantly battles with **[[thaddeus-corvan]]** over the immense costs of steam locomotives and with **[[lord-meridian-aurelius]]** over defense budgets, urging negotiated treaties over costly military adventures.
