## Overview
A legendary serpentine Loong dragon from the dawn era of [[cathay]], remembered in ancient mythology as the greatest master of arcane magic among all early dragonkind. 

Mortal historians and scholars across [[rubicon]] believe the Archmage Dragon perished thousands of years ago alongside the first generation of primordial dragons. He is best known for the famous mythological prank that created the purple crystalline valley of **[[imladris]]** at the base of Mount Nurnen.

> [!read-aloud]
> Ancient temple scrolls in Grand Cathay depict a colossal serpentine dragon whose amethyst and starlight scales shimmer with impossible magic. Long whiskers drift among the clouds like ribbons of celestial fire, his golden eyes filled with the brilliant, unpredictable spark of a primordial trickster.

## The Myth of the Great Prank
According to northern folklore, thousands of years ago the dragon flew into northern [[aethelgard]] and encountered the founding clans of [[erebor]]:
- **The Stone and the Spark:** Finding that the stone-loving dwarves despised all forms of magic, the dragon amused himself by conjuring the surreal, crystal-studded magical wonderland of [[imladris]] directly over Erebor's front threshold.
- **The Dwarven Secret:** While most races believe the prank was the end of the story and that the dragon vanished into extinction, ancient dwarven song-smiths privately preserve oral histories recording that the dragon and the grumpy mountain kings eventually developed an unexpected, lasting respect for one another.
