## Overview
The diplomatic and financial master of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**, High Prelate Lucian Vane presides over the Canton of the Treasury within the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]**. While he holds the title of Warrior-Bishop and wears gilded cerulean plate armor during ceremonial processions, Vane rarely wields a blade outside the duelists' ring, fighting his wars instead with gold, embargoes, and confidential blackmail.

A scion of an old Cyrodilic mercantile house, Lucian views **[[orthodox-malorism]]** not merely as a faith, but as the most formidable and lucrative enterprise on the face of [[tamriel]]. Under his tenure, the collection of the imperial church tithe has doubled, transforming Vivec into an economic superpower capable of financing entire mercenary legions.

> [!read-aloud]
> Robed in cascading vestments of deep blue samite lined with ermine and spun-gold embroidery, High Prelate Lucian Vane smiles with easy aristocratic grace. Rings of sapphire and carved amber adorn every finger of his soft, manicured hands, and the faint scent of imported spiced cedar trails in his wake.

## Background & Rise to Power
Born into wealth as the second son of a prominent shipping baron, Lucian was placed in the church at eighteen to secure his family's tax exemptions. Discovering a profound talent for canon law and creative bookkeeping, he quickly uncovered vast inefficiencies in the church's tithe routes. 

By reorganizing the grain shipments and establishing licensed Church Indulgences—allowing wealthy nobles to purchase confidential spiritual dispensations to clear familial scandals—Lucian brought millions in gold into the High Temple coffers. He was elevated to the council of Five Warrior-Bishops at the unprecedentedly young age of thirty-six.

## The Frustrated Politician: Outvoted & Outmaneuvered
While Lucian projects absolute control over the high church's wealth, he harbors a burning, tightly concealed resentment toward the council chamber:
- **Played by the Old Soldier:** Lucian constantly seeks to divert holy knights toward securing profitable trade routes and extorting rival merchant houses, but he is routinely played and checkmated by **[[bishop-valen-of-the-marches]]**. The old veteran sees through every one of Lucian's gilded schemes and neutralizes them with deceptive battlefield simplicity.
- **The Permanent Minority:** Lucian can **never** outvote Valen on council policy. **[[holy-commander-alyssa-thorne]]** trusts Valen implicitly and votes with him, while **[[inquisitor-general-malachai]]** votes with Valen out of martial respect for an old trench warrior. Thus, Valen commands an unbeatable 3-vote bloc, forcing Lucian to concede funding for armies, fortifications, and hospital cloisters he would rather pocket.
- **Balthazar’s Amusement:** [[arch-justiciar-balthazar-karr]] remains indifferent to Lucian's protests, viewing Valen's political domination of the council as an elegant, lawful stabilizer that keeps the treasury from imploding into open scandal.
