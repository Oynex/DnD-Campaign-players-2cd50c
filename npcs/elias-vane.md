## Overview
The supreme magistrate of international law and Guildmaster of **[[the-high-juridical-synod]]**, sitting on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Known as "The Inflexible Scale," Elias Vane is a severe, ascetic human jurist who views emotions, political bribery, and nationalist fervor as contemptible human weaknesses.

A distant cousin to Cyrodiil's political family, Elias severed all national loyalties forty years ago to take the Vow of Blind Justice, dedicating his life to the absolute letter of international statutory law.

> [!read-aloud]
> Wearing robes of unadorned black silk with an immaculate white linen cowl, Chief Justiciar Elias Vane sits perfectly erect in his council throne. His gaunt face is as rigid as carved marble, his quartz-tinted spectacles reflecting the ambient candlelight as his quill meticulously notes every procedural deviation in council debate.

## Absolute Neutrality
- **The Stone Wall:** Unbribable and fearless. He has ruled against Cyrodiil, Noxus, and Grand Cathay with equal, unblinking severity, relying on the combined enforcement power of the remaining guilds to compel compliance.
