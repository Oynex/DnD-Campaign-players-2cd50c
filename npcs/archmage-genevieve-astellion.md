## Overview
High Magister of [[daggerfall]] and Archmage-Chancellor of the **[[academy-of-daggerfall]]**. A brilliant, fiercely independent arcane prodigy who rose to supreme leadership of humanity's greatest magical city through sheer intellectual supremacy and iron willpower.

She is the younger sister of **[[lady-vivienne-astellion]]**, the head of House Astellion on the Council of Cyrodiil. While her sister operates through courtly diplomacy and archival intelligence in the Imperial City, Genevieve rules Daggerfall directly from the front lines of arcane governance.

> [!read-aloud]
> Dressed in tailored high-collared azure silks adorned with intricate silver leyline embroidery, Archmage Genevieve Astellion stands before a wall of floating scrying mirrors in the High Observatory. Floating crystal styluses frantically record telemetry around her, her sharp, aristocratic features etched with exhaustion as she analyzes planar fractures across the western horizon.

## A City on the Brink
Genevieve finds herself caught in an impossible, multi-front crisis:
- **Impending Mage Rebellion:** She recognizes that institutional elitism and corruption within the Academy have pushed common mages and citizens to the brink of armed insurrection, yet moving against her conservative faculty risks a civil war within the high towers.
- **The Cracking Spire:** The great Resonance Tower is fracturing from catastrophic leyline drain. Genevieve is desperately hunting for the cause of the siphon, oblivious that [[piltover]]'s magitech mines are draining the same conduit.
- **Family Friction:** She resists interference from her sister Vivienne and the Council of Cyrodiil, fiercely guarding Daggerfall's traditional academic sovereignty.
