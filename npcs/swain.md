## Overview
The supreme visionary ruler of [[noxus]], Grand General Jericho Swain presides over the **[[noxian-trifarix|Noxian Trifarix]]**, embodying the principle of **Vision**. 

Unlike frontline warriors who rely on cold steel, Swain is a peerless **high sorcerer** and master planner. A visionary political reformer who replaced the decadent nobility with a ruthless meritocracy, Swain possesses supernatural foresight granted by an ancient demonic entity bound into his flesh, manifesting as an eerie murder of six-eyed ravens that observe the entire continent.

> [!read-aloud]
> Draped in a heavy military greatcoat with high black-iron pauldrons, Grand General Swain stands beside the high council map in the Immortal Bastion. A glowing, demonic crimson radiance smolders within his severed demonic left arm, while shadowy black ravens perch quietly on the vaulted rafters above him. His cold gray eyes gaze through parchment and stone, analyzing futures visible only to him.

## The Conquest of Nighthaven & The Demon's True Name
The defining crucible of Swain's rise occurred during the imperial invasion of the Kingdom of **[[nighthaven]]**:
- **Meticulous Preparation:** Knowing the Malakor royal family was backed by an ancient entity of shadow, Swain used the vast archives and intelligence networks of Noxus to research the fiend. Through years of clandestine occult study, he discovered the **True Name of the demon: Raum**, preparing specialized counter-binding wards before launching the campaign.
- **The Sorcerous Duel:** In the royal throne room, Swain engaged **[[king-victor-malakor]]** in an apocalyptic duel of high sorcery. Eldritch barriers shattered and arcane lightning illuminated the gloom. In his dying counter-strike, King Victor unleashed a dark curse that severed and disintegrated Swain's left arm at the shoulder before Swain's retaliatory spell annihilated the tyrant.
- **Subjugating the Fiend:** As Swain knelt wounded, the demon Raum materialized from the shadows to devour his soul. But Swain did not falter. Speaking the demon's True Name, Swain activated the prepared wards, turning the possession inward, bending the demon's will to his own, and binding the fiend into his severed stump. The entity was forced to regenerate his missing limb as a crimson claw of raw demonic power.

## Marriage & The Throne of Nighthaven
Following the conquest, Swain astonished the empire by marrying **[[lady-eleanor-malakor]]**, the sole surviving child of the Malakor dynasty, appointing her as the civil Caretaker of Nighthaven City. 

While court gossips and imperial rivals whisper that Swain was bewitched or compromised by the dark bloodline, Swain dismisses the rumors with cold indifference, knowing that Eleanor’s gentle rule has cemented the people's fanatical loyalty to Noxus.
