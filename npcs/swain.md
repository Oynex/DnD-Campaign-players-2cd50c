## Overview
The supreme visionary ruler of [[noxus]], Grand General Jericho Swain presides over the **[[noxian-trifarix|Noxian Trifarix]]**, embodying the principle of **Vision**. 

A master strategist and ruthless political reformer who seized power from the decadent aristocracy, Swain possesses supernatural foresight granted by a demonic pact with an ancient entity of secrets, manifesting as a murder of eerie, six-eyed ravens that observe the entire continent.

> [!read-aloud]
> Draped in a heavy military greatcoat with high black-iron pauldrons, Grand General Swain stands beside the high council map in the Immortal Bastion. A glowing, demonic crimson radiance smolders within his severed demonic left arm, while shadowy black ravens perch quietly on the vaulted rafters above him.

## Grand Strategy
While Darius leads the front lines and the common soldiers fight for land, Swain looks far beyond simple conquest. Through his demonic foresight, Swain is aware of the ancient cosmic seals weakening across [[rubicon]], believing that only a ruthlessly unified global empire can survive the coming planar storm.
