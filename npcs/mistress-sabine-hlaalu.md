## Overview
Master enchanter of the [[order-of-the-ethereal-sigil]]. Sabine advises on funding, magical procurement, and research monetization. Her bespoke enchanted foci and luxury magitech curios support both noble patrons and academy treasuries, maintaining close commercial relationships with House Sterling.
