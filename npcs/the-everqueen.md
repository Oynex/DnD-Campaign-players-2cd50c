## Overview
The spiritual co-monarch of **[[summerset-isle]]**, reigning from the High Palace of **[[alinor]]** alongside **[[alith-anar|Phoenix King Alith Anar]]**. As the living embodiment of elven spiritual continuity, the Everqueen is revered across all High Elven lands as the mother of the realm and the living custodian of the **[[kynarism|Unbroken Chain]]**.

While the Phoenix King directs military strategy, naval cordons, and foreign statecraft, the Everqueen oversees the cultural, ethical, and spiritual health of elven civilization. She presides over the Presentation of the Parapet in the Hall of Continuance, personally reviewing the ancestral masterworks that earn inscription into the Great Ledger of Ancestors.

> [!read-aloud]
> Robed in cascading gossamer silks the color of morning sea-foam, the Everqueen sits upon a living throne woven from silverwood roots and blossoming white lotus lilies. A delicate crown of crystallized starlight rests upon her flowing golden hair, her violet eyes radiating an ancient, timeless compassion that calms the fiercest elven warlords.

## Spiritual & Sovereign Role
- **The Sacred Dual Sovereignty:** Together with Phoenix King Alith Anar, she balances the martial steel of Skywatch with the moral and cultural purity of Alinor.
- **Heart of Kynarism:** In elven theology, the Everqueen is the living bridge connecting the ancestors of four millennia ago to the unborn generations of the future.
- **The Silent Gilded Weight:** Deeply mourns the growing complacency of the mainland races regarding the **[[waning-of-the-containment-fleet]]**, privately consulting with High Mage [[high-mage-teclis]] on whether Summerset must prepare to face the southern ash cataclysm alone.
