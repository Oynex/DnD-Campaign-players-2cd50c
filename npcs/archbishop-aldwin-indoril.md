## Overview
Head of House Indoril and the supreme spiritual leader of [[cyrodil]]. Aged, kind-eyed, and unsettlingly perceptive. His unshakeable faith and serene wisdom have provided moral guidance to the Council through five centuries of post-imperial governance.
