## Overview
Head of House Indoril and the supreme spiritual leader of [[cyrodil]]. Aged, white-bearded, and carrying himself with quiet grace, Archbishop Aldwin represents the moral conscience of the Council. 

While other lords squabble over taxes and fortifications, Archbishop Aldwin oversees the vast network of cathedrals, sacred shrines, hospices, and civil charity across the realm, serving as the high diplomatic liaison to the supreme ecclesiastical authorities of **[[orthodox-malorism]]** in the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Robed in ivory vestments woven with gold sun-threads, Archbishop Aldwin Indoril leans gently upon a cedar pastoral staff. Soft candlelight reflects from his silver brow, his kind, time-etched eyes conveying an unsettling depth of spiritual insight that cuts straight through aristocratic deceit.

## Spiritual Leadership & Holy City Ties
- **Primate of Orthodox Malorism:** As the primary representative of **[[orthodox-malorism]]** within the capital, he coordinates the church's charitable breadlines and temple festivals that keep the common populace peaceful and faithful.
- **Navigating the Knights of Malor:** Maintains delicate relations with **[[the-knights-of-malor]]** and the Five Warrior-Bishops, curbing the excesses of fanatical inquisitors like Bishop Malachai while turning a discreet, reluctant blind eye to the political wheeling-and-dealing of corrupt prelates in Vivec to preserve national stability.
- **Moral Bulwark:** Often acts as the peacemaker in heated Council disputes, reminding the six houses that power without compassion is the very tyranny they threw into the sea five centuries ago.
- **The Northern Schism:** Privately mourns the ancient split with the **[[new-malors]]**, acknowledging that Prophet Malor was indeed born of northern blood and believing that if the Orthodox church shed its obsession with gold, brother could embrace brother across Tamriel once more.
