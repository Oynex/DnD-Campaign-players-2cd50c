## Overview
The supreme spymaster of [[rubicon]] and Guildmaster of **[[the-unseen-syndicate]]**, sitting on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Known in diplomatic circles as "The Blind Broker," Mercer is an elderly, blind human information merchant who lost his sight decades ago to a poisoned needle.

Despite having sightless milky eyes, Mercer navigates the marble council rotunda with uncanny precision, guided by auditory clicks and the quiet rustle of parchment. He is said to remember over ten thousand ciphers in his head.

> [!read-aloud]
> Draped in a high-collared coat of dark charcoal wool devoid of any insignia, Master Silas Mercer sits perfectly motionless. A velvet blindfold covers his eyes, his acute ears twitching toward the slightest catch of breath from across the council chamber. A faint, gentle smile touches his thin lips as an ambassador speaks, already knowing the three lies contained within the envoy's opening sentence.

## Power & Blackmail
- **The Velvet Lever:** Rarely threatens or shouts; he simply slides a small sealed envelope across the table containing a private bank account slip, an illegitimate heir's birth certificate, or an assassination order.
- **Frustration with the First Seat:** Frustrated that he has never been able to pierce the veil surrounding the mysterious First Seat of the Council, completely oblivious that the quiet nun who changes his tea is an agent of the Lady Rose.
