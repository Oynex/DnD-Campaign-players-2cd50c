## Overview
The supreme mistress of alchemy and Guildmaster of **[[the-alchemical-conclave]]**, holding a permanent seat on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Known in diplomatic circles as "The Chem-Matriarch," Veridiana is a brilliant, enigmatic human alchemist whose youthful appearance is sustained through proprietary longevity elixirs.

Her veins occasionally glow with faint emerald light beneath her pale skin, and she carries a reputation for being immune to every known biological poison in the mortal world.

> [!read-aloud]
> Draped in sweeping robes of viridian velvet embroidered with silver mandrake roots, Grand Magistra Veridiana Hex cradles a crystal vial of swirling, phosphorescent tincture. Her fingertips are permanently stained with saffron and vitriol, her pale violet eyes gleaming with unnerving, razor-sharp intellect.

## Political Stance
- **Anti-Magitech Crusader:** Despises Piltover’s synthetic chem-substitutes, publicly accusing Consul Alaric Ferros of poisoning the western working class with toxic industrial stimulants.
- **Council Intrigues:** Sells confidential antidotes and truth serums to fellow councilors, using medical dependency as invisible political leverage.
