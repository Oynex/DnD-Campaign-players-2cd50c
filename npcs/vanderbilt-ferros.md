## Overview
The de facto paramount director of **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]** and Chief Executive of **Aethelgard Magitech-Refining Ltd.** Vanderbilt Ferros is the undisputed titan of Piltover's industrial economy, controlling the primary crystal dredging cranes and chemical distillation plants surrounding the Great Crater basin.

A cold, calculating industrial autocrat with silvered hair and tailored pinstripe coats, Ferros treats all of Aethelgard as a balance sheet. He views magic not as mysticism, but as raw caloric energy waiting to be refined, boxed, patented, and sold at a 400% markup.

> [!read-aloud]
> Standing behind a massive mahogany desk inlaid with brass leylines, Vanderbilt Ferros checks a gold pocket watch powered by a thrumming miniature crystal cell. His silver monocle magnifies a cool, calculating gray eye that dissects visitors in seconds. When he speaks, his tone is quiet and utterly devoid of warmth, like the click of a precision clockwork escapement.

## Industrial Empire & The Hidden Crisis
- **The Monopoly:** Under Ferros's direction, Aethelgard Magitech-Refining holds exclusive municipal rights to Grade-A and Grade-B crystal processing under **[[crystal-refinement]]**.
- **Quarry Panic:** Ferros is secretly terrified by the accelerating harmonic fractures in the crater basin. While publicly reassuring investors, he has secretly tripled private guard patrols around Crater Sector Four, where reality distortions recently dissolved twelve miners.
- **Council Dominance:** Operates as the dominant voice against **[[high-patricians-of-piltover|The High Patricians]]**, using energy tariffs and municipal utility cutoffs to force the old nobility into compliance.
