## Overview
The imperial ambassador representing **[[cyrodil]]** on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. A distinguished noblewoman from the premier ruling dynasty of Cyrodiil (cousin to Council Regent **[[lord-meridian-aurelius]]**), Countess Valeria is a brilliant, velvet-tongued diplomat skilled in navigating multi-empire negotiations.

Valeria’s primary mandate from the Council of Cyrodiil is to protect imperial commerce and discreetly negotiate reductions in Cyrodiil’s massive financial commitments to **[[the-grand-aegis-fleet]]**, arguing that Cyrodiil already bears the heavy burden of garrisoning continental borders against Noxus.

> [!read-aloud]
> Wearing an exquisite gown of crimson silk layered over an ivory velvet bodice adorned with the golden imperial gryphon, Countess Valeria Aurelius smiles with serene aristocratic confidence. A delicate gold circlet clasps her dark hair, her warm hazel eyes calculating every diplomatic opening while gracefully parrying demands from the High Elven delegation.

## Political Balancing Act
- **Reducing Fleet Tithes:** Secretly partners with Piltover to argue that modern magitech defensive barriers should reduce the need for expensive timber war galleons in the south.
- **Defending Trade Lines:** Fights to keep the shipping lanes between Cyrodiil and the Ring City exempt from heavy guild tariffs.
