## Overview
The **highest military figure** among the **Asur (High Elves)**, commander of the naval super-fortress of **[[sky-watch]]**, and supreme admiral of the High Elven armada. 

> [!read-aloud]
> Towering and clad in sea-scale silver plate draped in an ocean-blue admiral's cape, Fleet Admiral Aislyn surveys the vast harbor of Skywatch. His hand rests on the hilt of an ancient naval cutlass, his gaze sweeping with steely authority across the oceanic charts.

## The Dual Oceanic Fronts
Under Aislyn's iron command, the Asur fleet carries the defensive burden of the known world on two fronts:
- **The Southern Breach:** Holding back the primordial horrors emerging from the southern ash wastes, furious with continental empires for abandoning the **[[waning-of-the-containment-fleet]]**.
- **The Northern Quarantine:** Enforcing the absolute worldwide embargo on **[[the-serpentine-abyss]]**, commanding search-and-rescue patrol squadrons that routinely pull battered ships and half-mad privateers away from the mists guarding **[[the-world-tree]]**.
