## Overview
An ancient, primordial demon of secrets and forbidden knowledge, bound into the mortal realm for centuries beneath the gothic spires of **[[nighthaven]]** in [[duskwood]]. Known in occult tomes as the Raven Lord, Raum feasts upon the hidden guilts, illicit crimes, and suppressed truths of mortal minds.

For generations, the corrupt dark sorcerer dynasty of **[[king-victor-malakor]]** trafficked with Raum, sacrificing captured youths to extract demonic power. When Grand General **[[swain|Jericho Swain]]** led the assault on Nighthaven, losing his arm in the magical duel with King Victor, the demon attempted to possess the crippled Noxian general. However, through sheer willpower and having researched Raum's **True Name**, Swain turned the binding back upon the entity, trapping Raum inside his severed limb and forging his legendary demonic hand.

> [!read-aloud]
> Within the glowing crimson talons of Swain's demonic arm, shadows writhe and warp into the spectral silhouettes of countless black ravens. When the talons flex, a faint, discordant chorus of spectral whispers echoes in the air—the stolen secrets of dying kings and betrayed emperors, murmuring in terrified agony beneath the demon's claws.

## The Demonic Symphony
- **The Source of Swain's Foresight:** Swain utilizes Raum's demonic sight to perceive events hundreds of leagues away, peering through the eyes of carrion ravens across Aethelgard to out-strategize enemy armies.
- **The Host's Struggle:** Raum is not a willing servant; the entity constantly tests Swain's psychic defenses, whispering insidious temptations in the dead of night, waiting for the slightest moment of mortal exhaustion to tear free and devour the Grand General's soul.
