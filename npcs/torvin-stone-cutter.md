## Overview
The venerable dwarven patriarch and Guildmaster of **[[the-international-artificers-guild]]**, sitting on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. With a braided iron-gray beard clasped in mithril rings and shoulders as broad as a mountain wagon, Torvin is revered as "The Great Builder."

Having directed the reconstruction of the Ring City's Outer Seawall after the Kraken Storm three centuries ago, Torvin views modern clockwork shortcuts with vocal contempt, demanding that all civilization be built on solid, rune-chiseled stone.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in heavy iron-scale mail over thick leather smith's robes, Master Torvin Stone-Cutter slams his scarred, granite-calloused fist onto the council table. A heavy war-mallet carved from obsidian rests beside his chair, and his flinty eyes glare with uncompromising dwarven stubbornness at any ambassador who suggests cutting masonry repair budgets.

## Council Role
- **Unshakable Anchor:** Utterly immune to political flattery or financial bribes; votes strictly on structural integrity, civic safety, and long-term durability.
- **Dwarven Homeland Ties:** Maintains close ties with the smiths of **[[khazad-dum]]**, coordinating continental ore shipments.
