## Overview
Eldest son and heir apparent of House Redoran. Rather than purely following his father's mechanical and engineering path, Maximillian was touched by otherworldly forces beyond conventional mortal understanding, becoming a warlock. He bears the heavy mantle of noble expectations while concealing the unsettling nature of his patron.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in high-collared Cyrodilian silks trimmed with subtle bronze redoran filigree, Maximillian carries himself with aristocratic poise. Yet a faint, otherworldly luminescence occasionally dances at the edges of his fingertips when his focus drifts.

## Relationships & Secrets
- **Family Ties:** Son of Councilor [[thaddeus-redoran]], who suspects his son's powers and may hold vital clues regarding the patron.
- **Companion:** Travels with [[anirudh-b]], seeking to understand the mysterious connection between Anirudh's arrival and the patron's whispers.
