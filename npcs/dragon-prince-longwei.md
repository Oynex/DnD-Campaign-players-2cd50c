## Overview
The second son of the [[celestial-dragon-emperor]] and governor of the mystical floating mountain realm of the **[[yunshan-isles]]**. In his dragon form, Longwei is a tempestuous, azure-scaled serpentine dragon commanding the celestial winds.

> [!read-aloud]
> Standing at the edge of a floating mountain terrace high above the clouds, Prince Longwei wears azure dragon-scale battle plate. His twin blades crackle with wind-magic, his gaze sweeping across the imperial sky-ships anchored to the floating peaks below.

## Military Threat
Longwei disdains the political galas of Princess Zhao and the mercantile bribes of Prince Xian, relying instead on raw martial force and the devastating power of the imperial sky-fleets. He has placed his garrisons on high alert, preparing to march on the capital the moment the Emperor draws his final breath.
