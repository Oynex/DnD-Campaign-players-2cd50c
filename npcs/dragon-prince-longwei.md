## Overview
The second son of the [[celestial-dragon-emperor]] and supreme commander of the mystical floating mountain realm of the **[[yunshan-isles]]**. In his dragon form, Longwei is a tempestuous, azure-scaled serpentine dragon commanding the celestial winds and storms.

A martial purist, Prince Longwei disdains the political galas of his sister Princess Zhao and the mercantile bribes of his brother Prince Xian, relying entirely on the devastating firepower of the **Cathayan Air Force**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Standing atop a wind-swept floating mountain terrace, Prince Longwei wears azure dragon-scale battle armor that ripples with crackling wind magic. A heavy poleaxe rests in his armored grasp, his cold, reptilian golden eyes surveying his squadrons of armored war balloons, wyverns, and dragoon knights with grim, uncompromising pride.

## Master of the Skies
- **Supreme Aerial Command:** Commands the entire Cathayan Air Force stationed across the [[yunshan-isles]], personally drilling his Dragoon Knights and wyvern riders in lightning shock tactics.
- **Keeper of the Sanctuary:** Acts as the jealous guardian of the **Celestial Royal Observatory** atop the highest floating peak, ensuring no mortal defiles the ancient draconic sky-sanctuary.
- **The Succession March:** Viewing the appointment of the baby [[princess-mei-ling]] as proof of his father's senility, Longwei has placed his war balloons and wyvern legions on immediate launch alert, intending to swoop down upon [[nan-gau]] from the clouds the moment the Emperor draws his final breath.
