## Overview
The late monarch of the former **Kingdom of Nighthaven** and sovereign patriarch of the Malakor Dynasty. To foreign crowns and visiting envoys, King Victor cultivated an image of cultured aristocratic elegance and patron of courtly arts. Behind the heavy obsidian gates of Nighthaven, however, he and his immediate family formed an insular, terrifying cabal of dark sorcerers who preyed upon the people of [[duskwood]] for generations.

He was slain during the fall of Nighthaven in an apocalyptic sorcerous duel against Grand General **[[swain|Jericho Swain]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Stained-glass murals preserved in the high ruins of Nighthaven depict King Victor Malakor crowned in blackened iron and velvet ermine. His features were handsome yet unnervingly pale, his long fingers trailing dark ethereal energy. His obsidian eyes gaze outward with the chilling, absolute contempt of a predator looking down upon sheep.

## The Malakor Royal Cabal
Unlike normal feudal dynasties, the royal house of Nighthaven was an unbroken cabal of dark practitioners:
- **The Royal Family of Sorcerers:** King Victor ruled alongside his equally corrupt queen, his ancient father (the King-Father who pioneered their demonic pacts), and his ruthless elder sons. Together, they channeled forbidden blood-magic to extend their lifespans and maintain absolute dominion over their vassal dukes and counts.
- **The Demon's Vassals:** The royal family believed they had tamed the shadow demon spirit (**Raum**) slumbering beneath Nighthaven, offering regular sacrifices of gifted children, serfs, and political rivals in exchange for forbidden knowledge and dark power.
- **The Apocalyptic Duel:** When Noxus breached the inner keep, King Victor met Swain in a duel of cataclysmic high sorcery. Victor unleashed an ancient soul-flaying curse that severed and disintegrated Swain's left arm before the Noxian general’s counter-sorcery tore through Victor’s wards and eradicated the dark king.
