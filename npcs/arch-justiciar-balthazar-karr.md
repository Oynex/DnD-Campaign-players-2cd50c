## Overview
The supreme ecclesiastical magistrate of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**, Arch-Justiciar Balthazar Karr presides over the High Tribunal of the Grand Canton in the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]**. Wearing black lacquered magistrate's plate trimmed with silver scales of justice, Karr is neither a fanatic like [[inquisitor-general-malachai]] nor a venal opportunist like [[high-prelate-lucian-vane]]; he is a living machine of legalistic retribution.

To Karr, **[[orthodox-malorism]]** is the spine of civil civilization. He views the divine not through mysticism or prayer, but as the cosmic source of law and order without which humanity would tear itself to bloody shreds. Under his icy supervision, heresy trials are conducted with meticulous documentation, exact rules of evidence, and zero room for human sentiment.

> [!read-aloud]
> Seated upon a towering obsidian magistrate's chair, Arch-Justiciar Balthazar Karr peers down through spectacles of ground quartz. He wears austere black plate under a gray velvet mantle, his gaunt face as expressionless as a stone gargoyle. Before him lies the massive iron-bound tome of Malorian Statutory Code, its pages filled with neat, razor-sharp script.

## Background & Service
Born in the high towers of the Imperial City to a line of imperial jurists, Karr memorized all twelve volumes of Malorian Canon Law by the age of twenty. He served thirty years as an ecclesiastical magistrate across the border marches, earning a fearsome reputation for handing down identical sentences to peasant poachers and noble sons alike.

When a regional governor attempted to bribe him with three wagons of silver to dismiss charges of heresy against his nephew, Karr quietly confiscated the silver for the church treasury, arrested the governor for obstruction of divine justice, and had both men sentenced to the salt mines the following morning. His incorruptibility earned him the seat of Arch-Justiciar among the Five Warrior-Bishops.

## Council Role & Indifferent Observation
- **Rule of the Written Word:** Karr does not tolerate torture without judicial authorization, nor does he allow witch-burnings without verified evidence of demonic pacts or illegal blood rituals. However, once guilt is established by statutory law, his executions are swift, public, and merciless.
- **Cold Indifference to Politics:** In the council chamber of the Five Warrior-Bishops, Karr is utterly indifferent to personal ambition. When [[high-prelate-lucian-vane]] rants about being sidelined, Karr merely adjusts his quartz spectacles. 
- **Admiration for Valen:** Despite his lack of emotional warmth, Karr privately admires how smoothly and quietly **[[bishop-valen-of-the-marches]]** navigates volatile political crises. Valen never breaks canon law; he maneuvers around it with such soldierly discipline that Karr rarely finds reason to intervene.
- **Contempt for the North:** Views the **[[new-malors]]** of [[skyrim]] not as theological rivals, but as lawless mountain outlaws who use the name of Prophet Malor to dodge lawful taxation and civil magistrates.
