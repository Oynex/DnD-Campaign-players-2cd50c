## Overview
The third pillar of the **[[noxian-trifarix|Noxian Trifarix]]**, embodying the principle of **Guile**. Known as the **Pale Lady**, LeBlanc is the ageless, shape-shifting grand matron of the **Mirrored Veil**—an ancient occult cabal that has pulled the strings of empire from the catacombs beneath [[the-immortal-bastion]] for millennia.

Ancient chronicles identify her as the very sorceress who stabbed the tyrant **Sahn-Uzal** (Mordekaiser) in the back thousands of years ago, breaking his reign and clearing the way for the birth of [[noxus]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Stepping out from the velvet shadows of the high balcony, a breathtaking sorceress clad in ornate gilded robes holds an intricate mirror-staff. Her violet eyes gleam with ageless amusement, her true voice whispering from a dozen flickering phantom reflections all at once.

## The Arch-Conspirator
While Swain plans the wars and Darius executes them, LeBlanc controls espionage, assassinations, and clandestine occult diplomacy:
- **The Slayer of the Iron King:** Centuries ago, she feigned allegiance to the terrifying warlord Sahn-Uzal, learning his dark sorcery before orchestrating his downfall by severing his soul from his armor.
- **The Power Behind the Throne:** She allowed the mortal tribes to establish their council, content to steer the empire from the shadows through the Mirrored Veil while ensuring no monarch ever arose to challenge her shadow supremacy.
- **Global Infiltration:** She maintains covert agents in every court across Rubicon—from the Council of Cyrodiil to the merchant halls of the Ring City.
