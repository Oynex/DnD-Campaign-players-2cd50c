## Overview
The third pillar of the **[[noxian-trifarix|Noxian Trifarix]]**, embodying the principle of **Guile**. Known as the **Pale Lady**, LeBlanc is the ageless, shape-shifting grand matron of the **Mirrored Veil**—an ancient occult cabal that has pulled the strings of empire from the catacombs beneath [[the-immortal-bastion]] for centuries.

> [!read-aloud]
> Stepping out from the velvet shadows of the high balcony, a breathtaking sorceress clad in ornate gilded robes holds an intricate mirror-staff. Her eyes gleam with ageless amusement, her true voice whispering from a dozen flickering phantom reflections all at once.

## The Secret Influence
While Swain plans the wars and Darius executes them, LeBlanc controls espionage, assassinations, and clandestine diplomacy. She maintains covert spies in every court across Rubicon—from the Council of Cyrodiil to the merchant halls of the Ring City.
