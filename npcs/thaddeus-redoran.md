## Overview
Head of House Redoran and the Council's master of engineering and infrastructure. Practical, sharp-eyed, and meticulously detail-oriented. He is the father of [[maximillian-redoran]], and while a staunch traditionalist of imperial duty, he is acutely aware of his son's supernatural patron and harbors secret knowledge regarding its origins.

## Influence
Directs imperial roads, aqueducts, and mining concerns. His house pioneered modern [[steam]] engine mechanics and heavy construction across [[cyrodil]].
