## Overview
Head of House Tharn, overseer of imperial commerce, tariffs, and guild contracts. Charming, calculating, and accustomed to measuring global crises strictly in terms of profit and loss. He views open war as economically ruinous and maintains lucrative shipping contracts with [[the-ring-city]].
