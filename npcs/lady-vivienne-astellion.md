## Overview
Head of House Astellion and the Council's keeper of magical knowledge and imperial archives. Brilliant, highly observant, and seemingly always aware of more than she reveals in open assembly. She commands vast networks of antiquarians, astrologers, and arcane researchers.

Her younger sister is **[[archmage-genevieve-astellion]]**, the supreme High Magister of [[daggerfall]] and Chancellor of the [[academy-of-daggerfall]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Draped in midnight-blue velvet dusted with silver constellation charts, Lady Vivienne watches the court with piercing, luminous eyes. She speaks with a quiet serenity that commands absolute silence, her every word carrying centuries of studied lore.

## Arcane Vigil & Sisterly Concern
- **Planar Surveillance:** While other councilors focus on trade or borders, Lady Vivienne monitors the subtle degradation of the planar weave across [[tamriel]]. She is convinced that the disaster at [[vespers-rest]] and the fractures at Daggerfall are not isolated mishaps, but heralds of a grand cosmological convergence.
- **Watching Daggerfall:** Deeply alarmed by whispers of corruption and impending rebellion in Daggerfall, Vivienne secretly coordinated with House Aurelius to dispatch **[[general-kael-stormbringer]]** undercover into the city to discover the truth and safeguard her sister Genevieve from assassination.
