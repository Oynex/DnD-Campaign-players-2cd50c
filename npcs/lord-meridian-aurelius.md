## Overview
Head of House Aurelius and Councilor of Imperial Defense. A grizzled veteran of multiple frontier wars whose family bled heavily during the overthrow of the old monarchy five centuries ago. While blunt and unapologetically pragmatic in open assembly, Lord Aurelius is a master of grand strategy who acutely understands the bloody cost of battlefield failure.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in polished bronze-trimmed cuirass beneath a dark crimson cloak, Lord Meridian Aurelius stands beside the council map table. Weathered battle scars cut across his jawline and knuckles, his steely eyes scanning casualty reports and troop movements with the cold, unblinking focus of an eagle surveying a battlefield.

## Constitutional Watchdog
Under the imperial constitution, House Aurelius is forbidden from directly commanding regiments in the field, serving instead as the state's military watchdog:
- **Regulating High Command:** Lord Aurelius works closely with Supreme Commander **[[general-valorian-thorne]]**, ensuring the legions receive food, pay, and dwarven munitions while strictly monitoring officer loyalty to prevent any coup.
- **Dwarven Procurement:** Aurelius personally negotiated the monumental defense treaty with **[[gundabad]]**, exchanging Cyrodiil grain for masterwork dwarven armor and siege weapons.
