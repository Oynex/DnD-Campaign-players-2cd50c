## Overview
Head of House Aurelius and master of imperial defense. Battle-scarred, direct, and unapologetically pragmatic. He speaks sparingly on the council floor, but his words carry supreme weight among the military commands. Behind his stern exterior lies a veteran general who acutely understands the bloody cost of war.
