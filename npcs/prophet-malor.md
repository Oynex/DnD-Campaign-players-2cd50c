## Overview
The legendary founder and prophet of **[[orthodox-malorism]]** and **[[new-malors]]**, whose revelations and wanderings thirty-five centuries ago reshaped the spiritual landscape of [[tamriel]]. 

Born in the deep, frozen crags of northern [[skyrim]], Malor lived as an ascetic pilgrim, shepherd, and wanderer. Over seventy years of trials documented in the sacred *Book of Trials*, he traversed the length of Tamriel—scaling blizzard peaks, healing victims in plague trenches, calming storm-lashed seas, and enduring imprisonment by tyrants without lifting a blade. His odyssey concluded in the salt marshes of the southern bay, where his legendary forty-day prayer vigil produced the freshwater miracle that birthed the **[[holy-city-of-vivec]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Depicted across thousands of cathedral mosaics in cloth-of-gold and mountain stone carvings alike, Prophet Malor is shown as a barefoot, weathered wanderer carrying a simple cedar staff. His eyes gaze past worldly strife toward an eternal horizon, his posture radiating unyielding resilience and profound, quiet serenity.

## The Northern Birth and the Southern Awakening
- **The Nordic Son:** Born to a humble reindeer clan in Skyrim's highest glaciers, Malor grew up understanding hunger, bitter cold, and the sacred fellowship of the hearth fire.
- **The Continental Pilgrimage:** Driven by divine visions, he walked southward across Tamriel, preaching that God dwells not in stone temples or gold coffers, but in the love and endurance shared between mortals freezing in the dark.
- **The Barren Shore Miracle:** Kneeling in the stagnant brine of the southern sea, he fasted for forty continuous days until a freshwater geyser erupted through the sea floor, creating the sacred springs of Vivec.

## The Disputed Legacy
While Prophet Malor's physical remains lie entombed within the crystal vaults of the High Temple in Vivec, his spiritual soul remains the battleground of Tamriel:
- The **[[orthodox-malorism|Orthodox South]]** reveres him as the Divine Lawgiver whose grace founded their cathedrals and standing holy army.
- The **[[new-malors|Reformed North]]** reveres him as the Barefoot Brother of Frost, claiming the southern bishops twisted his austere message into an engine of greed and taxation.
