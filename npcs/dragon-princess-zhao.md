## Overview
The eldest daughter of the [[celestial-dragon-emperor]] and governor of the picturesque mountain province of **[[hana-no-miyako]]**. Capable of assuming the form of an elegant, silver-scaled serpentine dragon, Princess Zhao is a formidable politician, sorceress, and commander.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in flowing cherry-blossom silks trimmed with silver dragon scales, Princess Zhao reclines upon an ivory divan. Behind her serene, courtly smile, her sharp eyes calculate every angle, her fingers tapping rhythmically against an ornate jade fan.

## Claim to the Throne
Fiercely proud of her pure dragon heritage, Zhao believes she is the only child with the intellect and discipline required to lead Grand Cathay through the coming era. She was deeply infuriated by her father's declaration naming the infant **[[princess-mei-ling]]** as heir, and is secretly maneuvering her mountain guard to contest the succession.
