## Overview
The master builder of Piltover’s transport empire, Baroness Mireille Krom serves as Managing Director of **Krom & Sons Locomotive (KSL)** and sits as the senior logistics director on **[[council-of-piltover|The Council of Piltover]]**. 

Unlike her desk-bound peers, Mireille is a hands-on mechanical genius who often walks the roaring locomotive foundries in oil-stained leather overalls, personally inspecting boiler welds and crystal repulsion vanes for **[[krom-and-sons-locomotives]]**. Under her fierce leadership, KSL has laid thousands of leagues of pressurized track connecting Piltover to the mountain fortress of **[[rivendel]]**.

> [!read-aloud]
> Wearing a brass-riveted leather apron over high-collared tailored trousers, Baroness Mireille Krom wipes engine grease from her cheek with the back of a calloused hand. Her unruly copper curls are pinned back by brass calipers, and a pair of magnifying tinted goggles rests upon her brow. Her eyes sparkle with relentless kinetic energy as she gestures toward a scale model of a rigid-keeled sky-galleon.

## The Iron Lifeline
- **The Continental Artery:** Krom personally engineered the high-pressure crystal-steam locomotives that haul essential munitions and grain daily to hold the defensive lines at Rivendel against Noxian probes.
- **Rivalry with Ferros:** Often clashes with Vanderbilt Ferros over crystal pricing, arguing that price gouging on energy cells hampers the expansion of the western trade network.
- **Target of Sabotage:** Has survived three assassination attempts orchestrated by Noxian infiltrators and disgruntled subterranean union agitators from **[[the-broken-cycle]]**.
