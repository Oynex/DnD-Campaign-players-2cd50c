## Overview
Head of House Corvan and Councilor of Imperial Engineering, Industry, and Infrastructure. Practical, sharp-eyed, and meticulously detail-oriented, Lord Corvan is a visionary champion of iron, steam, and mechanical innovation who believes technological advancement is the true lifeblood of the realm.

> [!read-aloud]
> A broad-shouldered nobleman with calloused hands and iron-gray hair swept back from a furrowed brow. Even in high council sessions, Lord Corvan wears a tailored heavy doublet embroidered with bronze gearwork and brass caliper chains, his stern gaze missing no structural defect in word or stone.

## Industrial Expansion & Dwarven Ties
- **The Railway & Steam:** Speared the development and rapid deployment of **[[steam]]** locomotives, foundries, and deep-vein mining operations across Cyrodiil.
- **The Dwarven Bridge Conduit:** Maintains direct personal and technical relationships with the dwarven engineers of **[[khazad-dum]]** across the **[[ductus-bridge]]**, importing masterwork gears and volcanic lubricants in exchange for agricultural staples.
- **Current Concerns:** Deeply troubled by supernatural tremors and reality fractures in the Corvan Woodlands, which have begun disrupting rail lines and mine shafts.
