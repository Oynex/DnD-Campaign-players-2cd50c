## Overview
Head of House Corvan and the Council's master of engineering and infrastructure. Practical, sharp-eyed, and meticulously detail-oriented. He is a steadfast guardian of imperial industry who believes that iron, steam, and human ingenuity are the true foundations of civilization.

> [!read-aloud]
> A broad-shouldered nobleman with calloused hands and iron-gray hair swept back from a furrowed brow. Even in council chambers, Lord Corvan wears a tailored heavy doublet embroidered with bronze gearwork, his stern gaze missing no defect in word or stone.

## Influence & Current Troubles
Directs imperial roads, aqueducts, foundries, and deep-vein mining concerns. While House Corvan's [[steam]] engines and locomotives have ushered in an era of unprecedented industrial expansion, recent supernatural tremors and reality fractures in the Corvan Woodlands have begun halting timber and coal operations, forcing him to seek capable outside contractors.
