## Overview
The undisputed warlord and primary enforcer of **[[the-sump-syndicate]]**, known across the subterranean foundry slums of **[[piltover]]** as Silas the Red. A towering, scarred former deep-pit crystal miner who led the bloody "Sump Wharf Riots" a decade ago, Silas carved out a sprawling underworld empire across the toxic drainage canals and illegal distillation plants.

Silas commands the muscle, extortion rackets, and raw violence of the lower city. He controls the flow of cheap, undocumented day laborers into corporate factories, extracts protection taxes from every tavern and brothel in the sumps, and oversees the smuggling of stolen raw crystal ore to black-market dealers across continental [[aethelgard]].

> [!read-aloud]
> Towering over seven feet tall, Silas the Red sits upon a throne welded from broken copper pipes and scrap engine manifolds. A massive pneumatic gauntlet of crude iron and glowing purple chem-tubes encloses his right arm, hissing with pressurized steam whenever he flexes his scarred knuckles. His gaze is cold and brutal, assessing visitors like sacks of meat destined for the chem-vats.

## The Underworld Iron Fist
- **Master of the Docks:** Directs an army of heavily armed pit-bruisers, cutthroats, and chem-modified berserkers who enforce gang law across the lower city.
- **Symbiosis with the Topsiders:** Sells strikebreaking muscle to corporate executives like Vanderbilt Ferros, while quietly siphoning high-grade crystals from their transport barges.
- **Hatred for the Worker Cult:** Despises **[[the-broken-cycle]]**, viewing their refusal to buy syndicate narcotics and their talk of the "Great Lever" as a direct threat to his protection profits. He has ordered dozens of Split-Gear agitators drowned in the chemical sludge.
