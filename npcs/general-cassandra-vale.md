## Overview
A legendary, highly decorated veteran of the Northern Frontier Wars and one of the three supreme generals of [[cyrodil]]. Celebrated throughout the empire for unyielding stubbornness in defensive sieges, combat engineering, and forward bastion defense.

While General Thorne oversees the central legions, General Vale commands the fortified northern mountain wall, holding the line against relentless reavers from **[[skyrim]]** and hostile border skirmishers.

> [!read-aloud]
> Clad in fur-lined, battle-dented iron plate, General Cassandra Vale stands atop the wind-swept parapets of the northern barrier. A pair of heavy spyglasses rests in her gloved hands, her sharp hazel eyes scanning the snowy pine valleys below. Frost clings to her braided dark hair, but her energetic, commanding posture radiates pure, unyielding defiance.

## The Shield of the North
- **Holding the Line:** Has successfully broken seventeen major winter sieges along the northern passes, earning the moniker "The Iron Maiden of the Crags."
- **Innovative Combat Engineering:** Pioneered trench defense networks, quick-setting mortar earthworks, and heavy runic ballista batteries to neutralize northern brute force.
- **Strategic Independence:** Frequently clashes with the conservative doctrines of **[[general-valorian-thorne]]**, advocating for proactive skirmish raiding rather than passive shield walls.
