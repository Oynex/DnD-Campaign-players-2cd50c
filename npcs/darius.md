## Overview
Known as the **Hand of Noxus**, Grand General Darius is the supreme battlefield commander of the Noxian military and one of the three ruling figures of the **[[noxian-trifarix|Noxian Trifarix]]**, embodying the principle of **Might**. 

A warrior of ferocious physical strength and battlefield discipline, Darius personally spearheaded the conquest and pacification of [[durotar]], turning the warring orc clans into lethal shock infantry for the empire.

> [!read-aloud]
> A colossal warrior clad in brutal black iron plate armor adorned with the crimson sigils of Noxus. A massive, serrated two-handed battleaxe rests against his shoulder, his scarred, granite-jawed face showing zero tolerance for cowardice or hesitation.

## The Duel with the Warpath
Darius holds a mythic, near-supernatural reputation throughout Rubicon for an unmatched martial feat:
- During the **First Siege of [[stormwind]]**, when the desperate former king summoned **[[the-warpath]]**, Darius personally engaged one of the black-armored dread commanders in single combat.
- Darius fought with sheer monstrous endurance and survived the duel. To this day, he remains the **only mortal in recorded history** known to have crossed weapons with a commander of the Warpath in one-on-one combat and lived.

## Role in the Western Campaign
Darius currently oversees the front-line siege forces hammering against [[rivendel]]. He views the technological contraptions of [[piltover]] with contempt, determined to shatter their energy barriers with pure martial muscle and relentless legionary assaults.
