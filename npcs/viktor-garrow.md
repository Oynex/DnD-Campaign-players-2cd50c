## Overview
The mechanical mastermind of **[[the-sump-syndicate]]**, Baron Viktor Garrow is a brilliant, renegade magitech artificer who operates a sprawling, subterranean black-market foundry beneath the foundry sumps of **[[piltover]]**. 

A former senior research director at **Aethelgard Magitech-Refining Ltd.**, Garrow was stripped of his titles, patents, and livelihood twelve years ago when Vanderbilt Ferros stole his breakthrough designs for stabilized Grade-B crystal cells and had him cast into the chemical drainage sumps. Disfigured by industrial acid and burning with vengeance, Garrow reconstructed his broken body with crudely forged brass and crystal prosthetics, becoming the underworld's premier engineer of illegal magitech weaponry, illegal automaton bruisers, and explosive demolition charges.

> [!read-aloud]
> Hunched over an iron workbench littered with disassembled rifle receivers, sparking crystal cells, and whirring clockwork gears, Viktor Garrow works by the sputtering light of an oxy-acetylene torch. Half of his skull is encased in bolted bronze plating, and an articulated mechanical third arm mounted to his back spine holds soldering irons with terrifying precision. When he turns, his single natural eye burns with cold, unyielding hatred.

## Underworld Inventions & Vendetta
- **The Rogue Foundry:** Manufactures illegal black-market firearms, high-explosive crystal detonators, and crude steam-powered mechanical exosuits for **[[silas-the-red]]** and syndicate hit squads.
- **Vendetta Against Ferros:** His overriding obsession is the total financial and physical annihilation of Vanderbilt Ferros and Aethelgard Magitech-Refining Ltd.
- **Dangerous Sympathies:** While Garrow supplies the Syndicate for protection and resources, he secretly sympathizes with **[[the-broken-cycle]]**, quietly leaving crates of tools and sabotaged factory valves in drop-points used by the worker cult.
