## Overview
The youthful, tragic heir to the fallen crown of **[[stormwind]]**, Prince Liam was captured following the Second Siege of his homeland and brought to the dark heart of the empire. 

Held in the gilded high guest quarters of **[[the-immortal-bastion]]**, Prince Liam is officially treated by Noxian high society as an honored "diplomatic ward." In reality, he lives in a prison built not just of iron bars, but of ancient, insurmountable despair.

> [!read-aloud]
> Confined to a high stone balcony overlooking the spiked roofs of the Noxian capital, Prince Liam wears a tailored tunic of blue velvet bearing the faded gold lion of Stormwind. Though barely twenty years old, his aristocratic face carries the weary, hollow gaze of a man twice his age. A heavy iron signet ring—his father's only relic—rests on his thumb as he gazes eastward toward a homeland he can never see again.

## The Curse of the Warpath
Liam’s tragedy is the ultimate realization of the dark pact struck with **[[the-warpath]]**:
- **The Stolen Honor:** When his desperate father summoned the Warpath, the bargain demanded *everything the king cherished*. Beyond the king's life, the Warpath took his royal honor and his people's love. Liam must endure the agonizing knowledge that his father gave up his soul for his people, yet the farmers and citizens of **[[east-fall]]** and Stormwind despise the royal family as cowardly failures who abandoned them.
- **The Exile's Curse:** Bound by the metaphysical terms of the dark bargain, Liam is supernaturally **cursed never to return to his homeland**. Even if Noxus were to pardon him, or if he managed to escape the Bastion, crossing into East Fall or Stormwind would ravage his spirit. He is an eternal exile from the land his family ruled for ten generations.
- **Living in the Lion's Den:** Lacking any loyalist army marching to save him, Liam endures his captivity with quiet, mournful dignity, studying the Noxian court while knowing he is the last, unmourned ember of a forgotten kingdom.
