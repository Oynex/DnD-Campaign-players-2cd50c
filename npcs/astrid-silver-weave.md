## Overview
The supreme authority on celestial mechanics and Guildmaster of **[[the-arcane-consortium]]**, sitting on **[[high-council-of-the-ring-city|The High Council of the Ring City]]**. Known as "The Star-Weaver," Astrid is a High Elven archmage whose silver hair floats in slow ambient levitation.

Unlike the isolationist mages of Summerset Isle, Astrid chose to dwell in the Ring City to oversee planetary research, warning the High Council that cosmic alignments point toward an impending celestial convergence.

> [!read-aloud]
> Robed in deep midnight-blue silks embroidered with constellations that slowly shift and twinkle across the fabric, Arch-Astronomer Astrid Silver-Weave stands before her council seat. In her slender hands she holds a rotating orrery of glowing crystal spheres, her luminous silver eyes scanning her fellow councilors with sorrowful cosmic foresight.

## Warnings Ignored
- **The Crystal Drain Alarm:** Repeatedly warns the council that the aggressive crystal dredging in Piltover is weakening the planetary leylines that anchor the southern seal, though her warnings are routinely dismissed by the mercantile lobby as academic paranoia.
