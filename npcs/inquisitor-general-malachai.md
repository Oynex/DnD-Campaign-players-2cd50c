## Overview
The supreme inquisitorial commander of **[[the-knights-of-malor]]**, known across [[tamriel]] as the "Flaming Sword of Vivec." Carrying the ecclesiastical rank of Warrior-Bishop with the active operational title of **Inquisitor-General Militant Malachai**, he spends ten months of every year in the field, sleeping on campaign cots and personally leading frontline witch-hunts against bog hags, necromancers, and apostate cults.

A man of terrifying ascetic purity, Malachai wears battered, consecration-etched half-plate over a blood-stained linen tabard. He refuses personal wealth, donates his entire stipend to fund templar armories, and fasts on bread and water three days a week in imitation of **[[orthodox-malorism|Prophet Malor's]]** desert trials.

> [!read-aloud]
> Beneath a scorched iron skullcap, Inquisitor-General Malachai’s hollow, sun-darkened face is carved by deep lines of relentless vigilance. His eyes burn like stoked embers, and his scarred knuckles rest upon the pommel of a massive, double-handed executioner's broadsword etched with scripture. Around his neck hangs a heavy bronze reliquary containing ash from the pyre of the First Apostate.

## Background & Early Crusades
Born to an impoverished cobbler outside the Imperial City, Malachai was orphaned during a plague outbreak and taken in by a rural Church of Malor as an altar boy. At age fourteen, his village chapel was attacked by a coven of swamp hags from the southern marshes who slaughtered the resident monks. Malachai survived by barricading himself inside the sanctuary, impaling a hag matriarch with a heavy iron altar cross. 

Discovered by a passing patrol of templars, he was inducted into the martial cloisters of Vivec. Over four decades of ruthless service, he climbed through the ranks through sheer martial prowess and an incorruptible hatred for dark sorcery.

## Military & Inquisitorial Role
- **The Witch-Hunter General:** Directs the Ordo Exorcismus of the holy army, commanding five hundred elite inquisitorial templars who patrol the southern frontiers.
- **Respect for the Old Soldier:** Malachai holds absolute, unbending martial respect for **[[bishop-valen-of-the-marches]]**, recognizing him as an authentic soldier of iron who shared blood in the trenches. When Valen speaks in council, Malachai listens.
- **Steering Clear of Alyssa:** Because of his profound reverence for Valen, Malachai intentionally keeps his inquisitorial bloodhounds completely clear of **[[holy-commander-alyssa-thorne]]** and her hospital cloisters, conceding that the church requires both a flaming sword and a healing hand.
- **Voting Bloc:** Consistently votes alongside Valen and Alyssa on council decrees, cheerfully denying **[[high-prelate-lucian-vane]]** the commercial concessions the corrupt prelate craves.
- **View of the North:** Considers the **[[new-malors]]** of [[skyrim]] to be misguided apostates who have lost their way, but reserves his burning hatred for those who actively traffic with fiends or study forbidden arts.
